## DD-01: How to use this document
======================================================================
Every SOP refers to the tables and statuses defined here. Queries in SOPs are READ-ONLY
examples written in SQL. Named parameters look like :phone, :user_id, :deposit_id.
Timestamps are stored in UTC; convert to IST (UTC+5:30) before telling a customer a time.
Amounts in INR are stored in rupees with 2 decimals. Crypto amounts use the asset's precision.

Never show table names, internal status codes, reviewer names, risk reasons or vendor names
to a customer. Translate them into plain language using the reply templates in each SOP.

## DD-02: Identity tables
======================================================================
TABLE users
  user_id            BIGINT, primary key
  phone              TEXT, E.164 format, e.g. +919812345678 (unique)
  email              TEXT (unique, lowercase)
  full_name          TEXT, as entered at signup (KYC name may differ)
  status             ACTIVE | BLOCKED | FROZEN | DORMANT | CLOSED
                     BLOCKED = too many failed logins (temporary, auto-unblocks after 24h)
                     FROZEN  = frozen by Risk/Compliance, see risk_holds
                     DORMANT = no login for 24 months
  kyc_status         NOT_STARTED | IN_PROGRESS | UNDER_REVIEW | VERIFIED | REJECTED | RE_KYC_REQUIRED
  referral_code      TEXT, the user's own code
  referred_by        BIGINT, user_id of the referrer (nullable)
  created_at, last_login_at

TABLE user_devices
  user_id, device_id, platform (ANDROID | IOS | WEB), app_version, is_trusted, first_seen_at, last_seen_at

TABLE auth_events
  event_id, user_id, event_type, channel, ip_address, device_id, failure_reason, created_at
  event_type: LOGIN_SUCCESS | LOGIN_FAILED | OTP_SENT | OTP_VERIFIED | OTP_FAILED |
              PASSWORD_RESET | TWO_FA_ENABLED | TWO_FA_RESET | PHONE_CHANGED | EMAIL_CHANGED |
              SESSION_REVOKED
  failure_reason examples: WRONG_PASSWORD, WRONG_OTP, OTP_EXPIRED, ACCOUNT_BLOCKED, DEVICE_NOT_TRUSTED

TABLE otp_logs
  otp_id, user_id (nullable before signup), destination (phone or email), channel (SMS | EMAIL | WHATSAPP),
  provider, status (SENT | DELIVERED | FAILED | EXPIRED | VERIFIED), provider_error, created_at

## DD-03: KYC tables
======================================================================
TABLE kyc_applications
  kyc_id, user_id, status (IN_PROGRESS | SUBMITTED | UNDER_REVIEW | VERIFIED | REJECTED | RE_KYC_REQUIRED),
  submitted_at, reviewed_at, rejection_reason_code, review_queue (AUTO | MANUAL), attempt_number

  rejection_reason_code values:
    PAN_INVALID            PAN number not found at the income-tax database
    PAN_NAME_MISMATCH      name on PAN does not match name on Aadhaar
    PAN_AADHAAR_NOT_LINKED PAN is not linked with Aadhaar (inoperative PAN)
    AADHAAR_OTP_FAILED     Aadhaar OTP verification failed or timed out
    SELFIE_MISMATCH        selfie does not match the photo on the ID
    LIVENESS_FAILED        liveness check failed (photo of a photo, poor light, face covered)
    DOC_UNREADABLE         uploaded document is blurry, cropped or has glare
    UNDERAGE               date of birth shows the user is under 18
    DUPLICATE_PAN          PAN already used by another Acme account
    SANCTIONS_HIT          name matched a sanctions/watch list (Compliance only, never disclose)

TABLE kyc_documents
  doc_id, kyc_id, doc_type (PAN | AADHAAR | SELFIE | PASSPORT | ADDRESS_PROOF),
  vendor_ref, verification_status (PENDING | PASSED | FAILED), failure_reason,
  name_on_doc, dob_on_doc, uploaded_at

TABLE kyc_vendor_logs
  log_id, kyc_id, check_type (PAN_VERIFY | AADHAAR_OTP | FACE_MATCH | LIVENESS | SANCTIONS),
  response_code, response_message, latency_ms, created_at
  response_code 5xx or TIMEOUT means the vendor was down; the user did nothing wrong.

## DD-04: Bank and INR money movement tables
======================================================================
TABLE bank_accounts
  bank_account_id, user_id, account_number_masked (e.g. XXXXXX4321), ifsc, bank_name,
  account_holder_name (as returned by the bank), status (PENDING_VERIFICATION | VERIFIED | FAILED | REMOVED),
  verification_method (PENNY_DROP | REVERSE_PENNY_DROP), name_match_score (0-100), is_primary, created_at

TABLE deposits
  deposit_id, user_id, amount, method (UPI | IMPS | NEFT | RTGS), status, utr, gateway,
  gateway_txn_id, bank_account_id, failure_reason, created_at, updated_at
  status: INITIATED | PENDING | SUCCESS | FAILED | ON_HOLD | REFUND_INITIATED | REFUNDED
  failure_reason examples: USER_CANCELLED, BANK_DECLINED, TIMEOUT, THIRD_PARTY_ACCOUNT,
                           AMOUNT_ABOVE_LIMIT, UNVERIFIED_BANK_ACCOUNT

TABLE payment_gateway_txns
  gateway_txn_id, gateway, deposit_id, gateway_status (CREATED | PENDING | CAPTURED | FAILED | REVERSED),
  bank_reference (UTR/RRN), webhook_received_at, last_polled_at

TABLE refunds
  refund_id, source_type (DEPOSIT | WITHDRAWAL), source_id, user_id, amount,
  status (INITIATED | PROCESSED | FAILED), refund_utr, created_at, processed_at

TABLE withdrawals
  withdrawal_id, user_id, asset (INR or a crypto symbol), amount, fee, status,
  bank_account_id (INR), destination_address, network, memo_tag (crypto),
  payout_utr (INR), tx_hash (crypto), hold_reason, created_at, updated_at
  status: REQUESTED | PENDING_APPROVAL | PROCESSING | SUCCESS | FAILED | CANCELLED | ON_HOLD

TABLE wallet_balances
  user_id, asset, available, locked, updated_at
  locked = reserved by open orders or pending withdrawals

TABLE ledger_entries   (source of truth for every balance change)
  entry_id, user_id, asset, amount (+ credit / - debit), entry_type, reference_type, reference_id, created_at
  entry_type: DEPOSIT | WITHDRAWAL | TRADE_BUY | TRADE_SELL | FEE | TDS | REWARD | REFUND |
              ADJUSTMENT | REVERSAL
  reference_type -> reference_id:
              DEPOSIT -> deposits.deposit_id           CRYPTO_DEPOSIT -> crypto_deposits.crypto_deposit_id
              WITHDRAWAL -> withdrawals.withdrawal_id  TRADE -> trades.trade_id
              REWARD -> rewards_ledger.reward_id       REFUND -> refunds.refund_id
              ADJUSTMENT -> tickets.ticket_id (manual corrections by Ops, always with a ticket)
  How money is posted: see DD-10.

## DD-09: Platform policies (sample values)
======================================================================
Deposits (INR)
  - Methods: UPI (up to Rs 1,00,000 per transaction), IMPS/NEFT/RTGS via bank transfer.
  - Minimum deposit: Rs 100.
  - Deposits must come from a VERIFIED bank account in the user's own name. Third-party
    deposits are refunded to source.
  - UPI deposits normally reflect within 5 minutes; IMPS within 30 minutes; NEFT within
    2 banking hours; RTGS within 1 banking hour.
  - A deposit PENDING for more than 30 minutes is auto-reconciled by the system every
    15 minutes for 72 hours.
  - Refund to source: 5-7 working days after refund initiation.

Withdrawals (INR)
  - Minimum Rs 100. Fee Rs 10 per withdrawal. Only to a VERIFIED bank account in the user's name.
  - Normal SLA: within 24 hours. Withdrawals above Rs 5,00,000 go to PENDING_APPROVAL (manual review, up to 48 hours).
  - 24-hour withdrawal cooldown after a password reset, phone change, email change or 2FA reset.

Crypto transfers
  - Required confirmations and fees are in supported_networks.
  - New withdrawal addresses have a 24-hour cooldown (address_whitelist.PENDING_COOLDOWN).
  - Deposits on an unsupported network or without a required memo/tag need manual recovery:
    recovery fee Rs 2,500 equivalent, 7-15 working days, not guaranteed.

KYC
  - Automatic review: minutes. Manual review: up to 48 hours.
  - Maximum 3 KYC attempts; after the 3rd rejection the case goes to KYC Ops.
  - Users must be 18+ and Indian residents with a PAN linked to Aadhaar.

Trading and tax
  - Trading fee: 0.2% of order value (maker and taker), plus 18% GST on the fee.
  - TDS: 1% on the sale value of crypto (Section 194S), deducted at the time of sale.
  - Minimum order value: Rs 100 for INR pairs.

Rewards
  - Referral: referrer gets Rs 100 in BTC after the referee completes KYC AND a first trade
    of at least Rs 500 within 30 days of signup. Referral code must be applied at signup.
  - Rewards are credited within 72 hours of qualification and expire 90 days after credit
    if not used (where the campaign says so).

Security
  - Account unblocks automatically 24 hours after 5 failed login attempts.
  - Phone or email change needs a fresh selfie verification.

Support SLAs (first response / resolution target)
  - P1 (money missing, unauthorized access): 1 hour / 24 hours
  - P2 (money delayed, cannot trade or withdraw): 4 hours / 48 hours
  - P3 (KYC, rewards, statements): 24 hours / 5 working days
  - P4 (how-to questions, feedback): 48 hours / 7 working days

Internal teams (for escalation)
  - Payments Ops, KYC Ops, Trading Ops, Crypto Ops (on-chain), Risk & Compliance,
    Rewards Team, Tech On-call, Grievance Officer, Legal, Comms (media and public statements).
  - Support Lead: senior support agent for sensitive or repeated cases (SUP-05, SUP-07).
  - Support Operations: owns support processes, statements and SOPs; handles cases that
    need no specialist team.

## DD-10: How money is posted to the ledger
======================================================================
Every balance change is one or more rows in ledger_entries. Use these rules to explain a
customer's balance, or to spot a missing or wrong entry.

INR deposit (SUCCESS)
  +amount DEPOSIT (reference_type DEPOSIT). No deposit fee.
  A refunded deposit is never credited to the wallet, so it has no ledger rows; see refunds.

Trade (per fill, i.e. per trades row)
  BUY  : -price*quantity TRADE_BUY in the quote asset (e.g. INR), +quantity in the base asset,
         -(fee + gst) FEE in the quote asset.
  SELL : -quantity TRADE_SELL in the base asset, +price*quantity in the quote asset,
         -(fee + gst) FEE, and -tds TDS (1% of sale value) in the quote asset.
  All rows use reference_type TRADE and reference_id = trade_id. An order with 3 fills has
  3 sets of rows, so a partially filled order shows several FEE rows.
  Worked example (sell Rs 50,000 of BTC): +50,000.00, FEE -118.00 (100 fee + 18 GST),
  TDS -500.00 -> net Rs 49,382.00 credited.

INR withdrawal
  At request: amount + fee moves from available to locked (no ledger row yet).
  At PROCESSING: -amount WITHDRAWAL and -fee FEE (reference_type WITHDRAWAL), locked released.
  If the payout FAILS: +amount +fee REVERSAL (reference_type WITHDRAWAL): the full amount,
  including the fee, returns to available.
  CANCELLED before PROCESSING: nothing was posted; locked is released.

Crypto withdrawal
  Same as INR, in the crypto asset; the network fee (supported_networks.withdrawal_fee) is
  the FEE row. A FAILED on-chain send is reversed the same way.

Crypto deposit (CREDITED)
  +amount DEPOSIT (reference_type CRYPTO_DEPOSIT).

Rewards
  +amount REWARD (reference_type REWARD) when rewards_ledger.status becomes CREDITED.
  A revoked reward that was already credited is taken back with an ADJUSTMENT row.

Checking a balance
  available + locked for an asset = sum(ledger_entries.amount) for that user and asset.
  If they differ, do not guess: escalate to Payments Ops (INR) or Crypto Ops (crypto), P1.
