## FEE-00: Scope and quick triage
======================================================================
SCOPE:
  Every charge a customer may see on Acme Invest: trading fee and GST on it, INR withdrawal
  fee, crypto network (withdrawal) fee, fee disputes, fees on cancelled or partially filled
  orders, unexplained ledger deductions, GST invoices and fee refund requests.
  NOT in scope:
    - TDS (1% tax on crypto sales) -> tax_statements_sop (see FEE-05 for the short answer)
    - Crypto deposit recovery fee for wrong network / missing memo -> crypto_transfers_sop
    - Price, slippage and order fills -> trade_sop

FEE SCHEDULE (DD-09 unless marked):
  Charge                     Amount                                  Where it shows
  Trading fee                0.2% of trade value (maker and taker)   trades.fee; ledger FEE
  GST on trading fee         18% of the trading fee                  trades.gst; ledger FEE
  TDS (tax, not a fee)       1% of sale value on crypto sells        trades.tds; ledger TDS
  INR withdrawal fee         Rs 10 per withdrawal                    withdrawals.fee; ledger FEE
  Crypto withdrawal fee      per asset + network                     supported_networks.withdrawal_fee;
                                                                     withdrawals.fee
  INR deposit                no fee (sample value)                   -
  Account opening / AMC      no fee (sample value)                   -

HOW FEES SIT IN THE DATA (sample conventions):
  - Fees are charged per trade (per fill), not per order. One order can have several trades.
  - Trade fees are charged in the quote currency of the pair (INR for BTC-INR, USDT for SOL-USDT).
  - Rounding: fee and GST are rounded to 2 decimals for INR pairs (sample value).
  - Ledger: a trade creates TRADE_BUY or TRADE_SELL, one FEE entry equal to fee + gst, and on
    sells a TDS entry. All use reference_type = 'TRADE' and reference_id = trade_id (sample
    convention). An INR withdrawal fee uses reference_type = 'WITHDRAWAL'.

QUICK TRIAGE:

  "How is the fee calculated?"                              FEE-01

  "1% deducted on sell"                                     FEE-05

## FEE-01: How the trading fee is calculated
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "What is the brokerage/trading fee on Acme?"
  - "How much fee will I pay if I buy Rs 10,000 of BTC?"
  - "Why is GST charged on crypto trading?"
  - "Fee kitna lagta hai buy sell pe?"
  - "Are maker and taker fees different?"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - None (general question). For a specific trade, use FEE-02.
TOOLS: none
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Answer from the fee schedule in FEE-00 and the worked example below. Resolve.
  2. If the customer quotes a specific trade or amount that "does not match", identify the
     user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02) and go to FEE-02.
WORKED EXAMPLE (use real numbers in replies):
  BUY 0.01 BTC on BTC-INR at Rs 50,00,000 per BTC
    Trade value   = 0.01 x 50,00,000          = Rs 50,000.00
    Trading fee   = 0.2% of 50,000            = Rs 100.00
    GST           = 18% of 100                = Rs 18.00
    Total fee     = 100 + 18                  = Rs 118.00
    Total paid    = 50,000 + 118              = Rs 50,118.00  (from INR balance)
  SELL 0.01 BTC on BTC-INR at Rs 50,00,000 per BTC
    Trade value   = Rs 50,000.00
    Trading fee   = Rs 100.00, GST = Rs 18.00
    TDS (tax)     = 1% of 50,000              = Rs 500.00  (not a fee, see FEE-05)
    INR received  = 50,000 - 100 - 18 - 500   = Rs 49,382.00
  Effective fee rate including GST = 0.2% x 1.18 = 0.236% of trade value.
  Small order: BUY Rs 100 (minimum order value) -> fee Rs 0.20 + GST Rs 0.04 = Rs 0.24.
RESOLUTION:
  Explain 0.2% + 18% GST on the fee with an example at the customer's amount.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Thanks for your question. Acme Invest charges a trading fee of 0.2% of the trade value on
  every buy and sell (the same for all order types), plus 18% GST on that fee, as required
  by law.
  For example, if you buy Rs <amount> of <coin>:
  - Trading fee: 0.2% of Rs <amount> = Rs <fee>
  - GST: 18% of Rs <fee> = Rs <gst>
  - Total charges: Rs <fee + gst>
  When you sell, the same fee applies, and 1% TDS (a tax, not a fee) is also deducted.
  You can see the exact charges for every trade in the order details in the app.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Not needed for a how-to question.
DO NOT:
  - Do not quote fee rates other than DD-09 or promise discounts or special rates.

## FEE-02: Customer disputes the fee on a trade
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "You charged me Rs 250 fee on a Rs 50,000 trade, that is not 0.2%"
  - "Fee on my ETH sell is too high"
  - "Zyada fee kaat li aapne, check karo"
  - "Order details show different fee than what you advertise"
  - "Why is the fee more than 0.2%?"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Order ID or the coin, side, date/time and amount of the trade
TOOLS: check_order, get_ledger_entries
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify and verify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02). If they quote an order_id,
     confirm it belongs to them (order.user_id from check_order in step 2).
  2. Get the order and its trades:
     Call check_order(order_id=<order_id>). Use order and fills (trade_value, fee, gst, tds,
     expected_fee, expected_gst per fill).
  3. Compare, per trade (fill):
     - fee = expected_fee and gst = expected_gst (allow +/- Rs 0.01 per trade for rounding):
       fee is correct. Usually the customer is:
         a) counting GST as part of the fee (0.236% total), or
         b) counting TDS on a sell as a fee (-> FEE-05), or
         c) comparing to the order amount rather than the filled amount (partial fill), or
         d) adding fees of several fills (-> FEE-09).
       -> Explain with the numbers. Resolve.
     - fee or gst differs by more than Rs 0.01 on any trade: possible fee error ->
       escalate Trading Ops, P3 (P2 if the total excess is above Rs 1,000 (sample value)).
  4. Check the ledger matches the fills, for each trade_id:
     Call get_ledger_entries(reference_type="TRADE", reference_id=<trade_id>).
     - FEE entries equal -(fee + gst) per trade: consistent.
     - FEE entries larger than the fill's fee + gst, or duplicated (also listed in check_order
       fee_charged_twice): ledger error ->
       escalate Trading Ops, P2.
  5. For USDT pairs, the fee is in USDT; convert only if the customer asks, and say it is
     approximate.
RESOLUTION:
  Show the calculation per trade. Escalate only a confirmed mismatch.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Thanks for asking us to check. I recalculated the charges on your <buy/sell> of <coin> on
  <date>:
  - Trade value: Rs <value>
  - Trading fee (0.2%): Rs <fee>
  - GST (18% of the fee): Rs <gst>
  <- TDS (1% tax on the sale, not a fee): Rs <tds>>
  Total charges: Rs <total>. <These match our fee of 0.2% plus GST, so the amount charged is
  correct. / We found a difference of Rs <x> and have passed it to our trading team to
  review; we will update you within 5 working days.>
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Confirmed mismatch between trades.fee/gst and 0.2%/18% -> Trading Ops, P3 (P2 if excess
    > Rs 1,000), include: user_id, order_id, trade_ids, expected vs actual fee and gst.
  - Ledger FEE differs from trades -> Trading Ops, P2, include ledger rows.
DO NOT:
  - Do not promise a refund before Trading Ops confirms (FEE-08).
  - Do not show internal column names in the reply.

## FEE-03: INR withdrawal fee
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "Why Rs 10 deducted when I withdrew?"
  - "Withdrawal fee charged twice"
  - "Withdrawal failed but fee was taken"
  - "10 rupees kaat liye withdraw pe"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Amount and date of the withdrawal
TOOLS: get_withdrawals, check_withdrawal
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify and verify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Call get_withdrawals(asset="INR"). Look at the 10 most recent.
     Call check_withdrawal(withdrawal_id=<withdrawal_id>). Use withdrawal.fee, withdrawal.status,
     ledger_entries and reversed.
  3. Branch:
     - fee = 10, one FEE entry of -10, status SUCCESS/PROCESSING: correct per DD-09. Resolve.
     - Two FEE entries for one withdrawal_id: duplicate charge -> escalate Trading Ops, P3.
     - Status FAILED: the fee should be returned with the amount (REVERSAL entry, see
       withdrawal_sop WDR-03). reversed is true -> explain it was returned. Missing after
       5 working days -> withdrawal_sop WDR-03 escalation.
     - Status CANCELLED before sending: no fee should be charged; FEE entry present ->
       escalate Trading Ops, P3.
     - fee <> 10 on an INR withdrawal: wrong fee -> escalate Trading Ops, P3.
     - Several withdrawals on the same day: Rs 10 applies to each withdrawal. Resolve.
RESOLUTION:
  Explain the flat Rs 10 fee per withdrawal, returned if the withdrawal fails.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Thanks for checking. Each INR withdrawal on Acme Invest has a flat fee of Rs 10. For your
  withdrawal of Rs <amount> on <date>, Rs 10 was charged from your wallet and the full
  Rs <amount> was sent to your bank. <If a withdrawal fails, the fee is returned to your
  wallet along with the amount; for your failed withdrawal on <date>, the Rs 10 was returned
  on <date>.>
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Duplicate, wrong, or not-returned fee -> Trading Ops, P3, include withdrawal_id, ledger rows.
DO NOT:
  - Do not waive or refund the standard Rs 10 fee.

## FEE-04: Crypto network (withdrawal) fee
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "Why is the fee to withdraw USDT so high?"
  - "Network fee changed since yesterday"
  - "I sent 100 USDT but only 99 reached"
  - "TRC20 vs ERC20 fee difference?"
  - "Crypto withdraw pe itni fee kyu?"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Asset, network and the withdrawal (date or tx hash)
TOOLS: get_supported_networks, get_withdrawals
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify and verify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Current fee for the asset/network:
     Call get_supported_networks(asset=<asset>).
  3. The customer's withdrawal:
     Call get_withdrawals(asset=<asset>). Look at the 5 most recent.
  4. Branch:
     - The withdrawal's fee equals the network's current withdrawal_fee: correct. Explain
       that the fee pays the blockchain network, differs by network, and can change with
       network congestion (sample note). Suggest a cheaper network if the receiving platform
       supports it (e.g. compare the rows returned in step 2). Resolve.
     - The withdrawal's fee differs from today's fee: the fee may have changed since the
       withdrawal. get_supported_networks shows only today's fee; if the difference is large (more than
       2x, sample value) -> escalate Crypto Ops, P3 to confirm the fee at that time.
     - Received amount lower than amount - fee: the receiving exchange may charge a deposit
       fee, or the customer entered the amount after fee -> explain; if on-chain amount (by
       tx_hash) differs from amount minus fee -> crypto_transfers_sop / escalate Crypto Ops, P3.
     - Customer used the wrong network -> crypto_transfers_sop.
RESOLUTION:
  Explain the network fee and alternatives.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Thanks for your question. When you withdraw crypto, a network fee is charged to send the
  coins on the blockchain. It depends on the coin and the network you choose, and it is shown
  on the withdrawal screen before you confirm. For your <asset> withdrawal on <network> on
  <date>, the fee was <fee> <asset>. <Today the fee for <asset> on <other network> is
  <fee2> <asset>, which may be cheaper if the receiving platform supports that network. Please
  always check that the receiving platform supports the network before sending.>
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Fee charged far from the published fee -> Crypto Ops, P3, include withdrawal_id, asset,
    network, fee charged, current withdrawal_fee.
DO NOT:
  - Do not advise a network the customer's receiving platform may not support.
  - Do not refund network fees.

## FEE-05: TDS is not a fee
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "Extra 1% deducted when I sold, what is this charge?"
  - "Hidden charge on sell order"
  - "TDS kyu kaata? I am in loss"
  - "You are charging 1% extra fee on selling"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Which sell trade (order ID or date)
TOOLS: check_order
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify and verify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Call check_order(order_id=<order_id>). Use order.side and each fill's tds and trade_value
     (expected_tds = 1% of trade_value, rounded to 2 decimals).
     - side SELL and tds = expected_tds: correct; TDS is 1% of the sale value under Section
       194S, deducted even if the trade is at a loss. Explain and point to tax_statements_sop
       for claiming it. Resolve.
     - side BUY with tds > 0, or tds differs from 1% by more than Rs 0.01: escalate Trading
       Ops, P3.
  3. Questions about TDS certificates, Form 16A or claiming TDS -> tax_statements_sop.
RESOLUTION:
  Explain TDS as a tax deducted on behalf of the government, not an Acme fee.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Thanks for asking. The 1% deducted on your sale of <coin> on <date> (Rs <tds>) is not an
  Acme Invest fee. It is TDS (tax deducted at source), which the Income Tax Act requires on
  every crypto sale, whether it is in profit or loss. We deposit it with the government
  against your PAN, and you can claim it when you file your income tax return. Your TDS
  details are available in the app under Reports.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - TDS on a buy or wrong amount -> Trading Ops, P3, include trade_id, expected vs actual.
DO NOT:
  - Do not give personal tax advice; point to tax_statements_sop and a tax adviser.

## FEE-06: Fee charged on a cancelled order
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "I cancelled my order but still got charged fee"
  - "Order cancelled, why is fee deducted?"
  - "Cancel kiya tha, fir bhi charges lage"
  - "Expired order but fee taken"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Order ID or coin, date/time
TOOLS: check_order, get_ledger_entries, check_wallet_balance
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify and verify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Call check_order(order_id=<order_id>). Check order.user_id is the customer's; use order
     and fills.
  3. Branch:
     - status CANCELLED / EXPIRED with filled_quantity > 0 and fills exist: part of the
       order filled before cancellation. The fee is only on the filled part. Explain. Resolve.
     - status CANCELLED / EXPIRED / REJECTED with filled_quantity = 0 and no fills: there
       should be no fee. Check the ledger for FEE entries around the order time:
       Call get_ledger_entries(entry_type="FEE", from_time=<order created_at>,
            to_time=<order updated_at + 1 hour>).
       -> A FEE linked to this order or a trade with no fill: system error -> escalate Trading
          Ops, P2.
       -> No FEE: nothing was charged. The customer is probably seeing funds that were locked
          for the order and are now released (Call check_wallet_balance().
          Look at available), or a fee from
          another trade -> explain. Resolve.
     - Funds still locked after cancel for more than 15 minutes (sample value) -> escalate
       Trading Ops, P2 (withdrawal_sop WDR-10 explains locked funds).
RESOLUTION:
  Fee only on filled quantity; an unfilled cancelled order with a fee is a bug to escalate.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Thanks for flagging this. I checked your <buy/sell> order for <coin> on <date>. <Before you
  cancelled it, <filled quantity> of <total quantity> had already been bought/sold, and the
  fee of Rs <fee + gst> applies only to that filled part. Nothing was charged for the part
  you cancelled. / No part of the order was filled, so no fee should apply. We found a charge
  of Rs <x> and have passed it to our trading team to correct; we will update you within
  48 hours.>
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Fee on an unfilled order -> Trading Ops, P2, include order_id, ledger entry_id, amount.
DO NOT:
  - Do not reverse the fee yourself; Trading Ops corrects it.

## FEE-07: Unexpected deduction in the ledger / balance
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "Rs 523 deducted from my wallet, I did nothing"
  - "Unknown debit in my transaction history"
  - "My balance reduced without any trade"
  - "Bina kuch kiye paisa kam ho gaya"
  - "What is this adjustment entry?"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Approximate date and amount, asset (INR or coin)
TOOLS: get_ledger_entries, check_order, get_tickets
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify and verify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Call get_ledger_entries(from_time=<from>, to_time=<to>) (the customer's
     date range). Look at entries with amount < 0.
  3. Explain each debit by entry_type (plain words in brackets):
     - TRADE_BUY  (money used to buy a coin) -> Call check_order(order_id=<order_id>) and check
                  order.source; source = 'SIP' means an automatic SIP purchase (see sip_run:
                  sip_id, status, run_at). Customer forgot the SIP -> trade_sop.
     - TRADE_SELL (coin sold; the coin balance goes down, INR goes up)
     - FEE        (trading fee + GST, or Rs 10 withdrawal fee) -> FEE-02 / FEE-03
     - TDS        (1% tax on a sale) -> FEE-05
     - WITHDRAWAL (money sent to bank or crypto sent out) -> withdrawal_sop / crypto_transfers_sop
     - REVERSAL   (a credit that was undone, e.g. a deposit or withdrawal return corrected)
                  -> check reference_id; if the customer disputes it -> Payments Ops, P2
     - ADJUSTMENT (manual correction by Acme) -> check for a related ticket
                  (Call get_tickets().). Explain if a ticket explains it; otherwise
                  escalate Trading Ops (trade) or Payments Ops (INR), P2.
     - REWARD negative (reward revoked) -> rewards_sop.
  4. The customer says they did not place the trade or withdrawal -> account_security_sop
     SEC-01, P1. Stop here.
  5. No debit matches the amount: the customer may be looking at the value of their holdings
     (price change), not a deduction -> explain portfolio value vs balance (trade_sop).
RESOLUTION:
  Map every debit to its cause in plain language.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Thanks for asking us to check. I looked at your account for <date range>. The Rs <amount>
  on <date> was <a trading fee and GST on your sale of <coin> / an automatic purchase from
  your SIP for <coin> / the Rs 10 fee on your withdrawal / 1% TDS on your sale>.
  <Here is the breakdown: ...>
  If you do not recognise any of these, please reply right away so we can secure your account.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - ADJUSTMENT or REVERSAL with no explanation -> Trading Ops / Payments Ops, P2, include entry_id.
  - Customer denies an activity -> account_security_sop SEC-01, P1.
DO NOT:
  - Do not use internal entry type names in the reply.

## FEE-08: Fee refund requests
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "Please refund the fees, the app was lagging"
  - "Waive my fee, I am a loyal customer"
  - "Refund my trading fee, the order was placed by mistake"
  - "Fee wapas karo, galti se order lag gaya"
  - "Other apps have zero fee, refund me"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Order IDs / dates and why they think the fee should be refunded
TOOLS: get_price_incidents
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify and verify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. POLICY: fees are not refunded unless a system error is confirmed by Trading Ops.
     Mistaken orders, market moves, change of mind or comparisons with other apps do not
     qualify.
  3. Check for a possible system error:
     - Fee calculation wrong -> FEE-02.
     - Fee on an unfilled/cancelled order -> FEE-06.
     - Duplicate fees -> FEE-03 / FEE-09.
     - Platform incident during the trade:
       Call get_price_incidents(symbol=<symbol>, at_time=<executed_at>).
       -> Incident found: escalate Trading Ops, P3, with the order; Trading Ops applies the
          incident's compensation_policy. Do not promise anything.
     - Order rejected with SYSTEM_ERROR yet charged -> FEE-06 path, Trading Ops, P2.
  4. None of the above: politely decline. Resolve.
RESOLUTION:
  Decline standard fees; escalate only possible system errors.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Thanks for writing to us. I checked your <order/trades> on <date>. The trading fee of
  Rs <amount> was calculated correctly at 0.2% plus GST, and the order was processed as
  placed. Fees are refunded only when a trade is affected by an error on our side, and we did
  not find one here, so we are unable to refund this fee.
  <We found that there was a platform issue during your trade and have passed it to our
  trading team to review; we will update you within 5 working days.>
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Possible system error or matching price incident -> Trading Ops, P3 (P2 if also money
    missing), include order_id, trade_ids, incident_id, fee amounts.
DO NOT:
  - Do not promise or give a refund, credit, coupon or compensation yourself.
  - Do not reverse trades.

## FEE-09: Fee charged many times on one order (partial fills)
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "I placed one order but fee was charged 5 times"
  - "Multiple fee entries for a single buy"
  - "Ek order pe baar baar fee kat rahi hai"

  3. Branch:
     - fee_charged_twice is empty and totals.fee is about 0.2% of totals.value: the order

  which is our standard 0.2% plus GST. You were not charged more than once for the same part.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
