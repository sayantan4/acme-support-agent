## KYC-00: Scope, the KYC flow and quick triage
======================================================================
Covers identity verification from "Start KYC" to VERIFIED, every rejection reason, vendor
outages, attempt limits, re-KYC, and changes to name or address after verification.

The KYC flow (in-app, in this order):
  1. PAN: customer enters PAN; we verify it with the income-tax database (check_type
     PAN_VERIFY) and read the name and date of birth on PAN.
  2. Aadhaar: customer enters Aadhaar and an OTP sent by the Aadhaar system to the mobile
     linked with Aadhaar (check_type AADHAAR_OTP). We receive name, DOB, photo and address.
     We also check that PAN is linked to Aadhaar.
  3. Selfie + liveness: customer takes a live selfie; we check it is a live person
     (LIVENESS) and that it matches the Aadhaar/ID photo (FACE_MATCH).
  4. Screening: name is screened against watch lists (SANCTIONS). Never discussed.
  5. Decision: review_queue AUTO decides in minutes; MANUAL review takes up to 48 hours
     (DD-09).
Status mapping:
  kyc_applications.status: IN_PROGRESS (not yet submitted) -> SUBMITTED -> UNDER_REVIEW ->
  VERIFIED | REJECTED. RE_KYC_REQUIRED = was verified, needs fresh verification.
  users.kyc_status mirrors the latest application (NOT_STARTED if none).
Policies (DD-09): 18+, Indian resident, PAN linked to Aadhaar; max 3 attempts, after the 3rd
rejection the case goes to KYC Ops; automatic review minutes, manual review up to 48 hours.
KYC issues are P3 by default (DD-09), unless the customer cannot withdraw existing money
(P2) or a sanctions case (Risk & Compliance).

Accepted documents (sample policy):
  - PAN card (mandatory) - physical or e-PAN.
  - Aadhaar via OTP (preferred). If Aadhaar OTP is not possible: Aadhaar image upload
    (front and back) or passport as the ID/address document (doc_type PASSPORT).
  - ADDRESS_PROOF only when the current address differs from Aadhaar (KYC-18).
  - Not accepted: voter ID alone, driving licence alone, photocopies of photocopies, masked
    screenshots from other apps (sample policy).

TOOLS: get_kyc_applications, get_kyc_vendor_logs
Quick triage:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02). Check the user's status (BLOCKED ->
     onboarding_sop ONB-05; FROZEN -> fraud_risk_sop RSK-01; DORMANT/CLOSED ->
     account_closure_sop CLS-04/CLS-05).
  2. Get the KYC history:
     Call get_kyc_applications().
  3. Route:
     - No applications / IN_PROGRESS and customer is stuck on a step -> KYC-01 or KYC-14
     - SUBMITTED / UNDER_REVIEW < 48h                                -> KYC-01
     - UNDER_REVIEW >= 48h                                           -> KYC-02
     - REJECTED: route by rejection_reason_code
         PAN_INVALID -> KYC-04        PAN_NAME_MISMATCH -> KYC-05
         PAN_AADHAAR_NOT_LINKED -> KYC-06   AADHAAR_OTP_FAILED -> KYC-07
         SELFIE_MISMATCH -> KYC-08    LIVENESS_FAILED -> KYC-09
         DOC_UNREADABLE -> KYC-10     UNDERAGE -> KYC-11
         DUPLICATE_PAN -> KYC-12      SANCTIONS_HIT -> KYC-13 (never disclose)
         attempt_number = 3 and REJECTED -> also KYC-15
     - RE_KYC_REQUIRED                                               -> KYC-16
     - VERIFIED, wants name/address change                           -> KYC-17 / KYC-18
     - Documents question                                            -> KYC-03
  4. For any rejection, also check the vendor logs for 5xx/TIMEOUT:
     Call get_kyc_vendor_logs(kyc_id=<kyc_id>).
     If the vendor failed, it is KYC-14 regardless of the code shown.

## KYC-01: How long does KYC take / KYC status check (within SLA)
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "How long will KYC verification take?"
  - "Submitted KYC 2 hours ago, still under review"
  - "KYC kab tak verify hoga? Want to invest today"
  - "Status shows in review, is anything pending from my side?"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Registered phone/email
TOOLS: get_kyc_applications
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Call get_kyc_applications(). Use the latest (first) application.
     - If no application or status IN_PROGRESS: the customer has not submitted. Check which
       step is pending from that application's documents (doc_type, verification_status,
       failure_reason, uploaded_at).
       -> Tell them the next step (PAN / Aadhaar / selfie) to complete in the app. Ask customer.
     - If SUBMITTED or UNDER_REVIEW with review_queue AUTO and submitted < 1 hour ago:
       normal. Ask them to wait up to 1 hour (sample value). Wait.
     - If UNDER_REVIEW with review_queue MANUAL and submitted < 48 hours ago: normal manual
       review. Give the latest expected time (submitted_at + 48h, in IST). Wait.
     - If AUTO and older than 1 hour, or MANUAL and older than 48 hours -> KYC-02.
     - If VERIFIED: tell them it is done; they may need to restart the app. Resolve.
     - If REJECTED: route per KYC-00 triage.
  3. Check that nothing is pending from the customer's side: look at the documents returned
     by get_kyc_applications for this kyc_id with verification_status FAILED.
     - If any FAILED doc while status is still UNDER_REVIEW: a reviewer may request a
       re-upload; the app will notify. Wait up to 48 hours; if no request comes -> KYC-02.
RESOLUTION:
  Give the stage and a concrete expected time. Nothing can be expedited for normal cases.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Thanks for completing your verification. Your documents were received on <date, time
  IST> and are being reviewed. Most verifications finish within minutes; some need a manual
  check, which can take up to 48 hours. You should hear from us by <date, time IST>.
  You do not need to do anything right now. We will notify you in the app as soon as it is
  done.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Only per KYC-02 (SLA breached).
DO NOT:
  - Do not promise faster verification or a specific reviewer.
  - Do not mention "auto" or "manual queue" terms; say "a manual check".

## KYC-02: KYC stuck UNDER_REVIEW beyond 48 hours
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "KYC under review for 4 days, this is ridiculous"
  - "It's been more than 48 hours, still pending verification"
  - "Kitne din lagenge? 3 din ho gaye KYC pending hai"
  - "My friend got verified in 5 minutes, why is mine stuck?"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Registered phone/email
TOOLS: get_kyc_applications, get_kyc_vendor_logs, get_tickets
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Call get_kyc_applications(). Use the latest (first) application.
     - If submitted_at is less than 48 hours ago (MANUAL) -> KYC-01 (still within SLA). Wait.
  3. Check whether a vendor check is hanging:
     Call get_kyc_vendor_logs(kyc_id=<kyc_id>).
     - If a check has 5xx/TIMEOUT and no later success for that check_type: the
       application is waiting on a vendor retry -> KYC-14 handling; escalate KYC Ops, P3
       to re-trigger the check.
     - If a SANCTIONS check exists with any non-success response or the case sits in manual
       review with no other explanation: do not speculate; escalate to KYC Ops (P3), who
       will involve Risk & Compliance if needed. Reply with the standard delay template only.
     - If all checks passed and it is still UNDER_REVIEW: stuck in the manual queue.
       -> Escalate KYC Ops, P3 (P2 if the customer already has funds they cannot withdraw).
  4. Check for an existing ticket to avoid duplicates (support_guidelines_sop SUP-09):
     Call get_tickets(category="kyc", open_only=true).
     - If one exists and its SLA (P3: 5 working days) is breached: raise priority by one
       level and escalate again.
RESOLUTION:
  Apologise for the delay, escalate to KYC Ops, and give the P3 time frame.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  I am sorry your verification is taking longer than the usual 48 hours. I have checked your
  application: your documents are complete and nothing further is needed from you at this
  point. I have asked our verification team to prioritise it, and you should receive an
  update within 2 working days (sample value).
  If we need anything else from you, we will ask in the app.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - UNDER_REVIEW for 48+ hours -> KYC Ops, P3 (P2 if funds are stuck), include: user_id,
    kyc_id, submitted_at, review_queue, attempt_number, vendor log summary.
  - Customer has written 3+ times -> raise one priority level (support_guidelines_sop SUP-07).
DO NOT:
  - Do not mention screening, watch lists or the reason a case went to manual review.
  - Do not ask the customer to re-submit while an application is under review (it creates
    a new attempt).

## KYC-04: Rejected - PAN_INVALID (PAN not found)
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "KYC rejected saying PAN is invalid, but my PAN is correct"
  - "PAN not found error during verification"
  - "I recently got a new PAN, it says invalid"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Registered phone/email; confirmation the PAN was typed correctly (never ask for the
    full PAN over email - ask them to re-check it in the app)
TOOLS: get_kyc_applications, get_kyc_vendor_logs
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02) and get the latest application
     (KYC-00 step 2): Call get_kyc_applications().
     Confirm rejection_reason_code = PAN_INVALID.
  2. Check the vendor response for the PAN check:
     Call get_kyc_vendor_logs(kyc_id=<kyc_id>, check_types=["PAN_VERIFY"]).
     - If 5xx/TIMEOUT: the tax database lookup failed, not the PAN -> KYC-14.
     - Else continue.
  3. Likely customer-side causes:
     - Typo (O vs 0, I vs 1): ask them to re-enter carefully in the app. Ask customer.
     - Newly issued PAN: the tax database can take up to 15 days (sample value) to show a
       new PAN. Ask them to retry after that. Wait.
     - Deactivated or surrendered PAN (e.g. a duplicate PAN they held): they must use their
       active PAN. Ask customer.
  4. Check attempts left: 3 - attempt_number. If 0 -> KYC-15.
RESOLUTION:
  Customer re-checks and re-submits PAN in the app, or waits for a new PAN to activate.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Thanks for your patience. Your verification could not be completed because the PAN
  entered could not be found in the income-tax records.
  Please check the PAN carefully (for example, the letter O and the number 0 look similar)
  and submit again in the app. If your PAN was issued recently, it can take up to 15 days
  to become available for verification, so please try again after that.
  You have <n> verification attempts remaining.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Customer insists the PAN is valid and active (they can verify it on the income-tax
    portal) and the check failed twice -> KYC Ops, P3, include: kyc_id, vendor responses.
DO NOT:
  - Do not ask for the full PAN or a PAN photo over email.
  - Do not reset attempts yourself.

## KYC-05: Rejected - PAN_NAME_MISMATCH (name on PAN differs from Aadhaar)
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "KYC failed saying name mismatch, but it is the same name"
  - "My PAN has my full name and Aadhaar has initials, what to do?"
  - "After marriage my Aadhaar has new surname but PAN has old one"
  - "Name mismatch aa raha hai, spelling thoda different hai"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Registered phone/email; which document has the "correct" name
TOOLS: get_kyc_applications
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02) and get the latest application:
     Call get_kyc_applications().
  2. Compare the names: in that application's documents, look at the PAN, AADHAAR and
     PASSPORT rows (name_on_doc, verification_status, failure_reason).
     - If the difference is minor (initials vs expanded name, a middle name missing, an
       extra space, order of first/last name) and review_queue was AUTO: a manual review
       can often accept it. -> Escalate KYC Ops, P3 for manual name review. Wait (48 hours).
     - If the surname differs (marriage/name change) or spelling differs materially: the
       customer must update one document so both match (KYC-17 has the steps), then retry.
       Ask customer.
     - If the names are completely different: possible wrong document or someone else's
       document -> do not suggest workarounds; ask them to use their own PAN and Aadhaar.
       If repeated with different identities, escalate Risk & Compliance (P3).
  3. Note for later: the bank account holder name must also match the KYC name closely
     (bank_account_sop).
RESOLUTION:
  Minor differences -> manual review; material differences -> customer corrects PAN or
  Aadhaar with the issuing authority and re-submits.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Thanks for writing in. Your verification did not go through because the name on your PAN
  does not match the name on your Aadhaar.
  <Minor difference:> The difference looks small, so I have asked our verification team to
  review it manually. You will hear from us within 48 hours.
  <Material difference:> Please update your name on either PAN or Aadhaar so that both
  match, and then submit your verification again in the app. Updates are made with the
  issuing authority and can take a few weeks.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Minor mismatch (initials, order, middle name) -> KYC Ops, P3, include: kyc_id, both
    names (internal note only), attempt_number.
DO NOT:
  - Do not tell the customer which name we "prefer" or suggest editing one document only
    for Acme.
  - Do not quote the exact names from the documents back in full; describe the difference.

## KYC-06: Rejected - PAN_AADHAAR_NOT_LINKED (inoperative PAN)
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "It says my PAN is not linked with Aadhaar, but I linked it last week"
  - "PAN inoperative error during KYC"
  - "How do I link PAN and Aadhaar?"
  - "Linked PAN-Aadhaar yesterday, still rejected"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Registered phone/email; date they linked PAN and Aadhaar (if they did)
TOOLS: get_kyc_applications, get_kyc_vendor_logs
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02) and get the latest application:
     Call get_kyc_applications().
  2. Call get_kyc_vendor_logs(kyc_id=<kyc_id>, check_types=["PAN_VERIFY"], limit=3).
     - If 5xx/TIMEOUT -> KYC-14.
  3. Branches:
     - Customer has not linked: explain they must link PAN with Aadhaar on the income-tax
       portal (a government fee may apply), then retry. Ask customer.
     - Customer linked recently: the status can take up to 7 days (sample value) to update.
       Ask them to retry after that. Wait.
     - Customer linked more than 7 days ago and the income-tax portal shows "linked": ->
       Escalate KYC Ops, P3 to re-run the check.
  4. Check attempts left. Advise not to retry before the link shows on the portal, to
     avoid using up attempts.
RESOLUTION:
  Customer links PAN and Aadhaar, waits for it to reflect, then re-submits.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Your verification could not be completed because your PAN is not yet linked with your
  Aadhaar, which is required by law for financial accounts.
  Please link them on the income-tax e-filing portal. After linking, it can take up to 7
  days to reflect. Once the portal shows your PAN and Aadhaar as linked, please submit your
  verification again in the app. Please wait until then so you do not use up an attempt;
  you have <n> attempts remaining.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Linked for 7+ days (customer confirms portal shows linked) and still failing ->
    KYC Ops, P3, include: kyc_id, link date per customer, vendor responses.
DO NOT:
  - Do not give tax or legal advice beyond pointing to the official portal.

## KYC-07: Rejected - AADHAAR_OTP_FAILED
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "Aadhaar OTP not coming during KYC"
  - "My Aadhaar is linked to my old number, can't get OTP"
  - "Entered Aadhaar OTP, it said failed/timed out"
  - "Aadhaar wala OTP expire ho gaya"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Registered phone/email; whether their current mobile is linked to Aadhaar
TOOLS: get_kyc_applications, get_kyc_vendor_logs, get_kyc_vendor_health
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02) and get the latest application:
     Call get_kyc_applications().
  2. Call get_kyc_vendor_logs(kyc_id=<kyc_id>, check_types=["AADHAAR_OTP"]).
     For failures across users in the same hour: Call get_kyc_vendor_health(minutes=60).
     - If 5xx/TIMEOUT (or many AADHAAR_OTP failures across users in the same hour): the
       Aadhaar system or our vendor was down -> KYC-14.
     - If an "OTP not generated / mobile not linked" type response: the Aadhaar has no
       mobile linked, or it is an old number. -> Customer updates mobile at an Aadhaar
       centre, or uses the upload/passport alternative (KYC-03). Ask customer.
     - If "invalid OTP" / "OTP expired": customer entered it wrong or late. The Aadhaar OTP
       is valid for 10 minutes (sample value). Ask them to retry and enter it immediately.
       Ask customer.
     - Note: this OTP is sent by the Aadhaar system, not by Acme, so onboarding_sop ONB-01
       checks (get_otp_logs) do not apply.
RESOLUTION:
  Retry with the correct linked mobile, or use an alternative document.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Sorry for the trouble with the Aadhaar step. The OTP for this step is sent by the Aadhaar
  system to the mobile number linked with your Aadhaar, which may be different from the
  number you use with Acme Invest.
  <Cause-specific line.> Please try again in the app and enter the OTP within 10 minutes.
  If your Aadhaar is not linked to a mobile you can access, you can choose "Upload
  Aadhaar" or "Use passport" instead.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Repeated failures with a correctly linked mobile and no vendor error -> KYC Ops, P3,
    include: kyc_id, vendor responses and timestamps.
DO NOT:
  - Do not ask for the Aadhaar OTP or full Aadhaar number.

## KYC-08: Rejected - SELFIE_MISMATCH (selfie does not match ID photo)
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "KYC rejected saying face does not match, but it is my photo"
  - "My Aadhaar photo is 10 years old, that's why mismatch?"
  - "I have a beard now, selfie failed"
  - "Selfie match nahi ho raha"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Registered phone/email
TOOLS: get_kyc_applications, get_kyc_vendor_logs
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02) and get the latest application:
     Call get_kyc_applications().
  2. Call get_kyc_vendor_logs(kyc_id=<kyc_id>, check_types=["FACE_MATCH", "LIVENESS"]).
     Also look at the SELFIE document of that application (verification_status,
     failure_reason).
     - If FACE_MATCH has 5xx/TIMEOUT -> KYC-14.
     - If the mismatch is the first rejection: ask the customer to retake the selfie with
       the guidance below. Ask customer.
     - If this is the second SELFIE_MISMATCH, or the customer says the ID photo is very old
       or their appearance changed significantly: escalate KYC Ops (P3) for a manual face
       review instead of burning the last attempt. Wait (48 hours).
     - If the selfie clearly belongs to someone else (reviewer note / customer admits a
       family member took it): the account holder must complete it themselves. If an
       adult is verifying for someone else -> Risk & Compliance (P3).
RESOLUTION:
  Retake with better conditions; manual review for old ID photos.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Your verification did not go through because we could not match your selfie with the
  photo on your ID. This often happens with an old ID photo, poor light or a covered face.
  For your next try, please:
  - Sit facing a window or a bright light, with no light behind you
  - Remove glasses, cap or mask, and keep your whole face in the frame
  - Hold the phone at eye level and keep still
  <If escalated:> Because your ID photo is older, I have asked our team to review it
  manually; you will hear back within 48 hours.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Second SELFIE_MISMATCH, or known old-photo case -> KYC Ops, P3, include: kyc_id,
    attempt_number, vendor responses.
DO NOT:
  - Do not accept a selfie over email.
  - Do not disclose match scores.

## KYC-09: Rejected - LIVENESS_FAILED
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "Liveness check failed, I did everything it asked"
  - "Blink detection not working on my phone"
  - "It says take a live photo, I did"
  - "Camera ka issue hai kya? Selfie baar baar fail"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Registered phone/email, phone model/platform (optional)
TOOLS: get_kyc_applications, get_kyc_vendor_logs, get_user_devices
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02) and get the latest application:
     Call get_kyc_applications().
  2. Call get_kyc_vendor_logs(kyc_id=<kyc_id>, check_types=["LIVENESS"]).
     - If 5xx/TIMEOUT -> KYC-14.
     - If a failure mentions spoof / photo of a photo / screen detected: tell them to take
       the selfie live, not of a printed photo or another screen. If there are signs of
       deliberate spoofing across attempts -> Risk & Compliance, P3.
     - If failures mention low light, face not detected, multiple faces: guidance below.
  3. Check the device/app version (old versions can have camera issues):
     Call get_user_devices(). Use the first (most recently seen) device.
     - If the app is outdated: ask them to update before retrying. Ask customer.
     - If a camera error persists on the latest version -> app_technical_sop.
RESOLUTION:
  Retake in good light, alone, with an updated app.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Sorry the selfie step did not work. Our check needs to confirm that a real person is
  taking the selfie at that moment, and it can fail in low light or if the face is partly
  hidden.
  Please update the app, then try again: be alone in the frame, face a bright light,
  remove glasses or mask, and follow the on-screen prompts (such as blinking or turning
  your head) slowly. Please take the selfie directly, not a photo of a printed picture or
  another screen.
  You have <n> attempts remaining.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Genuine repeated failures on the latest app version -> KYC Ops, P3 (and
    app_technical_sop if a camera bug is suspected), include: kyc_id, platform, app_version.
  - Evidence of deliberate spoofing -> Risk & Compliance, P3.
DO NOT:
  - Do not accept selfies or videos by email.
  - Do not accuse the customer of fraud.
