## CMP-00: Scope and escalation ladder
======================================================================
Covers: general dissatisfaction, repeated follow-ups and SLA breaches, requests for a
manager or call-back, legal/police/regulator threats, abusive customers, media and social
media, deceased customers, compensation requests, and praise/feedback.

A complaint is usually about another problem (a deposit, a KYC rejection). Always:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Find and fix (or re-check) the underlying issue using its category SOP.
  3. Handle the complaint itself using this SOP.

Escalation ladder (sample timelines; Level 1 targets are the DD-09 SLAs):
  Level 1  Support (AI agent + support team)
           First response and resolution per DD-09 (P1 1h/24h, P2 4h/48h, P3 24h/5 working
           days, P4 48h/7 working days).
  Level 2  Support Lead
           When: customer unhappy with the Level 1 answer, SLA breached, 3+ contacts on the
           same issue (support_guidelines_sop SUP-07), manager requested, abusive customer.
           Target: acknowledgement within 24 hours, resolution within 3 working days
           (sample value).
  Level 3  Grievance Officer
           When: customer is not satisfied with the Level 2 outcome, formal grievance, or
           compensation request for a confirmed platform error. Target: acknowledgement within
           48 hours, final written response within 15 days (sample value).
  Special  Legal (with Grievance Officer): legal notices, police/court/regulator/consumer
           court/ombudsman/cybercrime portal mentions (CMP-04), deceased customer (CMP-07),
           media (CMP-06, with Comms).

Customers can raise a formal grievance in the app under Help > Raise a Grievance or by
writing to grievance@acmeinvest.example (sample values).

Complaint ticket fields: tickets.category = 'complaint', subcategory = the CMP topic (e.g.
'sla_breach', 'legal_threat'), priority per section, assigned_team per section.

Never auto-answer (support_guidelines_sop SUP-05): self-harm/violence, legal/regulator
threats, media, deceased customer, messages from banks/police/courts/government. Only the
SUP-05 holding reply may be sent in those cases.

## CMP-01: General complaint / dissatisfied with service
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "Worst app ever, my problem is not solved"
  - "Very disappointed with your service, nobody helps"
  - "Bahut bekaar service hai, 4 din se paisa atka hai"
  - "I want to file a formal complaint"
  - "Your replies are copy-paste, nobody reads my email"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - What the underlying problem is (if not clear from the thread), related IDs (UTR, order
    ID), what outcome they want
TOOLS: get_tickets
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02). Read the whole thread.
  2. Find related tickets:
     Call get_tickets(). Look at the latest 10.
  3. Identify the underlying issue and re-run that SOP's checks now (the issue may have
     resolved itself, or the previous answer may have been wrong).
     - Underlying issue now resolved: tell the customer the outcome clearly. Resolve.
     - Still in progress within SLA: give status and time frame. Resolve (wait).
     - SLA breached or 3+ contacts -> CMP-02.
     - Previous answer was wrong: apologise once, correct it, escalate if needed per that SOP.
  4. Complaint has no specific issue (general unhappiness, UI, policy): acknowledge, explain
     the policy briefly if relevant, log as feedback (CMP-10 / app_technical_sop APP-09).
  5. Customer explicitly asks for a formal grievance or is unhappy with a Level 1 answer
     already given twice: escalate to Level 2 Support Lead (P3, or the underlying issue's
     priority if higher). Share the grievance path.
RESOLUTION:
  Fix or re-check the actual problem, give a concrete status and next step; escalate per the
  ladder when the customer is not satisfied.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  I'm sorry that your experience with us has been frustrating, and thank you for telling us.
  I've gone through your earlier messages and checked your <issue> again: <what we found in
  plain words>. <next step and time frame>.
  If you are not satisfied with this, you can raise a formal grievance in the app under
  Help > Raise a Grievance, and a senior team member will review it.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Customer not satisfied after a correct Level 1 answer, or asks for a formal complaint ->
    Support Lead (Level 2), P3 (or higher if the underlying issue is higher), include:
    user_id, ticket_ids, summary of the issue, what was already told to the customer.
DO NOT:
  - Do not argue or defend at length; one short explanation of policy is enough.
  - Do not send a generic apology without checking the underlying issue.

## CMP-02: Repeated follow-ups / SLA breached
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "This is my 5th email, still no resolution"
  - "You said 48 hours, it's been a week"
  - "Koi reply nahi karta, ticket kab close hoga?"
  - "Escalate this now, I'm tired of waiting"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Ticket number if they have one (otherwise find by user)
TOOLS: get_tickets
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Open tickets and SLA check:
     Call get_tickets(open_only=true).
     Compare against DD-09: P1 1h/24h, P2 4h/48h, P3 24h/5 working days, P4 48h/7 working days.
     - first_response_at IS NULL and past the first-response target: first-response breach.
     - created_at + resolution target < now() and not resolved: resolution breach.
  3. Count contacts on the same issue:
     Call get_tickets(category=<category>, days=30). Count the tickets.
     - 3 or more: escalate per support_guidelines_sop SUP-07 even if SLA not breached.
  4. Re-run the underlying SOP's checks (support_guidelines_sop SUP-09 step 2).
     - Resolved now: tell the customer, close the ticket(s), merge duplicates. Resolve.
     - Still open, within SLA, fewer than 3 contacts: give status and remaining time. Wait.
     - SLA breached: apologise, raise priority by one level (P4->P3, P3->P2, P2->P1; P1 stays
       P1 and goes to Support Lead immediately), escalate to the owning team and notify
       Support Lead (Level 2).
     - Status PENDING_CUSTOMER: we are waiting for something from the customer; clearly
       restate what is needed (one message). Wait.
     - Status PENDING_INTERNAL with a team: chase that team with the breach flagged.
  5. Merge duplicate tickets on the same issue into the oldest ticket so the customer gets
     one owner.
RESOLUTION:
  Honest status, apology for the delay, a raised priority, and a firm next update time.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  I'm sorry you've had to follow up so many times - that shouldn't happen. I've checked your
  request from <date> again: <current status in plain words>.
  I have now escalated it on priority to the team handling it, and you will get an update
  from us by <date/time IST based on the new priority's first-response target>. You don't need
  to send another email - all your messages are linked to the same request.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - SLA breached -> owning team at priority raised by one level + Support Lead (Level 2),
    include: user_id, ticket_ids, original priority, breach type, underlying checks.
  - Breached again after Level 2 escalation -> Grievance Officer (Level 3), P2 or higher.
DO NOT:
  - Do not give a new time frame shorter than the team can meet; use the DD-09 SLA.
  - Do not blame another team or name it.

## CMP-03: Request to speak to a manager / call-back
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "I want to talk to your manager"
  - "Give me a phone number, I want to speak to someone"
  - "Please call me back on my number"
  - "Senior se baat karao"
  - "Why doesn't Acme have customer care number?"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Registered phone (for P1/P2 call-back), preferred time window, the underlying issue
TOOLS: get_tickets
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Find the underlying issue and its ticket priority:
     Call get_tickets(open_only=true).
  3. Policy (sample): Acme has no inbound phone support. Call-backs are offered only for P1/P2
     issues, within 24 hours, to the registered phone number only.
     - Issue is P1 or P2: arrange a call-back -> Support Lead, same priority. Tell the
       customer the call will come from Acme within 24 hours to their registered number and
       that the caller will never ask for OTP/password.
     - Issue is P3/P4: explain email/chat is the channel; give the concrete status of the
       issue. If they insist on a manager: escalate in writing to Support Lead (Level 2).
  4. Customer asks for a call on a number that is not registered: do not call it; only the
     registered number (support_guidelines_sop SUP-01).
  5. Warn about fake "Acme customer care" numbers found on search engines/social media
     (support_guidelines_sop SUP-06). Acme does not publish a helpline number (sample value).
RESOLUTION:
  Call-back within 24h for P1/P2 via Support Lead; written Level 2 escalation otherwise.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE (P1/P2):
  Hi <first name>,
  I understand you'd like to speak with someone about <issue>. I've arranged a call-back from
  a senior member of our team to your registered mobile number ending <last 4 digits> within
  24 hours. Our team will never ask for your OTP, password or ask you to install any app.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE (P3/P4):
  Hi <first name>,
  I understand you'd like to speak with someone. We don't offer phone support for this type
  of request, but I've shared your case with a senior team member, who will review it and
  reply to you by email within 24 hours. Current status: <status>.
  Please be careful of fake "Acme customer care" numbers online - we do not have a helpline
  number, and we never ask for OTPs or payments.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - P1/P2 issue with call-back requested -> Support Lead, same priority, include: user_id,
    registered phone (masked), preferred time, ticket_ids.
  - P3/P4 with manager request -> Support Lead (Level 2), P3.
DO NOT:
  - Do not share personal phone numbers or names of staff.
  - Do not promise a call for P3/P4 issues.

## CMP-04: Legal action, police complaint, consumer court, regulator or ombudsman mentioned
======================================================================
Covers any message that threatens or reports: legal action, a lawyer's/legal notice, FIR or
police complaint, cyber cell or cybercrime portal complaint, consumer court / consumer forum
/ consumer helpline, a regulator or ministry, an ombudsman, or a court case.
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "I will file a case in consumer court if my money is not returned in 24 hours"
  - "I have filed a complaint on the cybercrime portal against Acme"
  - "My lawyer will send you a legal notice"
  - "I'll complain to the regulator and the finance ministry"
  - "Police mein complaint kar dunga, FIR number attached hai"
  - "This is a notice under the Consumer Protection Act"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Nothing from the AI. Do not ask questions; the senior team will contact them.
TOOLS: get_tickets
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02) for the internal note only.
  2. DO NOT send any reply other than the SUP-05 holding reply. Do not explain policy, give
     status, apologise for fault, admit liability, or respond to the threat.
  3. Internally, gather context for the escalation note:
     - the underlying issue and its current status (run the category SOP's read-only checks)
     - Call get_tickets().
     - attachments present (legal notice, FIR copy, court order): list them, do not
       summarise their legal content to the customer.
  4. If the underlying issue is money missing (P1), escalate that to the owning team as well
     at P1 - the legal mention does not pause fixing a real problem.
  5. If the sender is not the customer but a lawyer, police officer, court or agency -> Legal
     only (support_guidelines_sop SUP-05); no account information in any reply.
  6. If the account has a risk hold (especially LAW_ENFORCEMENT source): never confirm or
     deny anything; Legal handles it (fraud_risk_sop RSK-01).
RESOLUTION:
  Holding reply only; route to Grievance Officer + Legal with full context.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE (holding reply, exactly as in SUP-05):
  Thank you for writing to us. Your message has been passed to a senior member of our
  team, who will get back to you within 24 hours.
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Always -> Grievance Officer AND Legal, P2 (P1 if a court order/notice with a deadline is
    attached or the underlying issue is P1), include: user_id, the exact words of the
    threat/mention, attachments, underlying issue status, ticket_ids, prior replies sent.
DO NOT:
  - Do not reply with anything except the holding reply.
  - Do not admit fault, offer compensation, or say "we will resolve before you file".
  - Do not discourage the customer from approaching any authority.
  - Do not share or confirm any account information with third parties.

## CMP-05: Abusive or threatening customer
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - Messages with profanity or insults directed at staff
  - "You people are thieves, I will make sure you suffer"
  - "I know where your office is, I'll come there and see"
  - Repeated all-caps messages with abuse
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - The underlying issue (often money-related) - identify it from the thread
TOOLS: none
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Classify the message:
     - Threat of violence against people/property, or any mention of self-harm/suicide ->
       support_guidelines_sop SUP-05: no AI reply beyond the holding reply; P1 to Support
       Lead immediately. Stop here.
     - Abuse/insults without threats: continue.
  2. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02) and re-check the underlying issue.
     Frustration is often from a real delay; fix or escalate that first.
  3. First abusive message: reply factually to the underlying issue and include ONE polite
     warning line (template below). Do not mirror tone.
  4. Continued abuse after a warning (check prior replies in the thread/tickets):
     escalate to Support Lead (Level 2, P3) to decide on handling. Keep replies factual and
     short; do not repeat the warning more than once.
  5. Abuse includes a legal threat -> also CMP-04. Mentions posting publicly -> CMP-09.
RESOLUTION:
  Stay factual, solve the underlying issue, one warning, escalate if it continues.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE (first abusive message):
  Hi <first name>,
  I understand you're upset about <issue>, and I want to help sort it out. I checked your
  request: <status in plain words and next step with time frame>.
  To help us assist you, please keep the conversation respectful. We may not be able to
  continue responding to messages that contain abusive language.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Threats of violence or self-harm -> Support Lead, P1 (SUP-05), include: full message,
    user_id, any location or time mentioned.
  - Abuse continues after one warning -> Support Lead, P3, include: user_id, thread,
    underlying issue status.
DO NOT:
  - Do not respond with sarcasm, threats or account restrictions.
  - Do not stop working on a genuine money issue because the customer is rude.
  - Do not send more than one warning.

## CMP-06: Media, journalist, influencer or viral social media post
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "I am a journalist with <publication>, I want Acme's comment on frozen accounts"
  - "I have 200k followers, fix my issue or I'll make a video exposing Acme"
  - "My tweet about you has gone viral, 10,000 people have seen it"
  - "We are writing a story about crypto scams and your platform"
  - "Video bana ke daalunga agar paisa nahi aaya"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Nothing from the AI. Do not ask questions.
TOOLS: none
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Classify:
     - Journalist / media organisation / request for comment -> no AI reply beyond the
       holding reply (support_guidelines_sop SUP-05). Route to Legal + Comms. Do not look up
       or discuss any account.
     - Customer who is (or claims to be) an influencer threatening a post, or whose post has
       gone viral: identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02), check the underlying
       issue internally, send only the holding reply, route to Legal + Comms and to Support
       Lead for the underlying issue.
  2. If a real P1 issue exists (money missing, unauthorized access) escalate it at P1 to the
     owning team in parallel - media involvement never delays a fix.
  3. For posts already public: also see CMP-09 for the public-reply rules (handled by the
     social/Comms team, not the AI).
RESOLUTION:
  Holding reply only; Legal/Comms decide the response. No comment on any matter.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE (holding reply, exactly as in SUP-05):
  Thank you for writing to us. Your message has been passed to a senior member of our
  team, who will get back to you within 24 hours.
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Always -> Legal (and Comms), P2 (P1 if a viral post alleges a security breach or
    widespread loss of funds), include: sender, outlet/handle, link to post, follower count
    if stated, user_id if a customer, underlying issue status.
DO NOT:
  - Do not give any statement, comment, figures or "off the record" information.
  - Do not confirm whether a person is an Acme customer.
  - Do not offer anything to have a post removed.

## CMP-07: Customer has died - legal heir / nominee request
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "My father passed away last month, he had crypto in Acme, how do we claim it?"
  - "I am the nominee of my late husband's account"
  - "Papa ka death ho gaya, unke account ka paisa kaise milega?"
  - "Please close my deceased brother's account and transfer his funds to me"
  - "What documents are required for transmission of a deceased person's holdings?"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Nothing from the AI in the first reply (SUP-05). Legal will request documents securely.
    For internal routing only: the deceased's registered phone or email if the sender gave it.
TOOLS: find_user, get_auth_events
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Send only the SUP-05 holding reply. Do not confirm whether the deceased had an account,
     and do not share any balance or transaction information by email with anyone until
     Legal has verified the claimant.
  2. Internally, find the account if details were given (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02
     lookups by phone/email), for the escalation note only:
     Call find_user(email=<email>, phone=<phone>).
  3. Protect the account: recent logins after the stated date of death may indicate misuse:
     Call get_auth_events(from_time=<date_of_death>).
     - Any activity after the date of death -> flag in the escalation as urgent (P1) so Legal
       /Risk & Compliance can restrict the account.
  4. Escalate to Legal. Legal (not support) then contacts the claimant and requests documents
     via the secure in-app/portal channel (sample policy), typically:
       - Death certificate (issued by the local authority)
       - Claimant's KYC: PAN and Aadhaar (or passport)
       - Proof of relationship with the deceased
       - If registered as nominee: nominee claim form (sample value) + the above
       - If total holdings exceed Rs 5,00,000 (sample value) and there is no nominee, or if
         there is a dispute between heirs: succession certificate or legal heir certificate
         from a competent court/authority; below the threshold, an indemnity bond and
         no-objection from other legal heirs may be accepted (sample value)
       - Claimant's own bank account details in their name for payout
  5. The support team may answer general "what documents are usually needed" questions only
     AFTER Legal takes over, using Legal's wording; the AI's first reply remains the holding
     reply.
RESOLUTION:
  Holding reply; Legal verifies the claimant and handles transmission of holdings. Nothing
  about the account is shared by email before verification.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE (holding reply, exactly as in SUP-05):
  Thank you for writing to us. Your message has been passed to a senior member of our
  team, who will get back to you within 24 hours.
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Always -> Legal, P2 (P1 if there is account activity after the date of death), include:
    user_id (if found), claimant's name and stated relationship, date of death as stated,
    whether a nominee is mentioned, auth_events after date of death.
DO NOT:
  - Do not express condolences in a way that confirms an account exists (the holding reply
    is enough; Legal will personalise).
  - Do not share balances, holdings, statements or transaction history.
  - Do not ask for the deceased's OTP, password or device.
  - Do not close the account or move funds on email instruction.

## CMP-08: Compensation request
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "I lost Rs 20,000 because the app was down, compensate me"
  - "Your delay cost me profit, I want interest for 5 days"
  - "Give me some bonus for this bad experience"
  - "Refund my trading fees as compensation"
  - "Mera loss aap bharo, aapki galti hai"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - What happened, when (IST), which orders/transactions, the loss they are claiming and how
    they calculated it
TOOLS: get_price_incidents, get_orders
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Policy: support never promises or offers compensation, credits, fee refunds, interest or
     goodwill rewards (support_guidelines_sop SUP-04). Compensation is decided only by the
     Grievance Officer, and only for a confirmed platform error.
  3. Check whether a platform error is on record:
     Call get_price_incidents(at_time=<event_time>).
     Call get_orders(from_time=<from>, to_time=<to>).
     - Recorded incident AND the customer's orders/transactions were affected (e.g.
       reject_reason = SYSTEM_ERROR during the window): possible platform error -> escalate
       to Grievance Officer (Level 3) with Trading Ops context, P2.
     - No incident and orders behaved normally (market movement, slippage, their own error):
       explain what happened with facts; no compensation. Resolve. If they insist -> Level 2.
     - Money delay within SLA (e.g. deposit 5 minutes late): explain SLA; no compensation.
       Resolve.
     - Money delay beyond SLA: fix the underlying issue first (CMP-02), note the claim for
       Grievance Officer (P3).
  4. Tell the customer the claim will be reviewed (if escalated) without implying an outcome.
RESOLUTION:
  No compensation from support. Explain facts; escalate confirmed/possible platform errors to
  the Grievance Officer, whose decision is final at Level 3.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE (escalated):
  Hi <first name>,
  I'm sorry for the trouble you faced on <date>. I checked your <orders/transaction>, and I
  have passed your request, with the details of what happened, to our Grievance Officer for
  review. You will receive a written response within 15 days. I'm not able to confirm any
  outcome at this stage.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE (no platform error):
  Hi <first name>,
  Thanks for explaining what happened. I checked your <order/transaction> on <date>: <plain
  facts, e.g. "the market order was filled at Rs X because the price moved quickly at that
  time; our systems were working normally">. As this was not caused by an error on our
  platform, we are not able to offer compensation. If you'd like a senior review, you can
  raise a grievance under Help > Raise a Grievance.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Possible platform error affecting the customer -> Grievance Officer, P2, include:
    user_id, incident_id, affected order/transaction IDs, customer's claimed amount and
    calculation.
  - Customer disputes a "no error" finding -> Support Lead (Level 2), P3.
DO NOT:
  - Do not offer or hint at compensation, cashback, fee waivers or rewards.
  - Do not agree with the customer's loss calculation.
  - Do not admit fault before the Grievance Officer's review.

## CMP-09: Complaint on social media
======================================================================
Applies when a complaint is posted publicly (tickets.channel = 'SOCIAL') or a customer
emails a link to their public post. Public replies are posted by the social team; the AI
drafts them using this template.
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "@AcmeInvest my withdrawal pending for 3 days, is this a scam?"
  - "Posted on your page, nobody responds there either"
  - "Sharing my UTR and phone here so you can check fast: ..."
  - "Acme is looting people, don't use it"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Nothing publicly. Move to DM/email to identify and verify the customer.
TOOLS: none
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Never look up or discuss account details based on a public post alone.
  2. Classify the post:
     - Mentions legal/police/regulator -> CMP-04 handling (public reply: none; Legal decides).
     - Journalist or high-reach/viral post -> CMP-06 (Legal/Comms).
     - Threats of violence or self-harm -> SUP-05, P1 Support Lead.
     - Customer posted personal data (phone, UTR, PAN, bank number): advise them publicly to
       delete it; ask the social team to hide the post if the platform allows.
     - Scam claims about Acme or impersonation accounts -> Legal (P3).
     - Normal complaint -> public reply template, then handle privately via the right SOP.
  3. Once the customer DMs/emails: identify them (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02), verify
     (SUP-01), check tickets, and follow the underlying SOP. Link the social ticket to the
     existing ticket to avoid duplicates.
RESOLUTION:
  Short, polite public reply with no account details; move to private channel; resolve there.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE (public reply):
  Hi <handle>, we're sorry to hear about this and want to help. For your security, please
  don't share account details, phone numbers or transaction IDs publicly. Please send us a
  direct message or write to support@acmeinvest.example from your registered email, and
  we'll look into it right away. - Acme Invest Support
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE (private, after they contact us):
  Hi <first name>,
  Thanks for getting in touch privately after your post. I checked your <issue>: <status in
  plain words and next step with time frame>.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Viral post, influencer or media -> CMP-06 (Legal/Comms), P2.
  - Legal/regulator mention -> CMP-04, P2.
  - Personal data posted publicly -> social team to hide, P3.
DO NOT:
  - Never include names, balances, transaction status, KYC status or any account detail in a
    public reply, even if the customer posted it first.
  - Do not argue publicly or reply more than once or twice in public.
  - Do not ask the customer to post more information publicly.

## CMP-10: Feedback and praise
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "Thanks, the issue is solved, great support!"
  - "Loving the app, very smooth"
  - "Bahut accha app hai, keep it up"
  - "Your SIP feature is excellent, thank you"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - None
TOOLS: none
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Check the message has no hidden request or issue (e.g. "thanks, but one more thing...").
     - Contains a question/issue: handle it with the right SOP.
