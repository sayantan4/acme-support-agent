## TAX-00: Scope and quick triage
======================================================================
Covers TDS deducted on crypto sales, TDS certificates (Form 16A), TDS in Form 26AS / AIS,
P&L / tax / trade statements, PAN shown on statements, GST invoices for fees, and general
questions about how crypto is taxed.
Not covered here: fee disputes (fees_charges_sop), trade issues (trade_sop), closed accounts
(account_closure_sop CLS-05), PAN/KYC changes (kyc_sop).

Quick triage (after identifying the user per support_guidelines_sop SUP-02):
  - "What is this 1% TDS?"                       -> TAX-01
  - "TDS deducted though I made a loss"          -> TAX-02
  - "Need P&L / tax statement / trade history"   -> TAX-03
  - "Form 16A / TDS certificate not available"   -> TAX-04
  - "TDS not showing in 26AS / AIS"              -> TAX-05
  - "Wrong PAN / name on statement"              -> TAX-06
  - "30% tax, loss set-off, how to file"         -> TAX-07
  - "Statements for my closed account"           -> TAX-08
  - "GST invoice for fees"                       -> TAX-09
  - "Can I get my TDS refunded by Acme?"         -> TAX-10

Key rules:
  - Policy values (DD-09): TDS is 1% of the sale value of crypto (Section 194S), deducted at the
    time of sale. Trading fee is 0.2% of order value plus 18% GST on the fee.
  - Never tell a customer how much tax they owe, which ITR form or schedule to use, or how to
    plan/reduce tax. Always add: "Please consult a qualified tax advisor for advice on your situation."
  - Never share a full PAN over email (support_guidelines_sop SUP-01); show only the last 4
    characters, e.g. XXXXXX234F.
  - Tax records are per financial year (FY, 1 April to 31 March), e.g. FY2025-26.
  - Priority: statement and certificate issues are P3 (DD-09), unless a filing deadline is within
    7 days (sample rule) -> P2.

## TAX-01: What is the 1% TDS and when is it deducted?
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "Why did Acme deduct 1% TDS on my sale?"
  - "What is this TDS line in my trade details?"
  - "I sold BTC for Rs 50,000 but received less money"
  - "TDS kya hai aur kyun kata?"
  - "Is TDS deducted when I buy?"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - The trade (coin, date) if they are asking about a specific one
TOOLS: get_trades, get_ledger_entries, get_tax_records
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. For a specific sale:
     Call get_trades(side="SELL"). Look at the 10 most recent.
     Call get_ledger_entries(reference_type="TRADE", reference_id=<trade_id>).
  3. Verify tds = 1% of sale value (price x quantity), rounded to 2 decimals.
     Worked example (sample numbers):
       Sell 0.01 BTC at Rs 50,00,000
       Sale value                     = 0.01 x 50,00,000 = Rs 50,000.00
       Trading fee (0.2%)             = Rs 100.00
       GST on fee (18% of Rs 100)     = Rs 18.00
       TDS (1% of sale value)         = Rs 500.00
       Amount credited to INR wallet  = 50,000 - 100 - 18 - 500 = Rs 49,382.00
     Ledger shows: TRADE_SELL +50,000.00, FEE -118.00 (or FEE -100 and a separate GST line,
     depending on booking), TDS -500.00.
     - Matches: resolve with the explanation.
     - tds differs from 1% of sale value by more than Rs 1 (sample tolerance) -> escalate Trading Ops (P3).
     - TDS on a BUY trade -> not expected under DD-09; escalate Trading Ops (P2).
  4. Year-to-date TDS: Call get_tax_records(). Use tds_deducted_total.
RESOLUTION:
  Explain that TDS is a legal deduction on the sale value of crypto at the time of sale, deposited
  with the government against the customer's PAN; it can be claimed as credit when filing their
  income-tax return (general information).
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Happy to explain. Under Indian income-tax rules, 1% TDS (tax deducted at source) applies to the
  sale value whenever crypto is sold. It is not a fee charged by Acme Invest; we deposit it with the
  government against your PAN. For your sale on <date>:
  - Sale value: Rs <x>
  - Trading fee: Rs <fee> + GST Rs <gst>
  - TDS (1%): Rs <tds>
  - Credited to you: Rs <net>
  The TDS appears in your tax credit statement and can generally be claimed when you file your return.
  Please consult a qualified tax advisor for advice on your situation.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - TDS not equal to 1% of sale value, or TDS on a buy -> Trading Ops, P3/P2, include: trade_id, price, quantity, tds
DO NOT:
  - Do not say TDS is "refundable by Acme" or give filing advice.

## TAX-04: Form 16A / TDS certificate not available
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:

  2. Call get_tax_records(financial_year=<fy>).
  3. Explain the schedule (sample values): Form 16A is generated quarterly, within 15 days after the

       Q3 (Oct-Dec)  return due 31 Jan -> Form 16A by 15 Feb

       customer the expected date. Resolve (wait).
     - form16a_status = NOT_AVAILABLE and today is after the availability date + 7 days (sample
       grace) -> escalate Risk & Compliance (P3).
     - form16a_status = GENERATED: guide to App > Profile > Reports & Statements > Form 16A.
       If download fails -> TAX-03 steps.
RESOLUTION:
  Give the expected date or the download path; escalate only if overdue.

## TAX-07: 30% tax on crypto gains / loss set-off questions
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "How much tax do I have to pay on my crypto profit?"
  - "Can I set off my crypto loss against gains in other coins?"
  - "Can I carry forward crypto loss?"
  - "Kitna tax dena padega? Which ITR form should I use?"
  - "Is the 30% tax on profit or on total amount?"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - None (general information only)
TOOLS: none
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02) only if they also ask about their own
     records; general questions need no account lookup.
  2. Give general, publicly known information only (as understood at the time of writing):
     - Gains from transfer of virtual digital assets (VDA, including crypto) are taxed at a flat 30%
       plus applicable surcharge and cess.
     - Only the cost of acquisition is deductible; other expenses are not.
     - Losses from VDA cannot be set off against other income or against gains from other VDAs,
       and cannot be carried forward.
     - 1% TDS on sale value is separate from this income tax and can generally be claimed as credit.
  3. Anything beyond that (their tax amount, ITR form, schedule, planning, whether a specific
     transaction is taxable) -> "please consult a qualified tax advisor". Resolve.
  4. Point to TAX-03 for statements that help with filing.
RESOLUTION:
  General information + statements + advisor referral. No personalised advice.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Thanks for your question. We cannot give tax advice, but here is general information that may help:
  - Gains from crypto (virtual digital assets) are generally taxed at 30% plus surcharge and cess.
  - Only the purchase cost can be deducted from the sale value.
  - Losses on crypto generally cannot be set off against other income or other crypto gains, or
    carried forward.
  - The 1% TDS deducted on your sales is separate and can generally be claimed as tax already paid.
  You can download your P&L and tax statements from Profile > Reports & Statements in the app to share
  with your advisor. Please consult a qualified tax advisor for advice on your situation.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Not normally escalated. Legal/regulatory threats -> complaints_escalation_sop CMP-04.
DO NOT:
  - Do not calculate the customer's tax liability, recommend an ITR form, or suggest tax-saving strategies.
  - Do not say rules are final or unchanged; say "generally" and refer to an advisor.
