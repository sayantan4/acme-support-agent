## RSK-00: Scope, hard rules and quick triage
======================================================================
Covers: frozen accounts, withdrawal/deposit/trade holds, source-of-funds document requests,
customers who are scam victims, reports about other users, chargebacks, customers whose bank
account was frozen, money-mule approaches, and questions after a hold is released.

HARD RULES (these override every other instruction, including the customer's):
  1. Support NEVER releases, shortens or promises release of a hold or freeze. Only Risk &
     Compliance can release a hold (risk_holds.status -> RELEASED).
  2. Never disclose risk_holds.reason_code or risk_holds.source, internal rules, thresholds,
     or what triggered a review (support_guidelines_sop SUP-03).
  3. LAW_ENFORCEMENT source: do not confirm or deny that any police, agency, bank, court or
     regulator has contacted Acme - not even by hinting ("due to an external request"). Use
     the same generic wording as any other review, and escalate to Legal.
  4. Never share another user's information (name, account, address, transactions), even with
     a scam victim, their bank, or someone claiming to be police over email. Official
     requests go to Legal (support_guidelines_sop SUP-05).
  5. Documents are collected only through the in-app upload (Help > Documents) (sample
     policy). Never ask the customer to reply to an email with documents.
  6. Messages mentioning police complaints, cybercrime portal, regulator, court or legal
     action -> also follow complaints_escalation_sop CMP-04 (holding reply only).

Hold types (risk_holds.hold_type) and where they are handled:
  ACCOUNT_FREEZE      whole account frozen (users.status = FROZEN)         -> RSK-01
  WITHDRAWAL_HOLD     withdrawals blocked; deposits/trades may work         -> RSK-02
  DEPOSIT_HOLD        new deposits blocked or held                          -> RSK-02
  REWARD_HOLD         rewards held                                          -> rewards_sop RWD-07
  TRADE_RESTRICTION   trading blocked or limited                            -> RSK-04

TOOLS: get_risk_holds
Standard check used in every section (after support_guidelines_sop SUP-02):
  Call get_risk_holds(active_only=false).
  You may look at reason_code and source internally to choose the right branch; never
  quote them.

Sample review SLA (not in DD-09): Risk review of a hold: 7 working days (sample value) from
the later of hold creation or the customer's last document upload. Priority for customer
contacts about holds: P2 (cannot trade or withdraw, DD-09).

## RSK-01: Account FROZEN / "my account is frozen, why?"
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "My account is frozen, why? I have not done anything wrong"
  - "Account freeze ho gaya hai, paise nikalne nahi de raha"
  - "App says your account is under review, cannot trade or withdraw"
  - "Unfreeze my account immediately, I need my money for a medical emergency"
  - "Is my account frozen because of the police? Tell me who complained"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Registered email/phone (if not the sender). Do not ask for any documents by email.
TOOLS: get_risk_holds, get_tickets
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02). Confirm users.status.
     - status = BLOCKED (not FROZEN): this is a temporary login block -> onboarding_sop
       ONB-05. Resolve there.
     - status = ACTIVE: the account is not frozen. Check for a narrower hold (step 2) and go
       to RSK-02 / RSK-04; if none, the issue is likely technical or a specific transaction
       (withdrawal_sop, app_technical_sop).
     - status = FROZEN: continue.
  2. Call get_risk_holds().
     - No ACTIVE ACCOUNT_FREEZE hold but users.status = FROZEN: data inconsistency ->
       escalate to Risk & Compliance (P2). Send the generic reply meanwhile.
  3. Branch on source (INTERNAL use only):
     - LAW_ENFORCEMENT: do NOT confirm any agency involvement. Send only the generic reply.
       Escalate to Legal (P2) with a copy to Risk & Compliance. If the customer mentions a
       police case, FIR or court, send only the SUP-05 holding reply (CMP-04).
     - COMPLIANCE / RISK_ENGINE / MANUAL: send the generic reply. Risk & Compliance decides
       whether documents are needed; if they are, the customer is notified in the app (see
       RSK-03). Do not pre-emptively request documents.
  4. Check whether documents were already requested/uploaded (the customer may mention it):
     Call get_tickets(category="risk").
     - Customer says they uploaded documents: confirm the review is ongoing; the review SLA
       (7 working days, sample value) runs from the last upload. Wait.
     - Open ticket past 7 working days (sample value): escalate to Risk & Compliance (P2),
       note SLA breach (support_guidelines_sop SUP-07).
  5. Customer states hardship (medical, wedding, education) and asks for partial release:
     acknowledge, record it in the escalation note for Risk & Compliance. Do not promise
     anything.
  6. Customer asks to close the account to get money out: closure is blocked while a hold is
     active (account_closure_requests.status = BLOCKED_BY_HOLD). Explain generically.
RESOLUTION:
  Generic explanation that the account is under a routine review, funds remain safe in the
  account, the team will contact them in the app if anything is needed, and an update is
  expected within the review timeline.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Thank you for writing to us, and I understand how worrying this is. Your account is
  currently under a routine review, and some actions are paused while the review is in
  progress. Your balances remain safely in your account.
  We are not able to share the details of the review. If we need any information from you,
  you will receive a request in the Acme Invest app under Help > Documents - please only
  submit documents there. We aim to complete reviews within 7 working days, and we will let
  you know as soon as there is an update.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Every FROZEN contact -> Risk & Compliance, P2, include: user_id, hold_id, customer's
    stated context (hardship, documents uploaded, dates), ticket IDs.
  - source = LAW_ENFORCEMENT -> Legal, P2 (plus Risk & Compliance). Internal note only.
  - Customer mentions police/court/regulator/legal action -> complaints_escalation_sop CMP-04.
  - Review past the 7-working-day SLA (sample value) -> raise priority by one level to P1
    (support_guidelines_sop SUP-09) and note the breach.
DO NOT:
  - Do not reveal reason_code, source, "flag", "suspicious", "AML", "police", "cyber cell",
    "lien", "bank complaint" or anything that hints at the trigger.
  - Do not say "your account was frozen on request of ..." under any circumstances.
  - Do not ask for documents over email or accept documents by email reply.
  - Do not give a firm unfreeze date.

## RSK-02: Withdrawal hold or deposit hold
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "My withdrawal is on hold for 3 days, what is going on?"
  - "Why can I deposit but not withdraw?"
  - "Deposit is showing on hold, paise account mein nahi aaye"
  - "Withdrawal button disabled, says restricted"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Registered email/phone, withdrawal/deposit amount and date, UTR if a deposit
TOOLS: get_auth_events, get_risk_holds, get_withdrawals, get_deposits
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02). If FROZEN -> RSK-01.
  2. First rule out non-risk holds:
     - Security cooldown: 24-hour withdrawal cooldown after a password reset, phone, email or
       2FA change (DD-09):
       Call get_auth_events(event_types=["PASSWORD_RESET", "PHONE_CHANGED",
       "EMAIL_CHANGED", "TWO_FA_RESET"], days=1). Use only events from the last 24 hours.
       -> Event found: explain the cooldown and when it ends (IST). Resolve (withdrawal_sop).
     - New crypto address cooldown -> crypto_transfers_sop.
     - Large INR withdrawal (> Rs 5,00,000) in PENDING_APPROVAL up to 48 hours -> withdrawal_sop.
  3. Call get_risk_holds().
     Branch on hold_type:
     - WITHDRAWAL_HOLD:
       Call get_withdrawals(statuses=["ON_HOLD", "PENDING_APPROVAL"]).
       -> Withdrawal ON_HOLD: funds are reserved (wallet_balances.locked) and safe. Send the
          generic hold reply. Escalate to Risk & Compliance (P2).
       -> Customer may cancel a held withdrawal only if the app allows it; support does not
          cancel it for them while a hold is active.
     - DEPOSIT_HOLD:
       Call get_deposits(statuses=["ON_HOLD", "PENDING"]).
       -> Deposit ON_HOLD: money received but not yet credited pending review. Generic reply;
          escalate to Risk & Compliance (P2). If Risk later rejects it, it is refunded to
          source in 5-7 working days (DD-09) - payment_sop.
       -> failure_reason = THIRD_PARTY_ACCOUNT: not a risk hold; third-party deposits are
          refunded to source (payment_sop). Resolve there.
     - Both WITHDRAWAL_HOLD and DEPOSIT_HOLD: treat as RSK-01-level review; generic reply,
       P2 to Risk & Compliance.
     - No ACTIVE hold, but withdrawal ON_HOLD: hold_reason may be operational -> withdrawal_sop.
  4. If source = LAW_ENFORCEMENT on any hold: same generic reply, escalate to Legal (P2);
     never confirm an agency request.
  5. Hold older than 7 working days (sample value) with no update to the customer: escalate
     as SLA breach (P2, note breach).
RESOLUTION:
  Explain that the specific transaction or feature is paused for a routine review, the money
  is safe, and the next update timeline. Offer the in-app document path only if Risk has
  requested documents.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Thanks for reaching out about your <withdrawal/deposit> of Rs <amount> on <date>. I checked
  it: it is currently paused for a routine review. The amount is safely held in your account
  and has not been lost.
  We are not able to share details of the review. If any information is needed, you will get
  a request in the app under Help > Documents. We aim to complete reviews within 7 working
  days and will update you as soon as it is done.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Any ACTIVE WITHDRAWAL_HOLD or DEPOSIT_HOLD with a customer contact -> Risk & Compliance,
    P2, include: user_id, hold_id, withdrawal_id/deposit_id, amount, dates.
  - LAW_ENFORCEMENT source -> Legal, P2.
  - Money debited from bank but no deposit row at all -> payment_sop, P1.
DO NOT:
  - Do not tell the customer the hold "will be released after X".
  - Do not suggest the customer withdraw via crypto to get around an INR hold.

## RSK-03: Request for source-of-funds or other documents
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "You asked for source of funds, what should I submit?"
  - "Which documents do you need to unfreeze my account?"
  - "Can I just email you my bank statement?"
  - "Kya salary slip chalega source of funds ke liye?"
  - "I uploaded everything a week ago, no response"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Registered email/phone. Documents are NOT collected by email.
TOOLS: get_risk_holds, get_tickets
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Confirm a document request is valid - only Risk & Compliance raises them:
     Call get_risk_holds().
     Call get_tickets(category="risk").
     - No ACTIVE hold and no risk ticket: Acme has not requested documents. The request the
       customer received may be phishing. Warn them (support_guidelines_sop SUP-06); ask them
       not to send documents and to forward the message to support. Resolve.
     - ACTIVE hold and a Risk ticket exists: continue.
  3. Explain what is typically accepted (sample list; the in-app request lists the exact
     items and always wins):
       - Bank statements for the last 6 months (sample value) for the account used to deposit,
         as downloaded from net banking (PDF), not photos or screenshots
       - Proof of income: salary slips (last 3 months, sample value), ITR acknowledgement,
         or Form 16
       - For business income: GST registration or business bank statements
       - For proceeds of a sale (property, gold, FD): sale deed / maturity advice
       - A short written explanation of the source of the funds deposited
  4. Explain the channel: upload in the app under Help > Documents (sample policy), PDF/JPG,
     up to 10 MB per file (sample value). If the upload fails, see app_technical_sop APP-03.
  5. If the customer already emailed documents: do not open, forward or process them by
     email. Tell them to upload in the app instead. Flag the ticket so the email attachment
     is deleted per data handling policy.
  6. Uploaded already:
     - Less than 7 working days (sample value) since upload: ask them to wait; give the date.
     - More than 7 working days: escalate to Risk & Compliance (P2, SLA breach).
RESOLUTION:
  Tell the customer which kinds of documents are usually needed, that the exact list is in
  the in-app request, and that the only channel is Help > Documents.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Thanks for getting in touch about the document request. To keep your information safe,
  please upload documents only in the Acme Invest app under Help > Documents - please do not
  send them by email.
  The request in the app lists exactly what is needed. Usually this is:
  - bank statements (PDF from net banking) for the account you deposited from
  - proof of income, such as salary slips, ITR acknowledgement or Form 16
  - a short note explaining the source of the funds
  Once you upload, our team reviews them within 7 working days and will update you in the app.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Documents uploaded more than 7 working days ago with no outcome -> Risk & Compliance,
    P2, include: user_id, hold_id, upload date as stated by the customer.
  - Customer received a document request that Acme did not send -> Risk & Compliance, P3
    (possible phishing), include: the forwarded message headers.
DO NOT:
  - Do not accept, open or review documents sent by email.
  - Do not tell the customer which document will "clear" the review or hint at thresholds.
  - Do not ask for Aadhaar/PAN numbers in full by email.

## RSK-04: Trade restriction
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "My orders are getting rejected saying account restricted"
  - "I can only sell, buy button is disabled"
  - "Trading block kyun hai mere account pe?"
  - "Why can't I trade but I can withdraw?"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Registered email/phone, order ID or time of the failed order, screenshot
TOOLS: get_orders, get_instrument, get_risk_holds
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02). If FROZEN -> RSK-01.
  2. Rule out non-risk causes:
     Call get_orders(). Look at the latest 10 orders.
     - reject_reason = KYC_NOT_VERIFIED -> kyc_sop.
     - INSTRUMENT_HALTED / PRICE_OUT_OF_BAND / BELOW_MIN_ORDER_VALUE / INSUFFICIENT_BALANCE ->
       trade_sop. Resolve there.
     - Call get_instrument(symbol=<symbol>).
       -> HALTED / POST_ONLY / DELISTED: market-level, not a user restriction -> trade_sop.
     - reject_reason = ACCOUNT_RESTRICTED: continue.
  3. Call get_risk_holds(hold_type="TRADE_RESTRICTION").
     - Hold found: generic reply; escalate to Risk & Compliance (P2). Existing holdings are
       safe; open orders placed before the restriction may have been cancelled (check with
       Call get_orders(status="CANCELLED") around the hold's created_at)
       - tell the customer which ones.
     - No hold but ACCOUNT_RESTRICTED rejections: inconsistency -> Trading Ops (P2) and Risk
       & Compliance.
  4. LAW_ENFORCEMENT source: generic reply, escalate to Legal (P2).
RESOLUTION:
  Explain that trading is paused for a routine review; holdings are safe; update timeline.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Thanks for writing in. I checked your account: placing new orders is currently paused as
  part of a routine review. Your existing holdings remain safely in your account.
  <If orders were cancelled: "Your open order(s) from <date> were cancelled when the pause
  started, and the reserved amount is back in your available balance.">
  We are not able to share details of the review. We aim to complete it within 7 working days
  and will notify you in the app once it is done.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - ACTIVE TRADE_RESTRICTION with customer contact -> Risk & Compliance, P2, include: user_id,
    hold_id, rejected order_ids.
  - ACCOUNT_RESTRICTED rejections with no hold row -> Trading Ops, P2.
DO NOT:
  - Do not tell the customer how to trade via API or another route to avoid the restriction.
  - Do not reveal why trading was restricted.

## RSK-05: Customer is a scam victim
======================================================================
Covers: investment scams, "pig-butchering" (online friend/romance who pushes a fake trading
platform), fake tipster / "VIP signals" groups on Telegram/WhatsApp, fake Acme support
numbers, task/job scams, and "pay a fee to unlock your withdrawal" scams, where the customer
has sent INR or crypto to a scammer.
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "I sent USDT from Acme to an address given by a trading group, now they are asking for
    more tax to release my profit"
  - "Someone on WhatsApp said he is Acme support and I transferred money to him"
  - "Maine ek Telegram group ke kehne pe crypto bheja, ab woh block kar diya, help karo"
  - "I met a girl on a dating app who taught me crypto trading, I lost Rs 8 lakh"
  - "Please block the scammer's account and return my money"
  - "Scammer is sending money to my Acme account, please stop it"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Registered email/phone; date, amount and asset of each transfer; transaction hash or UTR;
    the destination address / UPI ID they paid; whether they have filed a cybercrime
    complaint (acknowledgement number, if yes); whether any funds are still in their Acme
    account; whether anyone has access to their Acme account now (screen-share app, OTP shared)
TOOLS: get_auth_events, get_user_devices, check_wallet_balance, get_withdrawals
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Check whether the account itself is compromised (scammer may have control):
     Call get_auth_events(days=30).
     Call get_user_devices().
     - Customer shared OTP / installed a screen-sharing app / unknown device or login:
       -> account_security_sop SEC-01 immediately (P1).
  3. Check whether funds are STILL on Acme and at risk:
     Call check_wallet_balance().
     Call get_withdrawals(statuses=["REQUESTED", "PENDING_APPROVAL", "PROCESSING"]).
     - Funds still on Acme, or a withdrawal to the scammer is not yet SUCCESS, and the customer
       asks to block: P1 escalation to Risk & Compliance (they can place a hold). Tell the
       customer not to make any further transfers and to change their password. Do not
       promise that the pending withdrawal will be stopped.
  4. Completed transfers to the scammer:
     Call get_withdrawals(status="SUCCESS", days=90).
     - Crypto withdrawals with status SUCCESS are final on the blockchain. Acme cannot reverse
       them or recover them from an external wallet.
     - INR paid directly to a scammer's bank/UPI (outside Acme): Acme is not in that payment
       chain; the customer must go to their bank and the cybercrime helpline.
  5. The customer asks Acme to block "the scammer's Acme account" or wants details of the
     receiving account: never confirm whether the address/UPI belongs to an Acme user and
     never share any details. Log the address/tx hash in the escalation for Risk & Compliance
     (RSK-06 handles reports about other users).
  6. Any message saying they filed a police/cybercrime complaint or asks Acme to "coordinate
     with police": send the recovery advice below AND treat as CMP-04 (Legal/Grievance). Law
     enforcement requests must come through official channels to Legal; the customer cannot
     relay them.
  7. "Pay tax/fee to release profit" messages: warn clearly that this is always a scam and
     they must not send more money.
RESOLUTION:
  What Acme CAN do: secure the customer's Acme account (SEC-01), escalate funds still on Acme
  for a possible hold (P1), record the scammer's address/details for Risk & Compliance, and
  respond to official law-enforcement requests through Legal.
  What Acme CANNOT do: reverse completed blockchain transfers, recover funds from external
  wallets or other platforms, share any other user's details with the customer, or guarantee
  recovery.
  Advise: report immediately to the National Cybercrime Helpline 1930 and at
  https://cybercrime.gov.in (the faster, the better chance banks can freeze funds), inform
  their bank, keep all screenshots, chats, tx hashes and UTRs, and stop all contact with the
  scammer.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  I'm really sorry this has happened to you. Please do not send any more money or crypto to
  anyone who promises returns, "unlock fees" or "tax" to release profits - these are always
  scams.
  Please act quickly:
  - Call the National Cybercrime Helpline on 1930 and file a complaint at
    https://cybercrime.gov.in. Keep your complaint number.
  - Inform your bank about any payments you made from your bank account.
  - Keep screenshots of chats, the wallet addresses or UPI IDs you paid, and transaction IDs.
  - Change your Acme Invest password, and never share OTPs or install screen-sharing apps.
  I have passed the details you shared to our specialist team. Transfers already completed on
  the blockchain cannot be reversed by us, but we will cooperate fully with any official
  request from the authorities. Acme Invest will never ask you to pay a fee to withdraw.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Funds still on Acme and customer asks to block, or a pending withdrawal to a scammer ->
    Risk & Compliance, P1, include: user_id, withdrawal_ids, destination addresses, amounts.
  - Account access shared / unknown login -> account_security_sop SEC-01, P1.
  - Completed losses reported -> Risk & Compliance, P2, include: all addresses/UTRs/tx hashes,
    dates, complaint acknowledgement number if given.
  - Customer mentions police/cybercrime complaint or asks Acme to contact police ->
    complaints_escalation_sop CMP-04 (Legal), plus the above.
DO NOT:
  - Do not say "your money will be recovered" or "we will get it back".
  - Do not confirm whether a scammer's address belongs to an Acme customer.
  - Do not blame the customer ("you should have known").
  - Do not recommend paid "recovery agents" or "crypto recovery services" - these are
    frequently scams themselves.

## RSK-06: Customer reports another user or a suspicious counterparty
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "This person on your platform cheated me, here is his Acme referral code"
  - "Someone is using Acme to run a Ponzi group, please take action"
  - "I think this wallet address belongs to a fraudster, check it"
  - "Is this Acme user genuine? He wants to buy my USDT via P2P"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - What happened, dates, any identifiers they have (address, tx hash, referral code, phone,
    screenshots). Whether they lost money (if yes -> also RSK-05).
TOOLS: none
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the reporting user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02) if they are a customer.
     Non-customers can also report; do not confirm or deny they have an account.
  2. Do NOT look up and discuss the reported person with the reporter. Support does not
     investigate other users; any lookup is for the escalation note only and is done by Risk.
  3. Classify:
     - Reporter lost money -> RSK-05 as well.
     - Report involves impersonation of Acme (fake app, fake support, fake offers) -> Legal
       (P3) and warn the reporter (support_guidelines_sop SUP-06).
     - "Is this user genuine / can I trade P2P with them": Acme does not verify or vouch for
       other users; off-platform trades are at the customer's own risk and not supported.
       Resolve with advice.
     - General suspicious activity report -> Risk & Compliance (P3).
  4. Acknowledge; explain that action taken (if any) is confidential.
RESOLUTION:
  Thank the reporter, log the report for Risk & Compliance, explain Acme cannot share the
  outcome or any details about other users.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Thank you for taking the time to report this. I have passed the details you shared to our
  specialist team for review. For privacy and security reasons, we cannot share information
  about other accounts or any action we take, but every report is reviewed.
  Please do not trade or send money outside the Acme Invest app to people you meet online -
  we cannot protect transactions made outside the app. If you have lost money, please report
  it on 1930 or at https://cybercrime.gov.in.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Every report -> Risk & Compliance, P3 (P2 if the reporter says funds are actively being
    collected now), include: reporter user_id (if any), identifiers supplied, screenshots.
  - Impersonation of Acme -> Legal, P3.
DO NOT:
  - Do not confirm that the reported person, code or address is linked to an Acme account.
  - Do not share what action was taken.

## RSK-07: Chargeback / bank dispute raised on a deposit
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "I raised a dispute with my bank for the deposit, now my account is locked"
  - "Bank reversed my UPI payment, why is my Acme balance negative?"
  - "I didn't authorise this deposit, I raised a chargeback"
  - "Bank ne paisa wapas kar diya, ab app kuch nahi karne de raha"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Registered email/phone, deposit date/amount/UTR, the reason they raised the dispute,
    dispute reference from their bank if available
TOOLS: find_deposits_by_reference, get_deposits, check_deposit, check_wallet_balance, get_risk_holds
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Find the deposit and gateway state:
     If the customer gave a UTR: Call find_deposits_by_reference(utr=<utr>).
     Otherwise: Call get_deposits(on_date=<deposit_date>).
     Use only a deposit whose user_id is the customer's. Then:
     Call check_deposit(deposit_id=<deposit_id>).
     - gateway.gateway_status = REVERSED: the bank pulled the money back after Acme credited it.
  3. Check reversal in the ledger and resulting balance:
     Use the ledger_entries returned by check_deposit (all entries for this deposit).
     Call check_wallet_balance().
     - REVERSAL entry and negative/insufficient INR: the deposit was already used (e.g. to
       buy crypto), so the account owes the reversed amount. Holds are typical until settled.
  4. Check holds: Call get_risk_holds().
  5. Branch on the customer's reason:
     - Customer says the deposit was NOT made by them / account compromised ->
       account_security_sop SEC-01 (P1).
     - Customer raised dispute because the deposit was "pending" / not credited, but Acme did
       credit it: explain it was credited (date/time IST) and ask them to withdraw the
       dispute with their bank; Risk & Compliance decides on the hold after that. Escalate P2.
     - Deposit genuinely failed/not credited and customer disputed: no REVERSAL impact on
       Acme; payment_sop handles refund status. Resolve there.

## RSK-08: Customer's bank account frozen by their bank ("linked to a crypto transaction")
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:

  - "Tell me who complained about the money I withdrew from Acme"
  - "Please send a letter to my bank that my withdrawal was genuine"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:

    reference number their bank gave them (if any)
TOOLS: get_withdrawals, get_risk_holds
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. For internal context only, confirm the withdrawal exists:
     Call get_withdrawals(asset="INR"). Look at the latest 10.
     - You may confirm to the verified customer the date, amount and UTR of THEIR OWN
       withdrawal (their own data).
  3. The freeze is on the customer's bank account, applied by their bank or on the direction
     of an authority. Acme does not control it and cannot lift it.
  4. Frequently the freeze relates to the source of funds of a counterparty in a trade, not

  5. Requests for an NOC, "genuine transaction letter", or account statement for the bank:

     - Any letter addressed to a bank, police or cyber cell -> escalate to Risk & Compliance

  6. Check if there is an Acme-side hold as well: Call get_risk_holds().
     Any ACTIVE hold -> also follow RSK-01/RSK-02.
     If source = LAW_ENFORCEMENT, do not confirm or deny anything; generic wording; Legal (P2).
  7. Customer mentions police complaint, FIR, court -> CMP-04 holding reply.
RESOLUTION:
  Information only: explain Acme cannot lift a freeze placed by their bank; advise them to ask
  their bank branch for the complaint/reference details and the issuing authority, and to
  contact that authority directly; they can download their own Acme statement. Route the case

  - Do not confirm that Acme received or responded to any police or bank request.

## RSK-09: Money mule - someone asked me to receive money into my Acme account
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "A person offered me 5% commission to receive deposits in my Acme account and buy USDT"
  - "Part-time job: they will send money to my bank, I have to buy crypto and send it to them"
  - "My friend wants to use my Acme account because his KYC is not done, is it ok?"
  - "Kisi ne bola apna account rent pe do, har mahine paisa milega"
  - "I already did it twice, now my account is on hold"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Registered email/phone; whether they have already received/forwarded funds (dates,
    amounts, addresses); who approached them and how (screenshots)
TOOLS: get_user_devices, get_auth_events, get_deposits, get_withdrawals
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Has anyone else accessed the account?
     Call get_user_devices().
     Call get_auth_events(days=30).
     - Customer shared login/OTP with the other person -> account_security_sop SEC-01 (P1).
  3. Has it already happened? (internal context; do not quote patterns back)
     Call get_deposits(days=30).
     Call get_withdrawals(days=30).
  4. Branch:
     - Only asking / not done yet: clear warning; do not proceed. Log for Risk & Compliance
       (P3). Resolve.
     - Already done (funds received/forwarded): escalate to Risk & Compliance (P2). Advise the
       customer to stop immediately, not to forward any further funds, and to report the
       person on 1930 / cybercrime.gov.in. Do not advise on refunding third parties
       themselves.
     - Account already on hold -> generic RSK-01/RSK-02 reply plus the warning.
     - Third-party deposits are refunded to source anyway (DD-09, payment_sop).
RESOLUTION:
  Explain clearly that letting others use your account or routing someone else's money
  through it is not allowed under Acme's terms, can be illegal, and can lead to the account
  and their bank account being frozen. Escalate.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Thank you for checking with us before going ahead - please do NOT do this. Receiving money
  from other people into your bank or Acme account and buying or sending crypto for them is
  not allowed on Acme Invest, and it is a common way criminals move stolen money. People who
  do this can have their bank and Acme accounts frozen and may face legal action.
  Please do not share your login, OTP or account with anyone, and stop all contact with the
  person who approached you. If you have already received or sent money for them, please
  report it on 1930 or at https://cybercrime.gov.in and tell us the details.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Already received/forwarded funds -> Risk & Compliance, P2, include: user_id,
    deposit/withdrawal IDs, amounts, addresses, screenshots of the approach.
  - Only an approach, nothing done -> Risk & Compliance, P3.
  - Login shared -> SEC-01, P1.
DO NOT:
  - Do not help the customer "return" money to anyone.
  - Do not explain what patterns Acme monitors.

## RSK-10: Hold released - when can I withdraw?
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "Got a message that the review is complete, when can I withdraw?"
  - "Account unfrozen but withdrawal still not working"
  - "Hold hat gaya kya? Withdrawal fail ho raha hai"
  - "My old withdrawal that was on hold, will it process automatically?"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Registered email/phone; screenshot of any error
TOOLS: get_risk_holds, get_user, get_withdrawals, check_wallet_balance
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Call get_risk_holds(active_only=false).
     - Any hold still ACTIVE: not fully released -> RSK-01 / RSK-02 / RSK-04 generic reply.
     - All RELEASED: continue.
  3. Call get_user(). Check status.
     - Still FROZEN though all holds RELEASED: inconsistency -> Risk & Compliance (P2).
  4. Held withdrawals after release:
     Call get_withdrawals(). Use the ones with created_at on or before the
     hold's released_at.
     - Moved to PROCESSING/SUCCESS: tell the customer the status (INR normally within 24
       hours, DD-09). Resolve.
     - CANCELLED at release (funds back in available): customer must place a new withdrawal.
       Resolve.
     - Still ON_HOLD more than 24 hours (sample value) after released_at -> escalate to
       Payments Ops (INR) / Crypto Ops (crypto), P2.
  5. New withdrawal failing: check the usual causes - security cooldown (auth_events within
     24 hours), unverified bank account, address cooldown -> withdrawal_sop /
     crypto_transfers_sop.
  6. Balance check: Call check_wallet_balance().
     - Amount locked with no open order/withdrawal -> escalate (P2).
RESOLUTION:
  Confirm the review is complete (do not say what it found), state what happened to any held
  withdrawal, and how/when they can withdraw now.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Good news - the review on your account is complete and withdrawals are available again.
  <One of: "Your withdrawal of Rs <amount> from <date> is now being processed and should reach
  your bank within 24 hours." / "Your earlier withdrawal request was cancelled and the amount
  is back in your available balance, so please place a new withdrawal from the app.">
  Thank you for your patience while we completed the review.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Released holds but users.status still FROZEN, or held withdrawal stuck 24+ hours after
    release -> Risk & Compliance / Payments Ops / Crypto Ops, P2, include: user_id, hold_ids,
    released_at, withdrawal_ids.
DO NOT:
  - Do not share the outcome or reason of the review.
  - Do not suggest the account is "cleared" of anything specific.
