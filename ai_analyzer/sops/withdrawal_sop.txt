## WDR-00: Scope and quick triage
======================================================================
SCOPE:
  INR withdrawals from the Acme Invest wallet to the customer's own verified bank account:
  pending, processing, failed, approval, holds, cooldowns, eligibility, locked balance,
  cancellation, limits and fees.
  NOT in scope:
    - Crypto withdrawals (withdrawals.asset <> 'INR') -> crypto_transfers_sop
    - Adding or verifying a bank account -> bank_account_sop
    - Fee disputes in detail -> fees_charges_sop FEE-03
    - Frozen accounts -> fraud_risk_sop RSK-01; account closure payouts -> account_closure_sop

TOOLS: get_withdrawals
QUICK TRIAGE (find the user first, see support_guidelines_sop SUP-02, then):
  Call get_withdrawals(asset="INR", days=30).

  withdrawals.status   Meaning                                         Go to
  REQUESTED            accepted, waiting to be sent to the bank          WDR-01
  PENDING_APPROVAL     large amount, manual review (up to 48 hours)      WDR-04
  PROCESSING           sent to the bank; payout_utr may exist            WDR-01 / WDR-02
  SUCCESS              bank confirmed the payout                         WDR-02 (not received)
  FAILED               payout did not complete; money returns to wallet  WDR-03
  CANCELLED            cancelled by the customer or the system           WDR-11
  ON_HOLD              held for review                                   WDR-05
  No row               customer could not place the withdrawal           WDR-06 / WDR-07 / WDR-08

LEDGER FLOW FOR INR WITHDRAWALS (sample flow, use to explain balances):
  - On request: amount + fee move from wallet_balances.available to locked. No ledger entry yet.
  - When sent to the bank (PROCESSING): ledger WITHDRAWAL debit (amount) and FEE debit (Rs 10),
    reference_type = 'WITHDRAWAL', reference_id = withdrawal_id; locked is released.
  - If the payout later fails: ledger REVERSAL credit of amount + fee, same reference.
  - If it fails or is cancelled before PROCESSING: the locked amount goes back to available,
    no ledger entries.

POLICY (DD-09): minimum Rs 100; fee Rs 10 per withdrawal; only to a VERIFIED bank account in
the user's own name; normal SLA within 24 hours; above Rs 5,00,000 -> PENDING_APPROVAL, up to
48 hours; 24-hour cooldown after password reset, phone change, email change or 2FA reset.

## WDR-01: Withdrawal pending beyond 24 hours
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "I withdrew Rs 25,000 yesterday, still not in my bank"
  - "Withdrawal pending since 2 days, please help"
  - "Paisa withdraw kiya tha, abhi tak bank me nahi aaya"
  - "Withdrawal stuck on processing"
  - "When will my withdrawal come? It says requested"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Amount and date of the withdrawal (withdrawal ID if they have it)
TOOLS: get_risk_holds, check_withdrawal
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify and verify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Find the withdrawal (quick triage query in WDR-00).
  3. Check age (now() - created_at) and status:
     - Less than 24 hours old, REQUESTED or PROCESSING: within SLA. Tell the customer the
       expected time (created_at + 24 hours, in IST). Wait.
     - PROCESSING with payout_utr present: the bank has the payout -> WDR-02.
     - PENDING_APPROVAL -> WDR-04. ON_HOLD -> WDR-05. FAILED -> WDR-03.
     - SUCCESS -> WDR-02.
     - REQUESTED or PROCESSING, no payout_utr, older than 24 hours: SLA breached, check
       step 4 then escalate.
  4. Rule out a hold that did not update the status:
     Call get_risk_holds().
     - Active WITHDRAWAL_HOLD or ACCOUNT_FREEZE: treat as WDR-05 (do not disclose).
     - None: escalate to Payments Ops, P2 (SLA breached, money delayed). Raise to P1 if
       older than 72 hours (sample value) or if the ledger shows a WITHDRAWAL debit but the
       status is not moving:
       Call check_withdrawal(withdrawal_id=<withdrawal_id>). Use its ledger_entries.
  5. Withdrawals placed on a weekend or bank holiday may be credited on the next banking day
     by some banks; this does not extend the 24-hour SLA for sending it from Acme.
RESOLUTION:
  Within SLA: give the expected time. Beyond SLA: apologise, escalate, give the P2 timeline.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Sorry that your withdrawal of Rs <amount> requested on <date> has not reached you yet.
  <It is being processed and should reach your bank account ending <last 4> by <date, time IST>. /
  It is taking longer than our usual 24 hours, so I have passed it to our payments team to
  push it through with priority. We will update you within 4 hours.>
  Your money is safe; if the transfer cannot be completed, the full amount will come back to
  your Acme wallet.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - REQUESTED/PROCESSING older than 24 hours without payout_utr -> Payments Ops, P2 (P1 after
    72 hours), include: user_id, withdrawal_id, amount, status, created_at, bank_account_id,
    ledger query result.
DO NOT:
  - Do not ask the customer to place a second withdrawal for the same money.
  - Do not cancel a PROCESSING withdrawal to "retry" it.

## WDR-02: Withdrawal PROCESSING/SUCCESS but not received in bank
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "App shows withdrawal successful but money not in my HDFC account"
  - "Withdrawal completed but bank says nothing received"
  - "Status success hai but account me credit nahi hua"
  - "Please give me the UTR for my withdrawal"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Amount and date; bank statement screenshot if the bank says not received after 2 working days
TOOLS: check_withdrawal
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify and verify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Call check_withdrawal(withdrawal_id=<withdrawal_id>). Check that withdrawal.user_id is the
     customer's and asset is INR. The bank_account it returns has bank_name,
     account_number_masked and ifsc.
  3. Branch:
     - payout_utr present (PROCESSING or SUCCESS): the bank has received the payout
       instruction. Give the customer the UTR, the amount (amount only; the Rs 10 fee is
       deducted from the wallet, not from the bank credit (sample rule)) and the account
       (bank name + last 4). Ask them to check their statement and, if not visible, to quote
       the UTR to their bank. Most banks credit within 2 hours; allow up to 2 working days
       (sample value) before escalating.
     - SUCCESS, payout_utr present, customer's bank confirms no credit after 2 working days:
       ask for the bank statement for the period -> escalate to Payments Ops, P2 for a trace.
     - PROCESSING with no payout_utr: not yet with the bank -> WDR-01.
     - Customer is checking a different account than bank_account_masked: explain which
       account it was sent to. Resolve.
  4. Check the ledger to confirm the debit and no reversal: use the ledger_entries returned
     by check_withdrawal.
     - REVERSAL present (reversed is true): the bank returned it -> WDR-03 (money back in wallet).
RESOLUTION:
  Share UTR and account; bank traces the credit. Escalate if the bank denies receipt after
  2 working days.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Thanks for checking with us. Your withdrawal of Rs <amount> on <date> was sent from our side
  on <date, time IST> to your <bank> account ending <last 4>.
  The bank reference number (UTR) is <payout_utr>. If you do not see the credit, please share
  this number with your bank; it lets them locate the payment. Banks usually credit within a
  few hours; if your bank still cannot find it after 2 working days, reply with your bank
  statement for those dates and we will trace it for you.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Bank denies receipt 2+ working days after SUCCESS -> Payments Ops, P2, include
    withdrawal_id, payout_utr, amount, masked account, IFSC, statement.
DO NOT:
  - Do not reissue the payout or promise a second transfer.
  - Do not share the full account number; use last 4 digits only.

## WDR-03: Withdrawal FAILED and money return to wallet
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "My withdrawal failed, where is my money?"
  - "Withdrawal failed but balance not back"
  - "Failed dikha raha hai, paisa wallet me wapas nahi aaya"
  - "Why did my withdrawal fail?"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Amount and date
TOOLS: get_withdrawals, check_withdrawal, check_wallet_balance
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify and verify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Call get_withdrawals(asset="INR", status="FAILED").
  3. Check the ledger:
     Call check_withdrawal(withdrawal_id=<withdrawal_id>). Use its ledger_entries.
     - No WITHDRAWAL debit and no REVERSAL: failed before being sent; the locked amount should
       be back in available. Confirm:
       Call check_wallet_balance(asset="INR").
       -> Available reflects it: tell the customer the money is back. Resolve.
       -> Still locked more than 1 hour (sample value) after FAILED: escalate Payments Ops, P2.
     - WITHDRAWAL debit and REVERSAL credit present: money returned to wallet on the REVERSAL
       date. Resolve.
     - WITHDRAWAL debit but no REVERSAL: the bank may still be returning the money. Bank
       returns take up to 3-5 working days (sample value) after the failure. Check the
       refunds returned by check_withdrawal (status, refund_utr, processed_at).
       -> Inside 5 working days of updated_at: wait, give date.
       -> Beyond 5 working days: escalate Payments Ops, P1 (money missing).
  4. Explain the likely reason in plain words (from the bank account record; there is no
     reason column on withdrawals): use the bank_account returned by check_withdrawal
     (status, bank_name, ifsc).
     - Account REMOVED / FAILED or customer says account closed -> WDR-09.
     - Otherwise say "the bank could not complete the transfer" and suggest retrying; if it
       fails twice in a row -> escalate Payments Ops, P3.
RESOLUTION:
  Confirm the money (including the fee) is back in the wallet or give the return date.
  The customer can place a new withdrawal.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Sorry that your withdrawal of Rs <amount> on <date> did not go through. The bank could not
  complete the transfer. <The full amount, including the Rs 10 fee, was returned to your Acme
  wallet on <date>, so it is available to use or withdraw again. / The bank is returning the
  money to us, and it will be back in your Acme wallet by <date>.>
  If you retry, please make sure the bank account ending <last 4> is active. If it fails
  again, let us know and we will look into it.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Debit without REVERSAL after 5 working days -> Payments Ops, P1, include withdrawal_id,
    ledger entries, payout_utr.
  - Balance stuck in locked after a pre-send failure -> Payments Ops, P2.
  - Repeated failures to a VERIFIED account -> Payments Ops, P3.
DO NOT:
  - Do not invent a failure reason. Do not blame the customer's bank by name.

## WDR-04: Withdrawal PENDING_APPROVAL (large amounts)
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "My withdrawal of Rs 8 lakh shows pending approval"
  - "Why does my big withdrawal need approval?"
  - "Approval pending since yesterday, please approve fast"
  - "Urgent hai, approval jaldi karo"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Amount and date. Nothing else unless the review team asks.
TOOLS: get_withdrawals, get_risk_holds
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify and verify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Call get_withdrawals(asset="INR", status="PENDING_APPROVAL").
     - amount > Rs 5,00,000 and less than 48 hours old: normal manual review (DD-09).
       Tell the customer the latest time (created_at + 48 hours). Wait.
     - More than 48 hours old: SLA breached -> escalate to Payments Ops, P2.
     - amount <= Rs 5,00,000 but PENDING_APPROVAL: unusual; check risk_holds (step 3).
  3. Call get_risk_holds().
     - Active hold: treat as WDR-05.
     - None and amount <= Rs 5,00,000: escalate Payments Ops, P3 (review the routing).
  4. Customer asks to speed it up: do not escalate only for urgency inside 48 hours, unless
     there is a medical or similar emergency stated; then note it and escalate P2.
  5. Tip for the future: splitting into smaller withdrawals is allowed but each one costs
     the Rs 10 fee; do not suggest it as a way to avoid review.
RESOLUTION:
  Explain the review, give the 48-hour window.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Thanks for your patience. Withdrawals above Rs 5,00,000 go through an additional check to
  keep your funds safe. Your withdrawal of Rs <amount> requested on <date> is in this check
  and will be completed by <date, time IST> at the latest. Once approved, it usually reaches
  your bank within a few hours. Your money remains safe in your account meanwhile.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Older than 48 hours -> Payments Ops, P2, include withdrawal_id, amount, created_at.
  - Stated emergency -> Payments Ops, P2, include the customer's stated reason.
DO NOT:
  - Do not advise splitting the amount to avoid review.
  - Do not promise approval; only the review time frame.

## WDR-05: Withdrawal ON_HOLD (risk hold)
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "Withdrawal on hold, why are you holding my money?"
  - "My funds are blocked, I cannot withdraw"
  - "Hold kyu lagaya hai? Mera paisa release karo"
  - "Withdrawal under review for 3 days"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - None beyond identification. Do not request documents unless Risk & Compliance asks.
TOOLS: get_withdrawals, get_risk_holds
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify and verify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02). If users.status = FROZEN
     -> fraud_risk_sop RSK-01 and stop here.
  2. Call get_withdrawals(status="ON_HOLD").
     Call get_risk_holds(active_only=false).
     (hold_reason, reason_code and source are for the internal note only. NEVER disclose.)
  3. Branch:
     - Active WITHDRAWAL_HOLD / ACCOUNT_FREEZE: follow fraud_risk_sop for holds. Send the
       generic review reply; route the ticket to Risk & Compliance, P2.
     - source = LAW_ENFORCEMENT: same generic reply only; do not hint at any external request.
     - No active hold but withdrawal ON_HOLD, or hold RELEASED more than 2 hours ago
       (sample value) and withdrawal still ON_HOLD: escalate Payments Ops, P2.
  4. If the customer mentions being told to pay a "release fee" or "tax" to unlock the
     withdrawal: that is a scam -> fraud_risk_sop RSK-05; tell them Acme never asks for this.
RESOLUTION:
  Generic review message; Risk & Compliance owns the outcome.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Thanks for reaching out about your withdrawal of Rs <amount>. It is currently going through
  a routine review that we carry out from time to time to keep accounts safe. Your funds
  remain in your account during this time. Our team is reviewing it and we will update you
  within 48 hours. If anything is needed from you, we will ask by email from this address.
  Please note Acme Invest will never ask you to pay any fee to release a withdrawal.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Always: Risk & Compliance, P2, include user_id, withdrawal_id, hold_id (internal).
  - Status inconsistent with risk_holds -> Payments Ops, P2.
DO NOT:
  - Do not reveal the hold reason, the source, thresholds, or that anyone external is involved.
  - Do not promise a release date or cancel the withdrawal to move funds.

## WDR-06: 24-hour withdrawal cooldown after security changes
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "I reset my password and now I can't withdraw"
  - "Changed my phone number, withdrawal blocked for 24 hours?"
  - "Withdrawal says try after some time"
  - "2FA reset kiya, ab withdraw nahi ho raha"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Whether they recently changed password, phone, email or 2FA
TOOLS: get_auth_events
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify and verify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Call get_auth_events(event_types=["PASSWORD_RESET", "PHONE_CHANGED",
     "EMAIL_CHANGED", "TWO_FA_RESET"], days=1). Use only events from the last 24 hours.
  3. Branch:
     - Event found: cooldown applies until <latest event created_at + 24 hours> (IST). Tell
       the customer the exact time. Wait; no override.
     - Event found and customer says they did NOT make that change: possible account
       takeover -> account_security_sop SEC-01, P1. Do not explain further in this SOP.
     - No event in the last 24 hours: cooldown is not the cause -> check WDR-07 / WDR-08 /
       WDR-05; if none match, escalate Tech On-call, P2 with the error text/screenshot.
RESOLUTION:
  Explain the security cooldown and when it ends.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Thanks for writing in. For your security, withdrawals are paused for 24 hours after a
  password, phone number, email or two-factor change on the account. Your account had such a
  change on <date, time IST>, so withdrawals will be available again from <date, time IST>.
  You can still trade and add money in the meantime.
  If you did not make this change yourself, please reply immediately so we can secure your account.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Customer denies making the change -> account_security_sop SEC-01, P1.
  - No cooldown event but withdrawals blocked with no other cause -> Tech On-call, P2.
DO NOT:
  - Do not lift or shorten the cooldown, even for urgent requests.
  - Do not share the IP address or device details of the change.

  - "No bank account option while withdrawing"

TOOLS: get_bank_accounts

======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "The withdraw button is greyed out"
  - "Can't withdraw, it says complete KYC"
  - "Withdraw option hi nahi aa raha"
  - "It says re-KYC required to withdraw"

  - Screenshot of the screen/error, app version if known
TOOLS: get_user, check_wallet_balance, get_user_devices
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify and verify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02). Status FROZEN / DORMANT /

     - VERIFIED: continue step 3.
     - NOT_STARTED / IN_PROGRESS / UNDER_REVIEW / REJECTED / RE_KYC_REQUIRED: withdrawals need

     - Cooldown (WDR-06 query) -> WDR-06.

     - No VERIFIED bank account -> WDR-07.
     - Available INR balance below Rs 110 (Rs 100 minimum + Rs 10 fee):
       Call check_wallet_balance(asset="INR").
       -> Explain the minimum. If funds are locked -> WDR-10.
  4. None of the above: possible app issue. Call get_user_devices().
     Ask them to update the app and retry; if it persists -> app_technical_sop / escalate
     Tech On-call, P2.
RESOLUTION:
  Name the one blocker in plain words and how to clear it.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:

  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - No blocker found and the app still blocks withdrawal after an update -> Tech On-call, P2,

DO NOT:
  - Do not tell the customer about a risk hold as the reason; use WDR-05 wording.

## WDR-09: Withdrawal to a wrong or closed bank account
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "I closed that bank account, but the withdrawal went there"
  - "Withdrew to my old account by mistake, please send to new one"
  - "Galat account select ho gaya withdrawal me"
  - "Account is inactive/frozen at bank, what happens to my withdrawal?"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Amount and date; which account they intended; whether the old account is closed or just
    not preferred
TOOLS: check_withdrawal
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify and verify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Call check_withdrawal(withdrawal_id=<withdrawal_id>). Check that withdrawal.user_id is the
     customer's. bank_account.status is the bank account's status.
     Note: withdrawals only go to the customer's own VERIFIED accounts, so a "wrong" account is
     always one of their own.
  3. Branch:
     - REQUESTED / PENDING_APPROVAL: the customer can cancel it (WDR-11) and place a new one
       to the right account.
     - PROCESSING / SUCCESS to an OPEN account of theirs: money is in their own account. It
       cannot be redirected. Give the UTR (WDR-02). Resolve.
     - PROCESSING / SUCCESS to a CLOSED or inactive account: the bank normally returns it
       within 3-5 working days (sample value); Acme then credits it back to the wallet
       (REVERSAL). Check the ledger_entries returned by check_withdrawal for a REVERSAL.
       -> REVERSAL present: money is back. Resolve.
       -> Not yet and within 5 working days of updated_at: wait, give date.
       -> Beyond 5 working days with no REVERSAL -> escalate Payments Ops, P1.
       -> Bank credited the closed account (SUCCESS, bank confirms credit): the customer must
          contact their bank to recover it; give UTR. Resolve (P3 follow-up if they need a
          confirmation letter).
     - FAILED -> WDR-03.
  4. Ask the customer to remove the closed account and set the correct primary account
     (bank_account_sop BNK-05, BNK-06) to prevent a repeat.
RESOLUTION:
  Cancel if still possible; otherwise the payout returns or stays in their own account.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Thanks for letting us know. Your withdrawal of Rs <amount> on <date> was sent to your
  <bank> account ending <last 4>. <Because that account is closed, the bank will return the
  amount to us, usually within 3-5 working days, and we will add it back to your Acme wallet.
  You can then withdraw to your active account.> To avoid this in future, please remove the
  old account and set your active account as primary under Profile > Bank Accounts.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - No return 5 working days after sending to a closed account -> Payments Ops, P1, include
    withdrawal_id, payout_utr, masked account, IFSC.
DO NOT:
  - Do not promise to "redirect" a payout that has been sent.
  - Do not add or change bank details on the customer's behalf over email.

## WDR-10: Balance shows locked
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "Part of my INR balance shows locked, why?"
  - "Available balance is less than total"
  - "Mera paisa locked dikha raha hai"
  - "Can't withdraw full amount, some is blocked"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - None beyond identification
TOOLS: check_wallet_balance, get_orders, get_withdrawals, get_risk_holds
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify and verify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Call check_wallet_balance(asset="INR").
  3. Find what is holding the locked amount:
     - Open buy orders:
       Call get_orders(side="BUY", statuses=["NEW", "OPEN", "PARTIALLY_FILLED"]).
     - Pending INR withdrawals:
       Call get_withdrawals(asset="INR",
                            statuses=["REQUESTED", "PENDING_APPROVAL", "ON_HOLD"]).
     - Sum of (open order value + withdrawal amount + fee) roughly equals locked: explain.
       Customer can cancel open orders (trade_sop) or wait for the withdrawal. Resolve.
     - Withdrawal ON_HOLD is part of the locked amount -> WDR-05 wording.
     - Locked is larger than anything open: stale lock -> Call get_risk_holds()
       (ACTIVE hold -> fraud_risk_sop wording). No hold -> escalate Payments Ops, P2.
RESOLUTION:
  Explain the open orders or withdrawals that reserve the funds.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,

## WDR-11: Cancel a withdrawal
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "Please cancel my withdrawal, I want to buy BTC instead"
  - "Withdrawal galti se kar diya, cancel karo"
  - "How do I cancel a pending withdrawal?"
  - "Why was my withdrawal cancelled?"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Which withdrawal (amount, date)
TOOLS: get_withdrawals, check_wallet_balance
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify and verify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Call get_withdrawals(asset="INR"). Look at the latest 5.
  3. Branch:
     - REQUESTED or PENDING_APPROVAL: the customer can cancel it in the app (Transactions >
       select withdrawal > Cancel) (sample value). Support does not cancel on their behalf by
       email. Resolve.
     - PROCESSING / SUCCESS: already sent to the bank; cannot be cancelled. Resolve.
     - ON_HOLD: cannot be cancelled while under review -> WDR-05 wording.
     - CANCELLED and the customer did not cancel it: check whether funds are back
       (Call check_wallet_balance(asset="INR"), available). If CANCELLED
       within 24h of a security change or by the system for an unverified/removed bank
       account, explain generically. If unclear ->
       escalate Payments Ops, P3. If the customer suspects someone else placed/cancelled it
       -> account_security_sop SEC-01, P1.
RESOLUTION:
  Guide self-cancel when allowed; otherwise explain why it cannot be cancelled.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Thanks for writing. <Your withdrawal of Rs <amount> has not been sent yet, so you can cancel
  it yourself: open Transactions in the app, select this withdrawal and tap Cancel. The amount
  returns to your available balance straight away. / Your withdrawal of Rs <amount> has
  already been sent to your bank, so it can no longer be cancelled. It should reach your
  account ending <last 4> by <date>.>
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - System-cancelled with no clear cause -> Payments Ops, P3, include withdrawal_id.
DO NOT:
  - Do not try to recall a PROCESSING or SUCCESS payout.

## WDR-12: Withdrawal limits and fees (how-to)
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "What is the minimum and maximum I can withdraw?"
  - "Why was Rs 10 deducted on withdrawal?"
  - "How long does withdrawal take?"
  - "Withdrawal charges kitne hai?"
  - "Can I withdraw to UPI ID?"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - None (general question)
TOOLS: check_withdrawal
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. If about a specific withdrawal's fee: identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02) and
     Call check_withdrawal(withdrawal_id=<withdrawal_id>).
     - withdrawal.fee = 10: correct per policy. Resolve.
     - withdrawal.fee <> 10: -> fees_charges_sop FEE-03.
  2. Otherwise answer from policy. Resolve.
POLICY (DD-09 unless marked):
  - Minimum Rs 100 per withdrawal; fee Rs 10 per withdrawal (charged from the wallet on top of
    the amount; the bank receives the full amount) (sample rule for how the fee is applied).
  - Maximum per day: Rs 25,00,000 (sample value). Above Rs 5,00,000 per withdrawal goes to
    manual approval (up to 48 hours).
  - Normal time: within 24 hours.
  - Only to a VERIFIED bank account in the customer's own name; not to UPI IDs, cards or
    third-party accounts (sample value for UPI ID).
  - 24-hour pause after password, phone, email or 2FA changes.
  - Crypto withdrawals have network fees instead -> crypto_transfers_sop / fees_charges_sop FEE-04.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Thanks for your question. Here is how INR withdrawals work on Acme Invest:
  - Minimum Rs 100 per withdrawal, with a flat fee of Rs 10 per withdrawal.
  - Withdrawals usually reach your bank within 24 hours. Amounts above Rs 5,00,000 go
    through an extra check that can take up to 48 hours.
  - Money can be sent only to your own verified bank account.
  - For your security, withdrawals pause for 24 hours after you change your password, phone,
    email or two-factor settings.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Not needed for a how-to question.
DO NOT:
  - Do not offer to waive the fee (see fees_charges_sop FEE-08).
