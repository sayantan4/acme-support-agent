## RWD-00: Scope and quick triage
======================================================================
Covers every question about rewards: referral rewards (for the referrer and the new user),
signup bonus, cashback, coupon codes, streak rewards, reward expiry, revoked or held rewards,
and how to use reward crypto. Rewards issues are P3 by default (DD-09) unless the customer
also reports missing money or a risk hold.

Key policy (data_dictionary DD-09):
  - Referral: referrer gets Rs 100 in BTC after the referee completes KYC AND makes a first
    trade of at least Rs 500 within 30 days of signup. The code must be applied at signup.
  - Rewards are credited within 72 hours of qualification and expire 90 days after credit
    if not used (where the campaign says so).
  - Acme does not credit rewards manually on customer request (support_guidelines_sop SUP-04).

Reward lifecycle (rewards_ledger.status):
  PENDING   qualifying action recorded, still being verified
  ELIGIBLE  qualified; credit is due within 72 hours
  CREDITED  in the customer's wallet (a ledger_entries row with entry_type = 'REWARD')
  EXPIRED   not used before expires_at
  REVOKED   taken back after credit or cancelled before credit (reason is internal)
  ON_HOLD   frozen by a REWARD_HOLD or account-level risk hold (see fraud_risk_sop RSK-02)

Referral lifecycle (referrals.status):
  SIGNED_UP -> KYC_DONE -> QUALIFIED -> REWARDED, or REJECTED (with rejection_reason)

TOOLS: get_campaigns
Quick triage:
  1. Identify the user: support_guidelines_sop SUP-02. If users.status is FROZEN, stop and
     follow fraud_risk_sop RSK-01.
  2. Decide which reward the customer means:
       "my friend joined but I got nothing"         -> RWD-01
       "I joined with a code, where is my bonus"    -> RWD-02
       "forgot to enter referral code"              -> RWD-03
       "cashback not received"                       -> RWD-04
       "coupon code invalid"                         -> RWD-05
       "reward disappeared / expired"                -> RWD-06
       "reward cancelled / on hold"                  -> RWD-07
       "how do I use / withdraw reward"              -> RWD-08
       "offer ended / what are the terms"            -> RWD-09
       "where is my referral code / how to refer"    -> RWD-10
       "got wrong amount / wrong coin"               -> RWD-11
  3. Always read the campaign rules before answering:
     Call get_campaigns(campaign_id=<campaign_id>).

DO NOT:
  - Do not promise or manually credit a reward. Only the Rewards Team can adjust rewards.
  - Do not tell a referrer anything about the referee beyond what the app already shows
    (no KYC status details, no trade amounts, no device or bank details) - SUP-03.
  - Do not disclose FRAUD_SUSPECTED or any risk reason.

## RWD-01: Referral reward not received by the referrer
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "My friend signed up with my referral code but I didn't get Rs 100 BTC"
  - "Referred 3 people last month, reward nahi aaya abhi tak"
  - "Referral shows pending since 2 weeks, when will I get it?"
  - "My brother joined using my link, why is my referral rejected?"
  - "Your referral program is a scam, I referred 5 friends and got nothing"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Registered email/phone (if not the sender address)
  - Approximate date the friend signed up, and the friend's first name (optional; never ask
    for the friend's phone, PAN or other personal details)
TOOLS: get_referrals, get_user, get_rewards, get_ledger_entries, get_campaigns
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the referrer (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02). Note the user_id and status.
  2. List the referrer's referrals:
     Call get_referrals(). Use the as_referrer list.
     - as_referrer is empty: no signup used this customer's code. Check the code matches:
       Call get_user(). Look at referral_code.
       -> Tell the customer no signup with their code was recorded; the friend may have
          signed up without the code (see RWD-03 - it cannot be added later). Resolve.
  3. For the referral in question, branch on its status:
     - SIGNED_UP: the friend has not completed KYC yet.
       -> If created_at is within 30 days: tell the customer the reward unlocks once the
          friend completes KYC and a first trade of Rs 500 or more within 30 days of signup.
          Resolve (no escalation).
       -> If created_at is older than 30 days: the qualifying window has passed; expect the
          referral to move to REJECTED (QUALIFYING_TRADE_NOT_MET). Explain the rule. Resolve.
     - KYC_DONE: KYC complete, first qualifying trade not yet made.
       -> Within 30 days of signup: tell the customer the friend still needs a first trade of
          Rs 500 or more. Do not reveal whether the friend traded or how much. Resolve.
       -> Older than 30 days: explain the 30-day window has passed. Resolve.
     - QUALIFIED: check the reward row (step 4).
     - REWARDED: check the credit (step 5).
     - REJECTED: go to step 6 and branch on rejection_reason.
  4. QUALIFIED - find the reward:
     Call get_rewards(reward_type="REFERRAL").
     - qualified_at less than 72 hours ago and reward PENDING or ELIGIBLE: normal. Ask the
       customer to wait until qualified_at + 72 hours (convert to IST). Resolve.
     - qualified_at more than 72 hours ago and still PENDING/ELIGIBLE, or no reward returned:
       SLA breached -> escalate to Rewards Team (P3; P2 if the customer has followed up 3+ times).
     - ON_HOLD or REVOKED: go to RWD-07.
  5. REWARDED - confirm the wallet credit:
     Call get_ledger_entries(entry_type="REWARD",
                             reference_type="REWARD", reference_id=<reward_id>).
     - Entry exists: reward was credited. Tell the customer the date (IST), asset and amount,
       and where to see it (Portfolio > BTC). If it was used or has expired, see RWD-06/RWD-08.
     - Reward status CREDITED but no ledger entry: data mismatch -> escalate to Rewards Team
       (P2), include reward_id and referral_id.
  6. REJECTED - branch on the referral's rejection_reason:
     - SAME_DEVICE: the referee's account was created or used on the same device as the
       referrer or another referred account. Programme terms exclude this.
       -> Explain generically: "referrals from the same device are not eligible". Do not say
          which account or device. Resolve. If the customer insists it is a genuine different
          person (e.g. family member who borrowed the phone once), log the reply; the decision
          stands. Escalate only if they provide new facts -> Rewards Team (P4).
     - SAME_BANK_ACCOUNT: referee added a bank account already linked to the referrer or to
       another account. -> Explain generically: "referrals where accounts share a bank account
       are not eligible". Never reveal whose bank account. Resolve.
     - SELF_REFERRAL: the referee is the referrer's own second account.
       -> Explain that referring yourself is not allowed. Also check duplicate accounts:
          onboarding_sop ONB-07. Resolve.
     - CODE_APPLIED_LATE: the code was not applied at signup. -> Explain the policy (RWD-03).
       Resolve.
     - QUALIFYING_TRADE_NOT_MET: the referee did not complete KYC plus a first trade of at
       least Rs 500 within 30 days of signup. -> Explain the rule without revealing the
       referee's actual activity. Resolve.
     - CAMPAIGN_ENDED: the referral campaign ended before the referral qualified.
       Call get_campaigns(campaign_id=<campaign_id>).
       -> Explain the offer ended on <end_at in IST>. See RWD-09. Resolve.
     - FRAUD_SUSPECTED: NEVER disclose. Use the generic RWD-07 wording ("did not meet the
       programme terms after a routine review"). Do not escalate on the customer's request
       alone; escalate to Rewards Team (P4) only if the customer disputes it with details.
  7. If several referrals are affected, summarise per referral using first names only if the
     customer already provided them; otherwise use signup dates ("the referral from 12 Aug").
RESOLUTION:
  Explain the current stage and what still has to happen, or the plain-language reason for
  rejection. Credits happen automatically; support does not add rewards manually.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE (pending stage):
  Hi <first name>,
  Thanks for referring your friend to Acme Invest. I checked your referral from <date>.
  Your reward of Rs 100 in BTC unlocks once your friend completes their KYC and makes a first
  trade of Rs 500 or more within 30 days of signing up. It is then added to your account
  automatically within 72 hours. You will get a notification when it is credited.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE (rejected, not fraud):
  Hi <first name>,
  Thanks for writing in about your referral from <date>. I checked it, and unfortunately it
  did not qualify for a reward because <plain reason: e.g. "both accounts were used on the
  same device", "the referral code was not added at signup", "the first trade of Rs 500 was
  not completed within 30 days of signup">. These conditions are part of the referral terms,
  so we are not able to credit a reward for this referral.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - QUALIFIED more than 72 hours ago and no credit -> Rewards Team, P3, include: user_id,
    referral_id, qualified_at, reward_id (if any), rewards_ledger.status.
  - rewards_ledger shows CREDITED but no ledger_entries row -> Rewards Team, P2.
  - Customer disputes a rejection with new facts -> Rewards Team, P4.
DO NOT:
  - Do not share the referee's KYC status, trade amounts, device, bank or name.
  - Do not mention "fraud", "flagged" or "suspicious" for FRAUD_SUSPECTED.
  - Do not promise a reward "as a goodwill gesture".

## RWD-02: Referee (new user) did not get the signup bonus
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "I signed up with my friend's code, where is my joining bonus?"
  - "Welcome reward not credited even after KYC"
  - "Mujhe signup bonus nahi mila, code toh daala tha"
  - "The ad said free Rs 50 bitcoin on signup, I didn't get it"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Registered email/phone; the code they used (optional); where they saw the offer
TOOLS: get_campaigns, get_referrals, get_rewards, get_kyc_applications, get_ledger_entries
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02). Check the user's kyc_status and created_at.
  2. Check whether a signup bonus campaign applies. Signup bonuses are campaign-specific
     (sample value: Rs 50 in BTC on completion of KYC, only while a SIGNUP_BONUS campaign is
     ACTIVE); the referral policy in DD-09 only rewards the referrer.
     Call get_campaigns(reward_type="SIGNUP_BONUS", active_at=<user created_at>).
     - No campaign active at the user's signup time: no signup bonus applied. Explain that
       the offer was not running when they joined (or the ad was not an Acme offer). Resolve.
  3. Check whether a code was applied at signup:
     Call get_referrals(). Use the as_referee list.
     - as_referee is empty and the campaign requires a code: code was not applied -> RWD-03.
       Resolve.
  4. Check the bonus reward:
     Call get_rewards(reward_type="SIGNUP_BONUS").
     - No reward and kyc_status != 'VERIFIED': bonus unlocks after KYC. Point to kyc_sop if KYC
       is stuck. Resolve.
     - No reward and kyc_status = 'VERIFIED' and an eligible campaign exists: check how long
       since KYC was verified:
       Call get_kyc_applications(). Take reviewed_at of the VERIFIED application.
       -> Less than 72 hours: ask the customer to wait until reviewed_at + 72 hours. Resolve.
       -> More than 72 hours: escalate to Rewards Team (P3).
     - PENDING / ELIGIBLE: within 72 hours of qualifying -> ask to wait with the IST time.
       Older -> escalate (P3).
     - CREDITED: confirm the ledger credit:
       Call get_ledger_entries(entry_type="REWARD",
                             reference_type="REWARD", reference_id=<reward_id>).
       Tell the customer the date and asset. If used/expired -> RWD-06 / RWD-08.
     - ON_HOLD / REVOKED: -> RWD-07.
  5. If the referral is REJECTED with SAME_DEVICE / SAME_BANK_ACCOUNT / SELF_REFERRAL,
     the referee's bonus is usually also not paid (per campaign rules_summary). Explain
     generically as in RWD-01 step 6. Resolve.
RESOLUTION:
  Confirm whether a bonus offer applied at signup, what condition is pending (usually KYC),
  or the credit date. Offers seen outside the Acme app/website may be fake (see RWD-09).
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Welcome to Acme Invest, and thanks for reaching out about your signup reward. I checked
  your account: <one of: "the reward is added once your KYC is verified, which is still in
  progress" / "your reward will be added by <date, time IST>" / "the signup offer was not
  running on <signup date>, so no bonus applied to your account">.
  You can see all your rewards under Profile > Rewards in the app.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Eligible campaign, KYC verified more than 72 hours ago, no reward row -> Rewards Team,
    P3, include: user_id, campaign_id, kyc reviewed_at, referral_id if any.
DO NOT:
  - Do not honour offers from third-party ads, Telegram groups or influencers not listed in
    campaigns; say they are not Acme offers.
  - Do not tell the referee anything about the referrer's account.

## RWD-03: Referral code not applied at signup / wants to add it later
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "I forgot to enter my friend's referral code, can you add it now?"
  - "Code field was not visible when I signed up, please link my account to my friend"
  - "Bhai ne code diya tha but maine signup ke time nahi daala, ab add kar do"
  - "My referral got rejected saying code applied late"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Registered email/phone. Nothing else is needed; the answer is policy.
TOOLS: get_user, get_referrals
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Confirm whether a code was recorded:
     Call get_user(). Look at referred_by.
     Call get_referrals(). Use the as_referee list.
     - referred_by is set and referral exists (not REJECTED for CODE_APPLIED_LATE): the code
       WAS applied. Tell the customer it is linked and follow RWD-01/RWD-02 for status. Resolve.
     - No referral in as_referee, or REJECTED with CODE_APPLIED_LATE: code was not applied at signup.
       -> Policy (DD-09): the referral code must be applied at signup. It cannot be added,
          changed or back-dated afterwards by support or any team. Resolve with the template.
  3. If the customer says the code field was missing or the app errored at signup, ask for
     app version, platform and a screenshot, and log for Tech On-call (P4) as product
     feedback (app_technical_sop APP-09). The policy answer does not change.
  4. If the customer asks to close and reopen the account to use the code: explain that a
     new account for the same person is not allowed (onboarding_sop ONB-07) and would be
     rejected as SELF_REFERRAL / duplicate. Resolve.
RESOLUTION:
  Explain politely that codes can only be applied during signup. No exceptions.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Thanks for writing to us. I checked your account, and a referral code was not added when
  it was created. Referral codes can only be applied at the time of signup, and we are not
  able to add or change a code afterwards. I'm sorry for the disappointment.
  You can still earn rewards by referring your own friends - your code is under
  Profile > Refer and Earn in the app.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Never for the code itself. Signup-screen bug reports -> Tech On-call, P4 (feedback log).
DO NOT:
  - Do not promise to "check with the team" whether an exception is possible.
  - Do not suggest creating a new account.

## RWD-04: Cashback not credited
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "I made a trade under the cashback offer but no cashback received"
  - "Cashback showing pending for 5 days"
  - "Offer said 10% cashback on first SIP, where is it?"
  - "Cashback kab aayega? Trade toh kar diya"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Registered email/phone, offer name (or screenshot), date of the qualifying trade/deposit,
    order ID if available
TOOLS: get_campaigns, get_orders, get_rewards, get_ledger_entries
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Find the campaign and its rules:
     Call get_campaigns(reward_type="CASHBACK", name_contains=<offer name>).
     If the customer gave no offer name: Call get_campaigns(reward_type="CASHBACK", status="ACTIVE").
     Read rules_summary for: qualifying action, minimum amount, per-user cap, and cashback
     verification period (sample value: up to 7 days after the qualifying trade).
  3. Check the qualifying action happened inside the campaign window:
     Call get_orders(from_time=<campaign start_at>, to_time=<campaign end_at>).
     - No qualifying FILLED order in the window, or below the minimum in rules_summary:
       not eligible. Explain the rule that was not met. Resolve.
     - Order CANCELLED / REJECTED: does not count. Explain. Resolve.
  4. Check the cashback reward:
     Call get_rewards(campaign_id=<campaign_id>, reward_type="CASHBACK").
     - No reward, qualifying order less than 24 hours old (sample value): rewards are created by
       a daily batch. Ask the customer to check again after 24 hours. Resolve.
     - No reward, qualifying order older than 24 hours and rules met: escalate (P3).
     - PENDING: being verified. If within the campaign verification period (sample value:
       7 days from the qualifying trade), tell the customer the expected date. If older,
       escalate to Rewards Team (P3).
     - ELIGIBLE: credit due within 72 hours of becoming eligible (DD-09). Give the date. If
       more than 72 hours have passed, escalate (P3).
     - CREDITED: verify:
       Call get_ledger_entries(entry_type="REWARD",
                             reference_type="REWARD", reference_id=<reward_id>).
       -> Present: tell the customer the date and asset. Wrong amount -> RWD-11.
     - EXPIRED -> RWD-06. REVOKED / ON_HOLD -> RWD-07.
  5. Check whether the qualifying order was later reversed:
     Call get_ledger_entries(entry_type="REVERSAL").
     If there is a REVERSAL entry on that order, the cashback is typically revoked per
     rules_summary -> RWD-07 generic wording.
RESOLUTION:
  State the stage (verifying / confirmed / credited) with a concrete date, or which offer
  condition was not met.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Thanks for checking on your cashback. I looked at your trade on <date> under the
  <offer name> offer. <one of: "It qualifies, and the cashback is being verified. It will be
  added to your account by <date> IST." / "The cashback was added on <date> as <amount>
  <asset>; you can see it under Profile > Rewards." / "Unfortunately it did not qualify
  because <plain condition, e.g. the order was below the Rs 500 minimum for this offer>.">
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - PENDING beyond the verification period, ELIGIBLE beyond 72 hours, or no row for a clearly
    qualifying order after 24 hours -> Rewards Team, P3, include: user_id, campaign_id,
    order_id, rewards_ledger row (or "none"), dates.
DO NOT:
  - Do not calculate and promise a cashback amount not shown in rules_summary.
  - Do not credit cashback for trades outside the campaign window.

## RWD-05: Coupon code not working
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "Coupon code ACME500 says invalid"
  - "The promo code from your email is not applying"
  - "Code expired message aa raha hai but offer page says valid till month end"
  - "My friend's coupon code worked for him but not for me"
  - "Applied coupon, but no benefit showing"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Exact coupon code, where they got it (Acme email/app/partner/social post), screenshot of
    the error, date/time tried
TOOLS: get_coupon, get_campaigns, get_rewards
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Look up the code (codes are case-insensitive; normalise to upper case):
     Call get_coupon(coupon_code=<code>).
     - Tool says the coupon does not exist: code does not exist. Ask the customer to recheck
       spelling (O vs 0, I vs 1). If they got it from a non-Acme source, tell them it is not a
       valid Acme code. Resolve.
  3. Branch on the coupon's status and fields:
     - EXPIRED, or valid_until < now(): code has expired. Give the expiry date in IST. Resolve.
     - EXHAUSTED, or redemptions >= max_redemptions: all uses are taken. Resolve.
     - ACTIVE and user_id is NOT NULL and user_id != :user_id: this is a user-specific code
       issued to another account. Do not say whose. Explain it can only be used by the
       account it was issued to. Resolve.
     - ACTIVE and user_id = :user_id or NULL: check campaign and user's prior use (step 4).
  4. Call get_campaigns(campaign_id=<campaign_id>).
     - Campaign PAUSED or ENDED: offer is not running. -> RWD-09. Resolve.
     - Rules require something the user lacks (e.g. KYC VERIFIED, first deposit, new users
       only): explain the condition. Resolve.
     Check whether this user already redeemed it:
     Call get_rewards(campaign_id=<campaign_id>, reward_type="COUPON").
     - Reward exists: already used (one use per user unless rules say otherwise). If status is
       PENDING/ELIGIBLE, the benefit is on its way (72 hours per DD-09). Resolve.
  5. Everything valid but the app still rejects it: ask for app version and screenshot and
     escalate to Tech On-call (P3) with the code and timestamp; also see app_technical_sop APP-03.
RESOLUTION:
  Explain in plain words why the code did not apply (expired, fully used, belongs to another
  account, conditions not met, already used) or escalate a genuine app error.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Thanks for letting us know about the code <CODE>. I checked it: <one of: "this offer ended
  on <date>" / "this code has reached its maximum number of uses" / "this is a personal code
  that can only be used by the account it was sent to" / "this code is for customers making
  their first deposit, and your account already has one" / "you have already used this code
  on <date>">. I'm sorry it didn't work this time. Current offers are always listed under
  Profile > Rewards in the app.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Code ACTIVE, conditions met, not used, but app shows an error -> Tech On-call, P3,
    include: user_id, coupon_code, campaign_id, app version, screenshot, time of attempt.
DO NOT:
  - Do not reveal which account a user-specific code belongs to.
  - Do not issue a replacement code; support has no ability to create codes.

## RWD-06: Reward expired
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "My reward BTC has disappeared from my wallet"
  - "Reward expired, I didn't even know it had an expiry"
  - "Please re-credit my expired cashback"
  - "Mera Rs 100 ka bitcoin reward kahan gaya?"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Registered email/phone, which reward (referral/cashback/coupon) and approximate date
TOOLS: get_rewards, get_campaigns, get_ledger_entries, get_notifications
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Call get_rewards().
     - status EXPIRED and expires_at in the past: the reward expired. Per DD-09 rewards
       expire 90 days after credit if unused, where the campaign says so. Check rules_summary
       of the campaign to confirm expiry was part of its terms:
       Call get_campaigns(campaign_id=<campaign_id>). Go to step 3.
     - status CREDITED and expires_at in the future: not expired. The reward may be locked
       until used (see RWD-08) or sold. Check the ledger entries for the reward asset:
       Call get_ledger_entries(asset=<asset>). Resolve.
     - status REVOKED / ON_HOLD: -> RWD-07.
  3. Check whether expiry reminders were sent (sample value: reminder 7 days before expiry):
     Call get_notifications(). Look at notifications whose template contains
     "reward_expiry".
     - Reminders SENT: explain expiry per terms. Resolve; no re-credit.
     - No reminder or all FAILED: still no re-credit by support, but note it. If the campaign
       rules_summary does NOT mention expiry, the expiry was an error -> escalate to Rewards
       Team (P3) for review.
  4. Confirm the reversal entry exists (for accuracy of what you tell the customer):
     Call get_ledger_entries(reference_type="REWARD", reference_id=<reward_id>).
RESOLUTION:
  Explain the expiry date and rule. Expired rewards are not re-credited unless the Rewards
  Team finds the expiry was applied against the campaign's own terms.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Thanks for reaching out. I checked your <reward type> reward of <amount> <asset> that was
  added on <credit date>. Under the terms of that offer, rewards had to be used within 90 days,
  and this one expired on <expiry date> as it had not been used. Expired rewards cannot be
  re-credited, and I'm sorry for the disappointment.
  To avoid this in future, you can see each reward's expiry date under Profile > Rewards.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Campaign terms do not include an expiry but the reward was expired -> Rewards Team, P3,
    include: user_id, reward_id, campaign_id, expires_at, rules_summary text.
DO NOT:
  - Do not re-credit or promise a re-credit.
  - Do not blame the notification provider.

## RWD-07: Reward REVOKED or ON_HOLD
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "My referral reward was credited and then taken back"
  - "Reward shows on hold, why?"
  - "You cancelled my cashback without any reason"
  - "Reward revoke kyun kiya? I didn't do anything wrong"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Registered email/phone, which reward and approximate date
TOOLS: get_rewards, get_risk_holds, get_referrals, get_ledger_entries
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02). If the user's status = FROZEN, stop and
     follow fraud_risk_sop RSK-01 instead.
  2. Call get_rewards(status="REVOKED").
     Call get_rewards(status="ON_HOLD").
  3. ON_HOLD: check for an active risk hold:
     Call get_risk_holds(active_only=true).
     - REWARD_HOLD or any other ACTIVE hold: the reward is under review by Risk & Compliance.
       -> Use the generic hold template; follow fraud_risk_sop RSK-02 for handling. Support
          cannot release it. Do NOT read or share reason_code or source.

  4. REVOKED: look at the internal context only (do not share it):
     Call get_referrals().
     Call get_ledger_entries(reference_type=<TRADE or DEPOSIT>,
                             reference_id=<qualifying trade or deposit id>).
     - Linked referral REJECTED for SAME_DEVICE / SAME_BANK_ACCOUNT / SELF_REFERRAL /
       QUALIFYING_TRADE_NOT_MET / CAMPAIGN_ENDED: you may give the matching plain reason from
       RWD-01 step 6. Resolve.
     - Qualifying trade or deposit was reversed/refunded (a REVERSAL or REFUND ledger entry on
       the qualifying reference): you may say "the transaction that qualified for this reward

     - FRAUD_SUSPECTED, or eligibility_reason mentions risk/abuse, or a risk hold was returned:

       programme terms". Resolve. Do not escalate on request alone.
  5. Customer disputes the revocation with specific facts (e.g. "the other account on my

     Do not promise a change.
RESOLUTION:
  Give a generic, non-accusatory explanation; for holds, state the review is in progress and
  the customer will be informed. Never reveal fraud or risk reasons.

  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Confirm the reward is credited:

     - Withdrawal ON_HOLD (Call get_withdrawals(status="ON_HOLD")):
       withdrawal_sop / fraud_risk_sop RSK-02.
RESOLUTION:
  Explain the steps: Portfolio > <asset> > Sell, then Withdraw INR; or Withdraw crypto to a
  whitelisted address. Mention minimums and fees.

  - Do not waive minimums or fees.

  - Do not honour terms from non-Acme sources.

## RWD-11: Reward credited in the wrong asset or wrong amount
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "I got Rs 87 worth of BTC, the reward was supposed to be Rs 100"
  - "Cashback came in USDT instead of INR"
  - "Reward was 10% but I got much less"
  - "Paisa kam aaya reward mein"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Registered email/phone, which reward, what they expected and why (screenshot of offer)
TOOLS: get_rewards, get_campaigns, check_order, get_ledger_entries
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Call get_rewards(reward_id=<reward_id>) (the reward the customer means).
     No reward_id from the customer: Call get_rewards() and pick the one they
     describe (campaign, amount, date).
     Call get_campaigns(campaign_id=<campaign_id>). Read rules_summary.
  3. Asset check:
     - Asset matches rules_summary (e.g. referral in BTC per DD-09): correct. Explain. Resolve.
     - Asset differs from rules_summary: error -> escalate to Rewards Team (P3).
  4. Amount check (crypto rewards are fixed in INR value at credit time):
     - Reward is "Rs 100 in BTC": the BTC quantity was calculated at the price at credit
       time. The INR value shown today changes with the BTC price. If amount x price at
       credited_at is about Rs 100, the credit is correct. Explain. Resolve.
     - Percentage cashback: recompute from the qualifying trade and the cap:
       Call check_order(order_id=<order_id>). Use the fills (price, quantity, fee).
       Apply rules_summary (percentage, on which value, cap). If the credit matches, explain
       the calculation (mention the cap if applied). Resolve.
     - Credit differs from the correct figure by more than rounding: escalate (P3).
  5. Confirm the ledger matches the reward:
     Call get_ledger_entries(entry_type="REWARD",
                             reference_type="REWARD", reference_id=<reward_id>).
     - Mismatch between the ledger entry and the reward's amount/asset -> escalate (P2).
RESOLUTION:
  Explain the calculation, price movement, or cap; escalate a genuine miscalculation.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Thanks for checking your reward so carefully. Your reward of Rs 100 in BTC was added on
  <date> as <quantity> BTC, based on the BTC price at that moment. Because the BTC price
  moves, the value shown in your portfolio today can be higher or lower than Rs 100.
  <or: "The offer gives 10% cashback up to a maximum of Rs <cap>, so your cashback was
  capped at Rs <cap>.">
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Wrong asset, or amount wrong beyond rounding -> Rewards Team, P3, include: user_id,
    reward_id, campaign_id, expected vs actual, calculation.
  - ledger_entries does not match rewards_ledger -> Rewards Team, P2.
DO NOT:
  - Do not promise a top-up of the difference.
  - Do not quote live prices as a guarantee.
