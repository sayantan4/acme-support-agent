## PAY-00: Scope and quick triage
======================================================================
SCOPE:
  INR deposits into the Acme Invest wallet by UPI, IMPS, NEFT or RTGS: pending, failed,
  failed-but-debited, refunds, third-party deposits, holds, duplicates, wrong amounts and
  how-to questions about deposit methods and limits.
  NOT in scope:
    - Crypto deposits (coins sent from another wallet/exchange) -> crypto_transfers_sop
    - INR withdrawals -> withdrawal_sop
    - Adding, verifying or changing a bank account -> bank_account_sop
    - Fees on trades -> fees_charges_sop (Acme charges no deposit fee, see PAY-11)
    - Frozen accounts or risk holds on the whole account -> fraud_risk_sop RSK-01

TOOLS: get_deposits
QUICK TRIAGE (find the user first, see support_guidelines_sop SUP-02, then):
  Call get_deposits(days=30).

  deposit status           Meaning                                   Go to
  INITIATED                user started, payment not completed yet   PAY-01 / PAY-10
  PENDING                  waiting for bank/gateway confirmation     PAY-01 (UPI) / PAY-03 (bank transfer)
  SUCCESS                  credited to wallet                         PAY-09 if amount looks wrong
  FAILED                   not credited                               PAY-02, PAY-06, PAY-10, PAY-12
  ON_HOLD                  held for review                            PAY-07
  REFUND_INITIATED         money is being returned to source          PAY-05
  REFUNDED                 money returned to source (refund_utr)      PAY-05
  No deposit at all        deposit never reached us / not matched     PAY-03 (bank transfer) / PAY-02 (UPI)

  failure_reason           Go to
  THIRD_PARTY_ACCOUNT      PAY-04
  AMOUNT_ABOVE_LIMIT       PAY-06
  UNVERIFIED_BANK_ACCOUNT  PAY-12
  BANK_DECLINED, TIMEOUT   PAY-02 / PAY-10
  USER_CANCELLED           PAY-02 (if customer says money was debited) else explain and close

GENERAL RULES:
  - Timestamps are UTC in the tables; convert to IST before quoting a time.
  - The ledger (ledger_entries) is the source of truth for whether money reached the wallet.
  - Money debited from the customer's bank and not credited or refunded is P1 (SUP-07)
    only once the auto-reconciliation window (72 hours) has passed, or when records conflict.
    Inside the window, it is P2 and normally resolves by itself.

## PAY-01: UPI deposit is pending
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "I added money through UPI but it's not showing in my wallet"
  - "Deposit pending since morning, money deducted from bank"
  - "UPI se 5000 dala, paisa kat gaya but wallet me nahi aaya"
  - "Add funds stuck on processing for 2 hours"
  - "My deposit is showing pending, when will it come?"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Registered phone or email (if writing from a different address)
  - Amount, date and approximate time of payment
  - UPI transaction ID / UTR (12-digit number shown in the UPI app), if available
TOOLS: get_deposits, find_deposits_by_reference, check_deposit
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify and verify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02). Stop if the account is
     not ACTIVE and follow the linked SOP.
  2. Find the deposit:
     Call get_deposits(method="UPI", days=7).
     If the customer gave a UTR: Call find_deposits_by_reference(utr=<utr>).
     - No matching deposit: the payment never reached Acme or was not linked -> go to PAY-02
       step 5.
     - used_by_another_account is true: stop, do not share details, verify identity
       (SUP-01).
  3. Check the matching deposit: Call check_deposit(deposit_id=<deposit_id>). Then by deposit.status:
     - SUCCESS: confirm ledger_credited.
       -> true: tell the customer the money is in the wallet (they may need to refresh
          or re-open the app). Resolve.
       -> false: status and ledger disagree -> escalate to Payments Ops, P1.
     - PENDING or INITIATED and age_minutes < 30: normal. Ask the customer
       to wait 30 minutes from the payment time; UPI normally reflects within 5 minutes.
     - PENDING and age_minutes > 30: check the gateway (step 4).
     - FAILED: go to PAY-02 (failed but money debited) or PAY-10 (UPI app shows success).
     - ON_HOLD: go to PAY-07.
     - REFUND_INITIATED / REFUNDED: go to PAY-05.
  4. Use the gateway record returned by check_deposit (gateway.gateway_status, bank_reference,
     webhook_received_at, last_polled_at):
     - CAPTURED but deposit still PENDING: the gateway received the money but our system has
       not updated (missed confirmation) -> escalate to Payments Ops, P2 (P1 if older than
       72 hours).
     - PENDING at gateway and last_polled_at within the last 15 minutes: the bank has not
       confirmed yet. Auto-reconciliation runs every 15 minutes for 72 hours -> tell the
       customer it will update automatically; wait until 72 hours after payment.
     - PENDING at gateway and last_polled_at older than 1 hour (sample value): reconciliation
       is not running for this deposit -> escalate to Payments Ops, P2.
     - FAILED at gateway: treat as failed -> PAY-02.
     - REVERSED at gateway: the bank has reversed the debit -> PAY-05 (reversal usually
       reaches the customer's bank within 5-7 working days).
     - CREATED (customer never completed the payment): ask the customer to check whether the
       money actually left their bank account; if not debited, nothing is lost -> resolve.
  5. Deposit older than 72 hours and still PENDING: auto-reconciliation window is over ->
     escalate to Payments Ops, P1 (money debited, not credited, not refunded).
RESOLUTION:
  - Within 30 minutes: reassure, ask to wait.
  - 30 minutes to 72 hours, gateway PENDING: explain the bank has not confirmed, it updates
    automatically; either credited to wallet or returned to their bank.
  - Gateway CAPTURED or window passed: escalate and give the P1/P2 update SLA.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Sorry for the worry about your deposit of Rs <amount> made on <date> at <time IST>.
  I checked it: we are still waiting for your bank to confirm the payment. Our system checks
  with the bank automatically, and the deposit will either be added to your wallet or, if the
  bank does not confirm it, returned to your bank account. You do not need to pay again.
  Most such payments are updated within a few hours; in rare cases it can take up to 72 hours.
  We will keep an eye on it and write to you once it is updated.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Gateway CAPTURED but deposit not SUCCESS -> Payments Ops, P2 (P1 after 72h), include:
    user_id, deposit_id, gateway_txn_id, bank_reference, amount, created_at, webhook_received_at.
  - deposit SUCCESS but no ledger credit -> Payments Ops, P1, include deposit_id and ledger query result.
  - PENDING beyond 72 hours, or last_polled_at stale -> Payments Ops, P1 / P2 as above.
DO NOT:
  - Do not ask the customer to pay again while a deposit is pending (risk of double payment).
  - Do not promise the money will be credited; it may be refunded instead.
  - Do not mention the gateway, webhooks, polling or reconciliation by name.

## PAY-02: Deposit failed but money debited from bank
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "Payment failed in the app but Rs 10,000 was debited from my SBI account"
  - "Transaction failed, paisa kat gaya, refund kab aayega?"
  - "App says deposit failed, bank says successful. Where is my money?"
  - "Amount deducted but deposit failed, please credit or refund"
  - "Money gone from bank, nothing in Acme"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Amount, date/time, UTR / bank reference number
  - A screenshot of the bank statement or SMS showing the debit (only if no deposit row is found)
TOOLS: find_deposits_by_reference, get_deposits, check_deposit
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify and verify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Find the deposit (by UTR if given, else by amount and date):
     If the customer gave a UTR: Call find_deposits_by_reference(utr=<utr>).
     Otherwise: Call get_deposits(on_date=<payment date>) and match the amount.
  3. For the FAILED deposit, check the gateway:
     Call check_deposit(deposit_id=<deposit_id>). Use gateway.gateway_status:
     - gateway_status FAILED or CREATED: the payment did not complete at the gateway. If the
       bank still debited the customer, the bank reverses it on its own, normally within
       5-7 working days (sample value, same as refund window). -> Tell the customer; ask them
       to write back with a bank statement if it has not returned after 7 working days.
     - gateway_status REVERSED: the gateway has already sent the money back -> PAY-05 to
       give the timeline / reference.
     - gateway_status CAPTURED: we did receive the money but the deposit is FAILED. Check
       whether a refund was started (step 4).
  4. Use the refunds returned by check_deposit (status, refund_utr, created_at, processed_at):
     - Refund PROCESSED: give the refund_utr and processed date -> PAY-05 reply.
     - Refund INITIATED: within 5-7 working days of created_at -> tell the customer to wait
       until <created_at + 7 working days>.
     - Refund FAILED, or no refund and gateway CAPTURED: money is with us and neither
       credited nor refunded -> escalate to Payments Ops, P1.
     - Also check the ledger in case it was credited later by reconciliation: look at
       ledger_credited from check_deposit.
       true -> the money is in the wallet; tell the customer and resolve.
  5. No deposit at all but the customer insists money was debited:
     - Look up by UTR at the gateway: Call find_deposits_by_reference(utr=<utr>). Use
       gateway_records.
       - Found with a deposit_id: continue from step 3 with that deposit (check it belongs to
         this user).
       - Not found: ask the customer for the UTR and a bank statement screenshot if not
         already provided. If provided and still not found -> escalate to Payments Ops, P2,
         for a bank-side search (P1 if the payment is older than 72 hours).
RESOLUTION:
  - Gateway failed / bank-side debit: bank auto-reverses within 5-7 working days.
  - Captured and refund in progress: give the refund timeline.
  - Captured, no refund, no credit: escalate P1.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  I understand how worrying it is to see money leave your bank without reaching your wallet.
  I checked your deposit of Rs <amount> on <date>: the payment did not complete on our side,
  so it was not added to your wallet. <Your bank will return the amount to your account
  automatically, usually within 5-7 working days. / We have started a refund of Rs <amount>
  to your bank account on <date>; it usually reaches you within 5-7 working days.>
  If you do not see it by <date>, please reply with a screenshot of your bank statement
  showing the debit and we will trace it for you.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Gateway CAPTURED, deposit FAILED, no refund and no ledger credit -> Payments Ops, P1,
    include: user_id, deposit_id, gateway_txn_id, bank_reference, amount, refund query result.
  - Refund FAILED -> Payments Ops, P1, include refund_id.
  - UTR not found anywhere after the customer sent proof -> Payments Ops, P2 (P1 if > 72h),
    include UTR, amount, date, the customer's statement screenshot.
DO NOT:
  - Do not manually credit the wallet or promise a credit.
  - Do not tell the customer "it's your bank's fault".
  - Do not ask for full bank account number, card details or OTP.

## PAY-03: IMPS/NEFT/RTGS bank transfer deposit not reflecting (incl. wrong reference/UTR)
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "I did NEFT of Rs 2,00,000 yesterday, still not in my wallet"
  - "IMPS transfer done to the Acme account details, not showing"
  - "I forgot to put the reference number / typed wrong remark in the transfer"
  - "RTGS kiya tha 11 baje, abhi tak credit nahi hua"
  - "Here is my UTR, please check my bank transfer"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Transfer method (IMPS / NEFT / RTGS), amount, date/time, UTR
  - Which of their bank accounts it was sent from (bank name and last 4 digits)
  - Screenshot of the bank's transfer confirmation showing UTR, beneficiary and remark
TOOLS: find_deposits_by_reference, get_deposits, check_deposit, get_bank_accounts
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify and verify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Check the expected credit time (DD-09): IMPS within 30 minutes; NEFT within 2 banking
     hours; RTGS within 1 banking hour. NEFT/RTGS sent outside banking hours or on bank
     holidays are processed on the next banking day.
     - Still inside the window: ask the customer to wait until <expected time>. Resolve for now.
  3. Search by UTR, then by amount:
     Call find_deposits_by_reference(utr=<utr>).
     Call get_deposits(method="IMPS", days=7).
     Call get_deposits(method="NEFT", days=7).
     Call get_deposits(method="RTGS", days=7).
     For the deposit found: Call check_deposit(deposit_id=<deposit_id>).
     - Found, SUCCESS: confirm ledger_credited is true. Credited -> resolve.
     - Found, PENDING: inside 72 hours auto-reconciliation -> wait; beyond -> escalate P1.
     - Found, FAILED with THIRD_PARTY_ACCOUNT -> PAY-04. UNVERIFIED_BANK_ACCOUNT -> PAY-12.
       AMOUNT_ABOVE_LIMIT -> PAY-06.
     - Found, REFUND_INITIATED / REFUNDED -> PAY-05.
     - used_by_another_account is true: stop, verify identity (SUP-01).
  4. Not found by UTR or amount (typical when the reference/remark was wrong or missing, or
     the UTR was mistyped):
     - Check that the sending account is one of the customer's verified accounts:
       Call get_bank_accounts(). Ignore accounts with status REMOVED.
       - Sent from an account not listed or not VERIFIED: the credit will be treated as a
         third-party deposit and returned to source (PAY-04), after it is matched.
     - Ask the customer for the bank transfer confirmation screenshot (if not already sent).
     - With the screenshot: escalate to Payments Ops, P2, to search unmatched incoming bank
       credits and link or refund it. Unmatched credits that cannot be linked are returned
       to the source account within 5-7 working days (sample value) after identification.
  5. Customer gave a UTR with the wrong number of characters or it does not look like the
     screenshot: ask the customer to re-copy the UTR from the bank statement (IMPS UTR is
     usually 12 digits; NEFT/RTGS references are 16-22 characters, often starting with the
     bank code) -> ask customer.
RESOLUTION:
  - Inside the time window: wait.
  - Matched and credited: confirm.
  - Unmatched / wrong reference: collect proof, escalate for manual matching or refund to source.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Thanks for sharing the details of your <NEFT/IMPS/RTGS> transfer of Rs <amount> on <date>.
  I could not yet link this transfer to your Acme account, which can happen when the
  reference is missing or different. Please reply with a screenshot of the transfer
  confirmation from your bank showing the reference number (UTR), amount and date. Once we
  have it, our payments team will trace the transfer and either add it to your wallet or
  return it to your bank account. We will update you within 48 hours of receiving it.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Transfer not found after proof received -> Payments Ops, P2 (P1 if older than 72 hours),
    include: user_id, UTR, amount, method, date/time, sending bank and last 4 digits, screenshot.
  - Found PENDING beyond 72 hours -> Payments Ops, P1, include deposit_id.
DO NOT:
  - Do not ask the customer to send the money again.
  - Do not share Acme's receiving account details in a reply unless copied from the in-app
    "Add funds" screen instructions (details can differ by user).
  - Do not promise the transfer will be credited if it came from an unverified account.

## PAY-04: Deposit from a third-party / unregistered bank account refunded
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "I paid from my wife's account and the money was sent back"
  - "Why was my deposit refunded? It was my own money"
  - "Paid via my father's UPI, deposit reversed"
  - "Used my company account for NEFT, you returned it"
  - "Deposit failed saying account not linked"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Amount, date, UTR; which bank account/UPI ID they paid from
TOOLS: get_deposits, get_bank_accounts, check_deposit
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify and verify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Call get_deposits(failure_reason="THIRD_PARTY_ACCOUNT").
     - No such deposit: the deposit failed for another reason -> use PAY-00 triage table.
  3. List the customer's bank accounts:
     Call get_bank_accounts(). Ignore accounts with status REMOVED.
     - The paying account is not in the list: correct rejection (policy DD-09: deposits only
       from a VERIFIED account in the user's own name). -> Explain, check refund (step 4),
       and tell them how to add their own account (bank_account_sop BNK-01).
     - The paying account IS in the list and VERIFIED, and the customer paid from that exact
       account (same last 4 digits): possible wrong rejection -> escalate to Payments Ops, P2.
     - The paying account is in the list but PENDING_VERIFICATION or FAILED -> PAY-12 /
       bank_account_sop BNK-08.
  4. Check the refund:
     Call check_deposit(deposit_id=<deposit_id>). Use its refunds.
     - PROCESSED: give refund_utr; the money went back to the account it came from. Resolve.
     - INITIATED: inside 5-7 working days -> wait until <created_at + 7 working days>.
     - Missing or FAILED -> escalate to Payments Ops, P1.
  5. Joint account, NRE/NRO or business account used: not supported -> bank_account_sop BNK-07.
RESOLUTION:
  Explain the own-account rule, confirm the refund to the source account, and guide the
  customer to deposit from their own verified bank account.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  I checked your deposit of Rs <amount> on <date>. For your security and as required by
  regulations, we can only accept money from a bank account in your own name that is
  verified on your Acme account. This payment came from a different account, so it was
  returned to the account it was sent from.
  <The refund was processed on <date> with reference <refund UTR>. / The refund is in
  progress and should reach that account by <date>.>
  To add money, please use your own bank account linked in the app under Bank Accounts, or
  add and verify it first.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Paid from the customer's own VERIFIED account but still flagged -> Payments Ops, P2,
    include deposit_id, bank_account_id, masked account, UTR.
  - Refund missing or FAILED -> Payments Ops, P1, include deposit_id, refund query result.
DO NOT:
  - Do not accept the deposit as an exception, even for close family.
  - Do not tell the customer the refund went to a different account than the source.
  - Do not share the other account holder's name.

## PAY-05: Refund status / refund not received after 7 working days
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "You said refund initiated, 10 days ho gaye, still not received"
  - "Where is my refund for the failed deposit?"
  - "Please share refund reference number"
  - "Bank says no money received from Acme"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Amount and date of the original deposit (or deposit reference)
  - For "not received" after a PROCESSED refund: bank statement for the period
TOOLS: get_refunds, check_deposit
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify and verify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Call get_refunds(source_type="DEPOSIT").
  3. Branch on refund status:
     - INITIATED and fewer than 7 working days since created_at: normal -> tell the customer
       the expected date (created_at + 7 working days). Wait.
     - INITIATED and more than 7 working days: stuck -> escalate to Payments Ops, P2
       (P1 if more than 10 working days (sample value)).
     - PROCESSED: give the refund_utr and processed_at date. Ask the customer to check the
       statement of the SOURCE account (the account/UPI they paid from) and quote the
       refund UTR to their bank if not visible. If still not visible 3 working days
       (sample value) after processed_at with the bank confirming no credit -> escalate
       Payments Ops, P2, with the customer's bank statement.
     - FAILED: the refund did not go through (for example, source account closed) ->
       escalate to Payments Ops, P1.
     - No refund, deposit REFUND_INITIATED or REFUNDED: records conflict -> escalate P2.
     - No refund, deposit FAILED: Call check_deposit(deposit_id=<deposit_id>) and check
       gateway.gateway_status; REVERSED
       means the gateway returned it directly (bank-side reversal, 5-7 working days from
       the deposit date); otherwise see PAY-02.
RESOLUTION:
  Give the status, the refund reference if processed, and the expected date. Escalate if the
  window is breached or the refund failed.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Thanks for following up on the refund of Rs <amount>. <The refund was processed on <date>
  and the bank reference number is <refund UTR>. It was sent to the account you paid from
  (<bank>, ending <last 4>). If you cannot see it, please share this reference number with
  your bank, they can trace it. / The refund is still being processed; refunds normally reach
  your bank within 5-7 working days, so you should receive it by <date>.>
  If it has not arrived by then, reply to this email and we will trace it with priority.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - INITIATED beyond 7 working days -> Payments Ops, P2 (P1 beyond 10 working days), include
    refund_id, deposit_id, amount, created_at.
  - FAILED -> Payments Ops, P1, include refund_id and whether the source account is closed.
  - PROCESSED but the bank confirms non-receipt -> Payments Ops, P2, include refund_utr and
    the customer's bank statement.
DO NOT:
  - Do not offer to send the refund to a different bank account; refunds go to source.
  - Do not credit the Acme wallet instead of refunding unless Payments Ops decides so.

## PAY-06: Deposit amount above limit or below minimum
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "Trying to add Rs 2,00,000 via UPI but it fails"
  - "Minimum amount error when adding Rs 50"
  - "Deposit failed with limit exceeded"
  - "UPI limit kitna hai? 1 lakh se zyada nahi ho raha"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Amount and method they tried; any error text shown
TOOLS: get_deposits
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify and verify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Call get_deposits(). Look at the 10 most recent.
  3. Branch:
     - failure_reason AMOUNT_ABOVE_LIMIT and method UPI with amount > Rs 1,00,000: UPI limit
       per transaction is Rs 1,00,000 (DD-09). -> Advise splitting into smaller UPI payments or
       using IMPS/NEFT/RTGS bank transfer. Resolve.
     - UPI failed at an amount below Rs 1,00,000 with BANK_DECLINED: the customer's own bank
       or UPI app daily limit may be lower (many banks set Rs 1,00,000 per day across all UPI
       payments, some lower for new UPI users) -> ask them to check with their bank or use
       bank transfer. Resolve.
     - Amount below Rs 100: minimum deposit is Rs 100 (DD-09). The app does not allow it, so
       usually no deposit exists -> explain. Resolve.
     - Amount is within limits but failure_reason AMOUNT_ABOVE_LIMIT: unexpected ->
       escalate to Payments Ops, P3.
     - Was money debited for a rejected amount? -> PAY-02.
RESOLUTION:
  Explain the limits and the alternatives.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Thanks for reaching out about adding Rs <amount>. UPI deposits on Acme Invest can be up to
  Rs 1,00,000 per transaction, and the minimum deposit is Rs 100. Your bank may also set its
  own daily UPI limit. For larger amounts you can make several UPI payments or use a bank
  transfer (IMPS, NEFT or RTGS) from your verified bank account, as shown in the app under
  Add Funds. No money was taken for the attempt that failed.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Limit error for an amount inside the limits -> Payments Ops, P3, include deposit_id, amount, method.
DO NOT:
  - Do not offer to raise the UPI limit; it is fixed.
  - Do not confirm "no money was taken" unless the deposit shows no gateway capture (see PAY-02).

## PAY-07: Deposit ON_HOLD
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "My deposit is on hold, why?"
  - "Rs 3 lakh deposit shows under review"
  - "Deposit hold pe kyu hai? Please release"
  - "Money deducted, app says deposit held"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Amount and date. Nothing else; do not ask for documents unless Risk & Compliance asks.
TOOLS: get_deposits, get_risk_holds
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify and verify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02). If the user's status = FROZEN,
     go to fraud_risk_sop RSK-01 instead.
  2. Call get_deposits(status="ON_HOLD").
  3. Call get_risk_holds(active_only=true).
     For the "Hold RELEASED" branch: Call get_risk_holds(active_only=false).
     (Read reason_code and source only for the internal note; never disclose them.)
     - Active DEPOSIT_HOLD or ACCOUNT_FREEZE: the review is with Risk & Compliance. Tell the
       customer it is under a routine review; no timeline promise beyond "we will update you
       within 48 hours (sample value)". Create/update the ticket for Risk & Compliance, P2.
     - source = LAW_ENFORCEMENT: send only the generic review message; do not confirm any
       external request; route to Risk & Compliance. See fraud_risk_sop.
     - No active hold but deposit ON_HOLD: records conflict -> escalate to Payments Ops, P2.
     - Hold RELEASED but deposit still ON_HOLD after 2 hours (sample value) -> escalate to
       Payments Ops, P2.
  4. If the customer says they were asked to deposit by someone promising returns or to
     "unlock" funds -> fraud_risk_sop RSK-05.
RESOLUTION:
  Acknowledge, say the deposit is under a routine review, give the next update time, route
  to Risk & Compliance.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Thanks for writing to us about your deposit of Rs <amount> on <date>. Your deposit is safe
  and is going through a routine review, which we do from time to time to keep accounts
  secure. Our team is looking at it and we will update you within 48 hours. If we need
  anything from you, we will ask by email from this address.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Always route the ticket to Risk & Compliance, P2, include: user_id, deposit_id, amount,
    hold_id (internal only).
  - Customer has written 3+ times or 48 hours passed without update -> raise priority (SUP-09).
DO NOT:

## PAY-08: Duplicate deposit / charged twice
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "I paid once but money was debited twice"
  - "Double deduction for the same deposit"
  - "Do baar paisa kat gaya, ek hi baar credit hua"
  - "I paid again because the first one was pending, now both are gone"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Both UTRs (from bank statement/UPI app), amounts, times
TOOLS: get_deposits, check_deposit, find_deposits_by_reference
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify and verify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Call get_deposits(on_date=<payment date>).
  3. Branch:
     - Two deposits, both SUCCESS: two real deposits; check the ledger shows both credits
       (Call check_deposit(deposit_id=<deposit_id>) for each; ledger_credited is true).
       -> Both credited: the wallet has both amounts; explain and resolve. The customer can
          withdraw the extra amount (withdrawal_sop).
     - Two deposits, one SUCCESS and one PENDING / FAILED: handle the second with PAY-01 / PAY-02.
     - One deposit but the customer shows two debits with DIFFERENT UTRs: search the
       second UTR (Call find_deposits_by_reference(utr=<second utr>).). Found -> handle that
       deposit. Not found -> escalate to
       Payments Ops, P2 with the statement.
     - Two debits with the SAME UTR on the bank statement: usually a bank display duplicate
       or a debit plus a reversal. Ask the customer to check for a matching credit; if two
       real debits remain after 2 working days (sample value) -> escalate to Payments Ops, P2.
     - Same deposit_id credited twice in the ledger (check_deposit returns two DEPOSIT
       ledger_entries for it): our error in the customer's favour -> escalate to Payments Ops, P2; do not
       tell the customer it will be reversed until Payments Ops confirms.
RESOLUTION:
  Match every bank debit to a deposit row; each one ends up credited, pending, or refunded.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Sorry for the confusion about the double payment. I checked both payments:
  - Rs <amount> at <time>: <added to your wallet>
  - Rs <amount> at <time>: <added to your wallet / still being confirmed by the bank, it will
    be added or returned automatically within 72 hours / being returned to your bank by <date>>
  <If both were added, you can use the extra amount or withdraw it to your bank at any time.>
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - A debit with a UTR we cannot find, after the customer sent proof -> Payments Ops, P2,
    include both UTRs, amounts, statement screenshot.
  - Duplicate ledger credit for one deposit -> Payments Ops, P2, include deposit_id, entry_ids.
DO NOT:
  - Do not refund a successful deposit to the bank; the customer withdraws it themselves.
  - Do not debit or adjust the wallet yourself.

## PAY-09: Deposit credited with the wrong amount
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "I deposited Rs 10,000 but only Rs 9,800 came"
  - "Wallet shows less than what I paid"
  - "Kam paisa credit hua hai"
  - "Deposit credited but amount is wrong"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Amount paid, date, UTR, screenshot of the bank debit
TOOLS: find_deposits_by_reference, get_deposits, get_ledger_entries, check_wallet_balance
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify and verify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. If the customer gave a UTR: Call find_deposits_by_reference(utr=<utr>).
     Otherwise: Call get_deposits(on_date=<payment date>).
  3. Call get_ledger_entries(asset="INR", from_time=<deposit created_at>).
  4. Branch:
     - DEPOSIT credit equals the deposit amount equals the amount paid, but the balance is lower:
       the balance was used after the deposit (TRADE_BUY, FEE, WITHDRAWAL, or an order
       locking funds). Check locked for open orders
       (Call check_wallet_balance(asset="INR").).
       -> Explain the entries in plain words. Resolve. For fee questions see fees_charges_sop FEE-07.
     - Customer's bank debit is higher than the deposit amount: Acme charges no deposit fee
       (sample value). The difference is likely a bank/UPI app charge or two separate payments
       -> ask for the bank statement; if the statement shows one debit of the higher amount
       with this UTR -> escalate to Payments Ops, P2.
     - DEPOSIT credit in the ledger is less than the deposit amount -> our error -> escalate to
       Payments Ops, P1.
     - Customer deposited several times and is comparing a total: list each deposit and credit.
RESOLUTION:
  Reconcile the paid amount, the deposit record, and the ledger entries, and explain the
  difference. Escalate only when our records show a short credit or a debit we cannot explain.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Thanks for flagging this. I checked your deposit of Rs <amount> on <date>: the full
  Rs <amount> was added to your wallet at <time IST>. After that, <Rs X was used for a buy
  order of <coin> on <date>, including a trading fee of Rs Y / Rs X is reserved for an open
  order>, which is why your available balance now shows Rs <balance>.
  <If your bank debited more than Rs <amount>, please send us the bank statement entry so we
  can check it.>
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Ledger DEPOSIT credit lower than deposits.amount -> Payments Ops, P1, include deposit_id, entry_id.
  - Bank debit higher than recorded amount for the same UTR -> Payments Ops, P2, include UTR, statement.
DO NOT:
  - Do not adjust balances or promise a top-up.
  - Do not list other users' transactions or internal entry names.

## PAY-10: UPI app shows success but Acme shows failed
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "My UPI app shows payment successful but Acme says failed"
  - "UPI app pe success aa raha hai, Acme app me failed"
  - "UPI app has a transaction ID, your app shows failed deposit"
  - "Bank sent SMS debit successful, Acme says deposit failed"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - UPI transaction ID / UTR from the UPI app, amount, time; screenshot of the UPI app screen
TOOLS: get_deposits, check_deposit, find_deposits_by_reference
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify and verify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Call get_deposits(method="UPI", on_date=<payment date>).
  3. Call check_deposit(deposit_id=<deposit_id>). Use gateway.gateway_status.
     If no deposit matches, search by UTR: Call find_deposits_by_reference(utr=<utr>). Use
     gateway_records.
     - gateway_status CAPTURED and deposit FAILED with TIMEOUT: the confirmation arrived
       after our payment window closed. Check the refunds returned by check_deposit:
       - Refund INITIATED / PROCESSED: money is on its way back -> give timeline. Resolve.
       - No refund and no ledger credit -> escalate to Payments Ops, P1.
     - gateway_status CAPTURED and deposit has since become SUCCESS: it was a delay; check
       ledger_credited and tell the customer it is now added. Resolve.
     - gateway_status FAILED or REVERSED: the UPI app may show "success" for the debit leg
       only. The bank reverses the amount, usually within 5-7 working days -> tell the
       customer; wait.
     - gateway_status PENDING: treat as PAY-01 (auto-reconciliation for 72 hours).
     - No gateway record for this UTR: the payment may have gone to another merchant/UPI ID.
       Ask for the UPI app screenshot showing the payee name. If the payee is Acme / the
       Acme payment collection name -> escalate Payments Ops, P2. If a different payee -> the
       customer must contact that payee or their bank; resolve.
  4. If the payee in the screenshot is an individual's UPI ID (not Acme) and the customer
     says someone asked them to pay there -> possible scam, fraud_risk_sop RSK-05.
RESOLUTION:
  Explain whether the money will be credited, refunded, or auto-reversed by the bank, with dates.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  I understand the confusion when your UPI app says successful and our app does not. I
  checked your payment of Rs <amount> on <date>: <the confirmation from the bank reached us
  late, so the deposit could not be completed and we have started returning Rs <amount> to
  your account; it should arrive by <date>. / the payment was not completed on our side;
  your bank will return the amount to your account automatically, usually within 5-7
  working days.> You do not need to do anything else.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Captured, deposit FAILED, no refund, no credit -> Payments Ops, P1, include deposit_id,
    gateway_txn_id, bank_reference, amount.
  - UTR not found but payee is Acme -> Payments Ops, P2, include UTR, screenshot.
DO NOT:
  - Do not tell the customer to raise a dispute with the UPI app before checking our records.
  - Do not name the payment gateway or bank partner.

## PAY-11: Deposit methods, limits and timings (how-to)
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "How do I add money to Acme?"
  - "What is the maximum I can deposit? Can I use NEFT?"
  - "Paisa kaise add kare? Any charges for deposit?"
  - "How long does NEFT take to reflect?"
  - "Can I deposit using a credit card or wallet?"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - None (general question). If about their own failed attempt, use the matching issue.
TOOLS: get_user, get_bank_accounts
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Optional: identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02) and check prerequisites:
     Call get_user(). Look at kyc_status.
     Call get_bank_accounts(status="VERIFIED").
     - kyc_status not VERIFIED: deposits need completed KYC -> kyc_sop. Answer and resolve.
     - No VERIFIED bank account: they must add one first -> bank_account_sop BNK-01.
  2. Answer from the policy below. Resolve.
POLICY (DD-09):
  - Methods: UPI (up to Rs 1,00,000 per transaction); IMPS / NEFT / RTGS bank transfer using
    the account details shown in the app under Add Funds.
  - Minimum deposit: Rs 100. For bank transfers Acme sets no per-transaction maximum; the

  - Only from a VERIFIED bank account in the customer's own name. Third-party money is refunded.
  - Timings: UPI within 5 minutes; IMPS within 30 minutes; NEFT within 2 banking hours; RTGS
    within 1 banking hour (NEFT/RTGS depend on bank working hours).
  - Not accepted: credit cards, debit card payments, prepaid wallets, cash, cheques (sample value).
  - Acme charges no fee for INR deposits (sample value); the customer's bank may.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,

  - UPI: up to Rs 1,00,000 per payment, usually added within 5 minutes.
  - Bank transfer (IMPS, NEFT or RTGS): use the account details shown in the app under Add
    Funds. IMPS is usually added within 30 minutes, NEFT within 2 banking hours and RTGS
    within 1 banking hour.

  verified in the app. We do not charge any fee for deposits.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:

  - Do not suggest workarounds such as depositing via a friend's account.
