## BNK-00: Scope and quick triage
======================================================================
SCOPE:
  Bank accounts linked to an Acme Invest account for INR deposits and withdrawals: how to add,
  penny drop and reverse penny drop verification, name mismatch, pending verification,
  primary account, removal, unsupported account types, bank mergers / IFSC changes and
  accounts already linked elsewhere.
  NOT in scope:
    - Deposits that failed because of the bank account -> payment_sop PAY-04 / PAY-12
    - Withdrawals to a closed account -> withdrawal_sop WDR-09
    - KYC name corrections -> kyc_sop

KEY RULES (DD-09 unless marked):
  - Only savings or current accounts held in the customer's own name, at an Indian bank,
    can be linked. The account must be VERIFIED before any deposit or withdrawal.
  - Maximum 3 active (non-REMOVED) bank accounts per user (sample value).
  - Exactly one account is primary (is_primary = true); withdrawals default to it.
  - Support never adds, edits or removes bank details because of an email request
    (support_guidelines_sop SUP-01). The customer does it in the app.

VERIFICATION METHODS:
  - PENNY_DROP: Acme sends Re 1 to the account number + IFSC the customer typed; the bank
    returns the account holder name (account_holder_name).
  - REVERSE_PENNY_DROP: the customer pays Re 1 by UPI from the account; the bank shares the
    account details and holder name; the Re 1 is refunded within 48 hours (sample value).

NAME MATCH THRESHOLDS (sample values; bank_accounts.name_match_score, 0-100, compares the
bank's account_holder_name with the KYC name):
  - 80 or more: VERIFIED automatically.
  - 60 to 79:   stays PENDING_VERIFICATION for manual review by Payments Ops (up to 48 hours,
                sample value).
  - Below 60:   FAILED.

TOOLS: get_bank_accounts
QUICK TRIAGE (find the user first, see support_guidelines_sop SUP-02, then):
  Call get_bank_accounts().

  status                 Go to
  PENDING_VERIFICATION   BNK-08 (or BNK-03 if score 60-79)
  VERIFIED               BNK-05 / BNK-06 / BNK-09 depending on the question
  FAILED                 BNK-02 (no name returned) / BNK-03 (score < 60) / BNK-04 (reverse penny drop)
  REMOVED                BNK-06
  No accounts            BNK-01

## BNK-01: How to add a bank account
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "How do I link my bank account?"
  - "Where do I add bank details for withdrawal?"
  - "Bank account kaise add kare?"
  - "Can I add a second bank account?"
  - "Please add my ICICI account to my profile" (email request)
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - None for a how-to. Do not ask for the full account number by email.
TOOLS: get_user, get_bank_accounts
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Call get_user(). Check kyc_status.
     - Not VERIFIED: a bank account can be added only after KYC is VERIFIED (sample rule) ->
       kyc_sop. Explain and resolve.
  3. Call get_bank_accounts(). Count the accounts whose status is not REMOVED.
     - 3 or more: limit reached (sample value); they must remove one first (BNK-06). Resolve.
     - Fewer than 3: give the steps below. Resolve.
  4. If the customer sent account details and asked us to add them: reply with the steps;
     do not add. Remind them not to share full account numbers by email.
  5. Joint, NRE/NRO, business or wallet accounts -> BNK-07.
STEPS FOR THE CUSTOMER (sample app flow):
  Profile > Bank Accounts > Add Bank Account, then either:
  - Verify with UPI (reverse penny drop): pay Re 1 from the UPI app linked to that account;
    it is refunded. Fastest option.
  - Enter account number and IFSC (penny drop): Acme sends Re 1 to the account.
  Verification usually completes within a few minutes.
RESOLUTION:
  Share the steps and the own-name rule.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Thanks for reaching out. You can add a bank account yourself in the app:
  1. Go to Profile > Bank Accounts > Add Bank Account.
  2. Choose "Verify with UPI" and pay Re 1 from the UPI app linked to that bank account (the
     Re 1 is refunded), or enter the account number and IFSC and we will send Re 1 to check it.
  The account must be a savings or current account in your own name, the same name as your
  PAN. You can link up to 3 accounts. For your security, we cannot add bank details sent by
  email, so please avoid sharing full account numbers with anyone.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Not needed for a how-to. If adding fails, use BNK-02 / BNK-04.
DO NOT:
  - Do not add or edit a bank account from email details.
  - Do not repeat the customer's full account number back in the reply.

## BNK-02: Penny drop verification failed
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "Bank verification failed while adding my account"
  - "It says unable to verify account, but details are correct"
  - "Penny drop failed, Re 1 not received"
  - "Account verify nahi ho raha, IFSC sahi hai"
  - "Error: account details invalid"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Bank name, last 4 digits of the account, IFSC; whether the account is active and
    whether it is savings/current/NRE/joint
TOOLS: get_bank_accounts, get_bank_verification_failures
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify and verify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Call get_bank_accounts(). Look at the accounts with verification_method
     PENNY_DROP.
  3. Branch on the latest FAILED account:
     - account_holder_name is NULL and name_match_score is NULL: the bank did not confirm the
       account. Common causes: wrong account number or IFSC, account closed/dormant/frozen at
       the bank, NRE or other unsupported type, or the bank's systems were temporarily down.
       -> Ask the customer to recheck the account number and IFSC (from a cheque or passbook,
          not from memory), confirm the account is active, and retry after 2 hours
          (sample value), or use Verify with UPI (BNK-04).
     - account_holder_name present and name_match_score < 60: name mismatch -> BNK-03.
     - Several FAILED attempts today (3 or more, sample value): the add option may be
       paused until the next day (sample rule). Ask them to retry tomorrow or use UPI.
     - The same account (same masked number + IFSC) failed 3 times on different days with
       the customer confirming it is active: escalate to Payments Ops, P3 for a manual check.
  4. IFSC looks outdated (bank merged): -> BNK-09.
  5. Multiple users failing for the same bank in the last hour (pattern visible in several
     tickets): possible bank outage -> escalate Tech On-call, P2.
     Call get_bank_verification_failures(minutes=60). Check failures_by_bank.
RESOLUTION:
  Recheck details and retry, or verify with UPI. Escalate only repeated failures on a
  confirmed active account.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Sorry that your bank account could not be verified. When we checked your account ending
  <last 4> with <bank>, the bank did not confirm the details. This usually happens when the
  account number or IFSC has a small error, or the account is inactive or not a regular
  savings/current account.
  Please:
  - Check the account number and IFSC against your cheque book or passbook, and try again
    after 2 hours, or
  - Use "Verify with UPI" instead, which confirms the account in a few seconds.
  If it still fails, reply to this email and we will check it for you.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - 3 failures on a confirmed active, supported account -> Payments Ops, P3, include user_id,
    bank_account_ids, bank_name, ifsc, masked number, created_at of each attempt.
  - Many users failing for one bank -> Tech On-call, P2, include bank_name and counts.
DO NOT:
  - Do not mark the account verified manually.
  - Do not ask for a cancelled cheque or statement unless Payments Ops requests it.

## BNK-03: Name mismatch between bank and KYC
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "Bank verification failed due to name mismatch but it is my account"
  - "My bank has my name as R. Kumar and PAN has Rahul Kumar"
  - "After marriage my surname changed in bank, not in PAN"
  - "Naam match nahi ho raha, same person hoon"
  - "Bank account under review for name check"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Confirmation that the account is in their own name (not joint/family)
  - If a legal name change: which document has the new name (PAN, bank, both)
TOOLS: get_bank_accounts, get_kyc_applications
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify and verify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Call get_bank_accounts().
  3. Get the KYC name:
     Call get_kyc_applications(). In the application with status VERIFIED, take
     name_on_doc of the document with doc_type PAN and verification_status PASSED.
  4. Branch on name_match_score (sample thresholds, see BNK-00):
     - 80 or more and status VERIFIED: no issue; resolve.
     - 60-79, PENDING_VERIFICATION: manual review. Within 48 hours of created_at -> tell the
       customer to wait. Beyond 48 hours -> escalate Payments Ops, P3.
     - Below 60, FAILED: compare the two names yourself:
       - Clearly the same person written differently (initials, order, spelling like
         "Mohd" / "Mohammed", missing middle name): ask the customer to add the account again
         via Verify with UPI (some banks return the full name that way). If it fails again ->
         escalate Payments Ops, P3 for manual review with both names.
       - Different surname after marriage or legal name change: the customer should update
         the name with the bank (or their KYC via kyc_sop re-KYC) so both match, then add the
         account again. Resolve.
       - A different person's name (spouse, parent, company): third-party account, cannot be
         linked. Resolve (explain the own-name rule).
  5. Never tell the customer the numeric score or the thresholds.
RESOLUTION:
  Same person, formatting difference -> retry/manual review. Different legal name -> align
  records. Different person -> not allowed.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Thanks for explaining. We could not verify your bank account ending <last 4> because the
  name your bank has on record does not closely match the name on your PAN.
  <It is being reviewed by our team, and you will hear from us within 48 hours. / Please try
  adding it again using "Verify with UPI"; if that also fails, reply and we will review it
  manually. / If your name has changed, please update it with your bank so it matches your
  PAN, then add the account again.>
  For your safety, we can only link bank accounts held in your own name.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Score 60-79 pending beyond 48 hours -> Payments Ops, P3, include bank_account_id,
    account_holder_name, KYC name, score.
  - Same-person formatting difference that failed twice -> Payments Ops, P3, same details.
DO NOT:
  - Do not reveal the name match score, thresholds or the bank's returned name if it is not
    the customer's (it may be another person's name).
  - Do not approve an account in another person's name, even a family member's.

## BNK-04: Reverse penny drop / UPI verification issues
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "I paid Re 1 for bank verification but account not added"
  - "Re 1 debited, not refunded"
  - "UPI verification linked the wrong bank account"
  - "Verify with UPI fails, my UPI app says payment declined"
  - "1 rupee kat gaya but verification fail"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - UPI app used, date/time, which bank account (bank + last 4) was used to pay the Re 1
TOOLS: get_bank_accounts
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify and verify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Call get_bank_accounts(). Look at the accounts with verification_method
     REVERSE_PENNY_DROP.
  3. Branch:
     - Account VERIFIED but for a different bank than the customer intended: the UPI app paid from
       its default account. The customer can keep it, or remove it (BNK-06) and retry after
       selecting the right account in the UPI app. Resolve.
     - Account FAILED with score < 60: name mismatch -> BNK-03.
     - Account FAILED with no name: the bank did not share details (some banks do not support this
       method) -> ask them to use account number + IFSC (penny drop, BNK-02). Resolve.
     - No account created for the attempt: the Re 1 did not complete or reached us late. The
       customer should retry. For the Re 1:
       - Less than 48 hours (sample value): it is refunded automatically -> wait.
       - More than 48 hours and still not refunded: ask for the UTR from the UPI app and
         escalate to Payments Ops, P4 (amount is Re 1).
     - Account PENDING_VERIFICATION -> BNK-08.
  4. UPI app declines the payment: usually the UPI app or bank limit/PIN issue; ask them to
     retry or use penny drop. Resolve.
RESOLUTION:
  Retry with the right account, or use penny drop; Re 1 is refunded automatically.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Thanks for letting us know. When you verify with UPI, your bank account is linked based on
  the account your UPI app paid the Re 1 from. <Your UPI app used your <bank> account ending
  <last 4>, which is now linked. If you want a different account, remove this one under
  Profile > Bank Accounts and try again after selecting the right account in your UPI app. /
  Your bank did not share the account details this way, so please add the account using the
  account number and IFSC instead.>
  The Re 1 used for verification is returned automatically within 48 hours.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Re 1 not refunded after 48 hours -> Payments Ops, P4, include UTR, date, user_id.
DO NOT:
  - Do not promise to link a different account than the one the UPI payment came from.

## BNK-05: Change primary bank account
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "Please change my primary bank to Axis"
  - "How do I make my new account the default for withdrawal?"
  - "Primary account kaise change kare?"
  - "Withdrawals keep going to my old account"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Which account they want as primary (bank + last 4)
TOOLS: get_bank_accounts, get_withdrawals
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify and verify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Call get_bank_accounts(). Look at the accounts whose status is not REMOVED.
     - Target account VERIFIED: the customer can set it as primary in the app (Profile > Bank
       Accounts > select account > Make Primary) (sample flow). Resolve.
     - Target account not yet added -> BNK-01. PENDING_VERIFICATION -> BNK-08. FAILED -> BNK-02/03.

       Call get_withdrawals(asset="INR"). Look at the latest 5 (bank_account_id).
       The account is chosen at the time of each withdrawal; earlier withdrawals are not
       changed. If a withdrawal after the change went to the non-primary account without the
       customer selecting it -> escalate Tech On-call, P3.
  3. Pending withdrawal to the old account -> withdrawal_sop WDR-09 / WDR-11.
RESOLUTION:
  Customer changes primary in the app.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:

  You can change your primary bank account yourself: go to Profile > Bank Accounts, select
  your <bank> account ending <last 4> and tap Make Primary. New withdrawals will go to that

  For your security, we cannot change bank details by email.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Withdrawal went to a non-selected account after the change -> Tech On-call, P3, include
    withdrawal_id, bank_account_ids, app version.
DO NOT:
  - Do not change is_primary on the customer's behalf.

## BNK-06: Remove a bank account
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "Please delete my old bank account from Acme"
  - "I closed my Kotak account, remove it"
  - "Remove option not showing for my bank account"
  - "Purana account hatana hai"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Which account (bank + last 4)
TOOLS: get_bank_accounts, get_withdrawals, get_deposits
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify and verify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Call get_bank_accounts(). Look at the accounts whose status is not REMOVED.
  3. Check what blocks removal (sample rules):
     - It is the primary account and another VERIFIED account exists: make another primary
       first (BNK-05), then remove.
     - It is the only VERIFIED account: it can be removed, but the customer cannot withdraw
       until a new one is verified. Warn them.
     - Pending withdrawal to it:
       Call get_withdrawals(bank_account_id=<bank_account_id>,
            statuses=["REQUESTED", "PENDING_APPROVAL", "PROCESSING", "ON_HOLD"]).
       -> Wait until it completes or cancel it (withdrawal_sop WDR-11).
     - Pending deposit or refund from it:
       Call get_deposits(status="PENDING").
       Call get_deposits(status="REFUND_INITIATED").
       A deposit with that bank_account_id: wait until complete; refunds still go to the source
       account.
     - No blocker: customer removes it in the app (Profile > Bank Accounts > select > Remove).
       Resolve.
  4. Remove option not visible and none of the blockers apply -> escalate Tech On-call, P3.
  5. Customer wants to remove all bank details because they are closing their account ->
     account_closure_sop.
RESOLUTION:
  Customer removes in the app after any blocker is cleared.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  You can remove the account yourself from Profile > Bank Accounts: select the <bank>
  account ending <last 4> and tap Remove. <As it is currently your primary account, please
  first make another verified account primary. / There is a withdrawal of Rs <amount> being
  sent to it right now, so please wait until it completes.> Past transactions stay in your
  history.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Remove fails with no blocker -> Tech On-call, P3, include bank_account_id, screenshot.
DO NOT:
  - Do not remove the account yourself because of an email request.

## BNK-07: Joint account, NRE/NRO or business account (not supported)
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "Can I link my NRE account? I live in Dubai"
  - "My account is a joint account with my wife, verification failed"
  - "Can I use my company current account?"
  - "NRO account add nahi ho raha"
  - "Can I add my mobile wallet instead of a bank account?"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Account type; residency status if NRE/NRO is mentioned
TOOLS: none
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Branch by account type (sample rules):
     - NRE / NRO: not supported. Acme accounts are for Indian residents only (DD-09 KYC). If
       the customer says they are now a non-resident, they should stop using the account;
       escalate to Risk & Compliance, P3, to review residency (do not close the account).
     - Joint account: supported only if the customer is the primary (first) holder and the
       bank returns their name first; otherwise name matching fails (BNK-03). Many banks
       return only the first holder's name. If the customer is second holder -> not possible.
     - Business / company / partnership / HUF current account: not supported; must be a
       personal account in the customer's name.
     - Wallets, payments-bank wallets, credit cards, foreign accounts: not supported.
       (Payments-bank savings accounts with an IFSC are treated like normal savings accounts.)
  3. Resolve with an explanation.
RESOLUTION:
  Explain which account types are supported; suggest a personal savings/current account.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Thanks for your question. Acme Invest can link only personal savings or current accounts
  at an Indian bank that are held in your own name. <NRE and NRO accounts / business
  accounts / accounts where you are not the first holder> cannot be linked. Please add a
  personal account in your own name from Profile > Bank Accounts.
  <As Acme Invest is available only to Indian residents, our team will get in touch about
  your residency status.>
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Customer says they are a non-resident -> Risk & Compliance, P3, include user_id and the
    customer's statement.
DO NOT:
  - Do not make exceptions for NRE/NRO or business accounts.
  - Do not close or freeze the account yourself.

## BNK-08: Bank account shows PENDING_VERIFICATION for a long time
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "Bank account still under verification since 3 days"
  - "Pending verification, can't withdraw"
  - "Bank verification pending hai kab hoga?"
  - "Added my account yesterday, still in progress"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Which account (bank + last 4)
TOOLS: get_bank_accounts
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify and verify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Call get_bank_accounts(status="PENDING_VERIFICATION").
  3. Branch:
     - name_match_score between 60 and 79: in manual name review (BNK-03). Within 48 hours of
       created_at -> wait. Beyond -> escalate Payments Ops, P3 (P2 if the customer has a
       withdrawal blocked by it and no other verified account).
     - name_match_score NULL and created_at less than 2 hours ago (sample value): the bank has
       not answered yet -> wait 2 hours.
     - name_match_score NULL and older than 2 hours: the verification call to the bank did not
       complete. Ask the customer to remove it and add again (preferably Verify with UPI). If
       they cannot remove it or it happens again -> escalate Payments Ops, P3.
     - name_match_score 80 or more but still pending: status not updated -> escalate Payments
       Ops, P3.
RESOLUTION:
  Wait inside the window; re-add or escalate outside it.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Thanks for your patience. Your bank account ending <last 4> is <being reviewed by our team
  to confirm the account name; this is completed within 48 hours, by <date> / still waiting for
  confirmation from your bank>. <Please remove it and add it again using "Verify with UPI",
  which usually confirms in a few seconds.> We will let you know as soon as it is verified.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Manual review beyond 48 hours -> Payments Ops, P3 (P2 if blocking the only withdrawal
    route), include bank_account_id, score, created_at.
  - Score >= 80 but not VERIFIED -> Payments Ops, P3.
DO NOT:
  - Do not tell the customer the account "will be approved"; only give the review time frame.

## BNK-09: Bank merged or IFSC changed
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "My bank merged with another bank, IFSC changed, withdrawals failing"
  - "Bank sent new IFSC code, how do I update?"
  - "Old IFSC not working while adding account"
  - "Branch shift ho gaya, IFSC naya hai"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Bank name, last 4 digits, the new IFSC (the IFSC itself is not sensitive)
TOOLS: get_bank_accounts, get_withdrawals
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify and verify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Call get_bank_accounts(). Look at the accounts whose status is not REMOVED.
  3. Branch:
     - Account VERIFIED with the old IFSC: IFSC cannot be edited in place (sample rule). The
       customer must add the account again with the new IFSC (it will be verified again),
       make it primary (BNK-05) and remove the old one (BNK-06). Resolve.
     - Recent withdrawals to the old IFSC failing
       (Call get_withdrawals(bank_account_id=<bank_account_id>). Latest 5.)
       -> FAILED: money returns to the wallet (withdrawal_sop WDR-03); re-add with new IFSC.
       -> Many customers of the same bank failing (mass change): escalate Payments Ops, P3,
          so the old IFSCs can be mapped.
     - Adding with the old IFSC fails: use the new IFSC from the bank's letter, passbook or
       cheque book. Resolve.
     - Adding the same account again is blocked because the old record exists: remove the old
       one first. If removal is blocked -> BNK-06.
RESOLUTION:
  Re-add the account with the new IFSC, set primary, remove the old one.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Thanks for letting us know about the IFSC change. To keep your withdrawals working, please:
  1. Add the same account again under Profile > Bank Accounts using the new IFSC <new IFSC>.
  2. Once it is verified, make it your primary account.
  3. Remove the old entry with the previous IFSC.
  <Your recent withdrawal of Rs <amount> could not go through with the old IFSC; the money is
  back in your Acme wallet, so you can withdraw again once the new details are verified.>
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Several customers of one bank affected -> Payments Ops, P3, include bank_name, old and new IFSC, ticket IDs.
DO NOT:
  - Do not update the IFSC in the customer's record yourself.

## BNK-10: Bank account already linked to another Acme account
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "It says this bank account is already in use"
  - "I created a new Acme account and can't add my bank"
  - "Bank already linked with another user error"
  - "Mera hi account hai, dusre account me bhi hai"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Bank name, last 4 digits, and whether they have another Acme account (old phone/email)
TOOLS: find_bank_account_owners
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify and verify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Look for other users with the same bank account (masked number + IFSC; the tool compares
     holder names because the masked number alone is not unique):
     Call find_bank_account_owners(account_number_masked=<masked>, ifsc=<ifsc>).
  3. Branch:
     - Match with same_name_as_customer = true: duplicate accounts ->
       onboarding_sop ONB-07. Do not share details of the other account.
     - Match with same_name_as_customer = false: a bank account cannot be shared
       between people -> explain the own-name rule. If the customer insists it is only theirs
       -> escalate Risk & Compliance, P3 (possible misuse).
     - Other account is CLOSED (other_account_status = CLOSED): escalate Payments Ops, P3, to release
       the bank account.
     - No match: the error may be wrong -> escalate Tech On-call, P3, with a screenshot.
RESOLUTION:
  Use the existing account, or follow the duplicate account process.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Thanks for writing. A bank account can be linked to only one Acme Invest account. The
