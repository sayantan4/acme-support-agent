## TRD-00: Scope and quick triage
======================================================================
Covers buy/sell orders on Acme Invest (MARKET, LIMIT, STOP_LIMIT), fills, rejections,
cancellations, SIPs, holdings and portfolio values, and trade reversal requests.
Not covered here: INR deposits (payment_sop), INR withdrawals (withdrawal_sop), crypto
transfers (crypto_transfers_sop), fee disputes (fees_charges_sop), TDS and statements
(tax_statements_sop), frozen accounts (fraud_risk_sop RSK-01).

Quick triage (after identifying the user per support_guidelines_sop SUP-02):
  - "Order failed / rejected"                         -> TRD-01
  - "Limit order not executing / still open"          -> TRD-02
  - "Only part of my order filled"                    -> TRD-03
  - "Got a worse price than shown"                    -> TRD-04
  - "Money deducted but no coins / order missing"     -> TRD-05
  - "Cancelled but it still executed / cannot cancel" -> TRD-06
  - "Portfolio value / coin balance wrong"            -> TRD-07
  - "Coin halted / delisted / cannot buy"             -> TRD-08
  - "SIP failed or skipped"                           -> TRD-09
  - "Pause / stop / change my SIP"                    -> TRD-10
  - "Trade I did not place"                           -> TRD-11 (P1, security first)
  - "Reverse / undo my trade"                         -> TRD-12
  - "Average price / P&L looks wrong"                 -> TRD-13
  - "Stop-limit did not trigger / order expired"      -> TRD-14

Key rules for every trading conversation:
  - Executed trades are final. Never promise a reversal, refund or compensation (TRD-12).
  - Never give investment advice ("should I buy/sell/hold", "will the price go up").
  - All timestamps are UTC in the tables; convert to IST for the customer.
  - Fees: 0.2% of order value plus 18% GST on the fee. TDS: 1% of sale value on sells
    (data_dictionary DD-09). Fee disputes -> fees_charges_sop; TDS -> tax_statements_sop.
  - If the user's status is FROZEN or a TRADE_RESTRICTION hold exists, go to fraud_risk_sop RSK-01
    and do not disclose the reason.

TOOLS: get_orders
Common lookup used by many sections:
  Call get_orders(days=7).

## TRD-01: Order was rejected
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "My buy order failed, it says order rejected"
  - "Why is my order getting rejected again and again?"
  - "Sell order not going through, showing error"
  - "Order reject ho gaya, balance toh tha account mein"
  - "Tried to buy ETH for Rs 50, it did not place"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Coin/pair, side (buy/sell), approximate amount and time, screenshot of the error if available
TOOLS: get_orders, check_wallet_balance, get_instrument, get_risk_holds, get_order_error_count, get_ledger_entries
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02) and check the user's status and kyc_status.
  2. Find the rejected order(s):
     Call get_orders(status="REJECTED", days=7).
     - No REJECTED order found: the order never reached us (app/network error) ->
       app_technical_sop; ask the customer for the time and a screenshot.
  3. Branch on reject_reason:
     - INSUFFICIENT_BALANCE:
       Call check_wallet_balance().
       -> available (not locked) was below order value + fee (buy) or below quantity (sell).
          If locked is high, explain funds are reserved by open orders or pending withdrawals
          (list them, see TRD-07). Resolve: customer can cancel open orders or add funds.
     - BELOW_MIN_ORDER_VALUE:
       Call get_instrument(symbol=<symbol>).
       -> order value was below min_order_value (Rs 100 for INR pairs). Resolve: ask the
          customer to place an order of at least that value.
     - ABOVE_MAX_ORDER_VALUE:
       Call get_instrument(symbol=<symbol>).
       -> Resolve: split into smaller orders, each below max_order_value.
     - PRICE_OUT_OF_BAND: limit price was too far from the current market price (price band
       protects against typing errors). Resolve: place the limit closer to the market price.
     - INSTRUMENT_HALTED: Call get_instrument(symbol=<symbol>). Check status.
       -> go to TRD-08.
     - KYC_NOT_VERIFIED: the user's kyc_status is not VERIFIED -> kyc_sop; tell the customer trading
       unlocks after KYC is verified.
     - ACCOUNT_RESTRICTED:
       Call get_risk_holds(active_only=true).
       -> fraud_risk_sop RSK-01. Never disclose reason_code or source.
     - SELF_TRADE_PREVENTION: the order would have matched against the customer's own open
       order on the other side. Resolve: cancel or modify the opposite open order.
     - SYSTEM_ERROR: check for other rejects at the same time:
       Call get_order_error_count(around_time=<created_at>, minutes=15).
       -> system_errors is 1 (single isolated case): ask the customer to retry; if it repeats,
          escalate P2.
          Many cases: platform incident -> escalate to Tech On-call (P2) and tell the customer
          we are aware; no funds were debited for a rejected order (verify in step 4).
  4. Confirm nothing was debited for the rejected order:
     Call get_ledger_entries(order_id=<order_id>).
     -> No entries or a matching REVERSAL: fine. A debit with no reversal -> escalate (P1).
RESOLUTION:
  Explain the reason in plain words and the exact fix (amount, minimum, retry). A rejected order
  never executes and does not charge a fee.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Sorry your <coin> order did not go through. I checked it, and it was not placed because
  <plain reason, e.g. "the order value was below the minimum of Rs 100" / "your available
  balance was lower than the order amount because Rs <x> is reserved for an open order">.
  No money was deducted for this order. You can place it again <fix>.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - SYSTEM_ERROR repeating or affecting many users -> Tech On-call, P2, include: order_ids, times, count
  - Rejected order but a ledger debit without reversal -> Trading Ops, P1, include: order_id, entry_id
  - reject_reason does not match balances (e.g. INSUFFICIENT_BALANCE but available was enough) -> Trading Ops, P2
DO NOT:
  - Do not reveal risk hold reasons or the exact price band percentage logic beyond "too far from market price".
  - Do not place, modify or retry orders for the customer.

## TRD-02: Limit order stuck OPEN / not executing
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "I placed a limit order yesterday and it is still open"
  - "Price touched my limit but order did not execute"
  - "Why is my buy order pending for so long?"
  - "Limit order laga diya tha, abhi tak execute nahi hua"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Coin/pair, limit price, time placed (or order id)
TOOLS: get_orders, get_market_price_range, get_instrument, check_wallet_balance
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Find the order:
     Call get_orders(statuses=["OPEN", "NEW", "PARTIALLY_FILLED"]).
  3. Explain how a limit order works: a BUY limit executes only at the limit price or LOWER;
     a SELL limit executes only at the limit price or HIGHER. It also needs a matching seller/buyer
     at that price; orders at the same price fill in time order (first come, first served).
  4. Check whether the market actually traded at or through the limit since the order was placed:
     Call get_market_price_range(symbol=<symbol>, from_time=<order_created_at>).
     - BUY: low > limit price -> the price never reached the limit. Resolve: explain; the order
       stays open until filled or cancelled by the customer.
     - SELL: high < limit price -> the price never reached the limit. Resolve as above.
     - Price traded exactly AT the limit only briefly (low = limit for BUY, high = limit for SELL):
       other orders at the same price were ahead in the queue. Resolve: explain queue priority.
     - Price traded clearly THROUGH the limit (e.g. BUY low well below limit) and the order is
       still OPEN with filled_quantity = 0 -> abnormal -> escalate to Trading Ops (P2).
     - Price the customer saw on a chart/another platform differs from Acme trades: prices differ
       across platforms; only Acme's order book fills Acme orders. Resolve: explain.
  5. Call get_instrument(symbol=<symbol>). If status is not TRADING -> TRD-08.
  6. Funds for an open order are locked; confirm with locked from
     Call check_wallet_balance() (TRD-07).
RESOLUTION:
  Explain that the order is working normally and will fill only if the market reaches the limit
  price with matching volume. The customer may keep it, cancel it, or place a new order at a
  different price. Do not suggest a price.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Thanks for checking on your <coin> order. Your limit order to <buy/sell> at Rs <price> is still
  open. A limit order only executes when the market price reaches your price (or better) and
  there is a matching order on the other side. Since you placed it on <date, IST>, the price on
  Acme Invest has ranged between Rs <low> and Rs <high>, so it has not reached your price yet.
  The funds for this order are reserved until it fills or you cancel it. You can cancel or change
  the order anytime from the Orders section of the app.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Market traded through the limit and the order did not fill -> Trading Ops, P2, include: order_id,
    limit price, created_at, low/high from trades since then
DO NOT:
  - Do not advise which price to set or whether to wait.
  - Do not compare with or name other exchanges' prices.

## TRD-03: Order PARTIALLY_FILLED
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "I ordered 1 ETH but got only 0.4 ETH"
  - "Order shows partially filled, where is the rest?"
  - "Half coins aaye, baaki paisa kahan gaya?"
  - "My order is partially executed, will the remaining happen?"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Coin/pair and order time or order id
TOOLS: check_order, check_wallet_balance, get_ledger_entries
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Call check_order(order_id=<order_id>). Check order.user_id is the customer's user_id.
  3. List the fills: use fills returned by check_order.
     Confirm the sum of fills quantity = order.filled_quantity. Mismatch -> escalate Trading Ops (P2).
  4. Branch on status:
     - PARTIALLY_FILLED (LIMIT order): only part of the quantity found a match at the limit price.
       The rest stays open and its funds stay locked. Resolve: explain; customer may wait or
       cancel the remaining part (unfilled funds return to available balance immediately).
     - CANCELLED with filled_quantity > 0: the filled part is final; the unfilled part was
       cancelled (by the customer or on expiry). Check the unfilled amount was released:
       Call check_wallet_balance(asset=<quote_asset>).
       -> If locked still includes the unfilled amount after 15 minutes (sample value) -> escalate P2.
     - MARKET order partly filled then CANCELLED/EXPIRED: not enough liquidity within the
       price protection band; the remainder was cancelled and funds released. Resolve: explain.
  5. Verify ledger for the filled part, for each trade_id in fills:
     Call get_ledger_entries(reference_type="TRADE", reference_id=<trade_id>).
     -> TRADE_BUY/TRADE_SELL + FEE (+ TDS for sells) present: fine. Missing -> escalate P1.
RESOLUTION:
  Explain what filled (quantity, average price), what happened to the rest, and that the unfilled
  amount is either still working (open) or back in the available balance.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  I checked your <coin> order from <date, IST>. <filled qty> of <total qty> <coin> was bought/sold
  at an average price of Rs <avg>. <"The remaining <qty> is still open at your price of Rs <price>
  and will fill only if the market reaches it; the money for this part stays reserved until it
  fills or you cancel it." / "The remaining part was cancelled and Rs <x> has been returned to your
  available balance.">
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Fills do not add up to filled_quantity, or ledger entries missing -> Trading Ops, P1/P2, include: order_id, trade_ids
  - Unfilled funds still locked after the order is closed -> Trading Ops, P2
DO NOT:
  - Do not promise the remaining quantity will fill.

## TRD-04: Market order executed at a worse price than shown (slippage)
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "App showed Rs 2,10,000 for ETH but I got it at Rs 2,14,500"
  - "I sold at market and got much lower price than displayed"
  - "Price dikhaya kuch aur, execute hua kuch aur. This is cheating"
  - "Huge slippage on my BTC buy, please refund the difference"
  - "Why is my average price higher than the price on the screen?"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Coin/pair, order time or order id, the price they saw (screenshot if possible)
TOOLS: check_order, get_market_price_range, get_price_incidents
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Call check_order(order_id=<order_id>). Check order.user_id is the customer's user_id.
     - order_type is LIMIT: a limit order can never fill worse than the limit price. If
       avg_fill_price is worse than price (higher for BUY, lower for SELL) -> escalate P1.
  3. Compare the individual fills returned by check_order with avg_fill_price:
     - A MARKET order fills against the best available orders in the book; a large order can
       consume several price levels, so later fills are at worse prices. The weighted average of
       fills should equal avg_fill_price. Mismatch -> escalate Trading Ops (P2).
  4. Calculate slippage = (avg_fill_price - price_seen) / price_seen x 100 (sign depends on side).
     Check market trades around the order time:
     Call get_market_price_range(symbol=<symbol>, from_time=<created_at - 1 minute>,
          to_time=<created_at + 1 minute>).
     - Fills are within the market range at that minute: normal slippage in a fast market or thin
       order book -> resolve with explanation.
     - Fills are outside the market range at that time -> possible system error -> step 5.
  5. Check for a known incident:
     Call get_price_incidents(symbol=<symbol>, at_time=<created_at>).
     - Incident found: tell the customer we are aware of an issue during that period and it is
       being reviewed; follow the incident's compensation_policy exactly (do not add to it).
       Escalate to Trading Ops (P2) with the order linked to the incident_id.
     - No incident and fills outside the market range -> escalate Trading Ops (P2).
     - No incident, fills within range -> resolve (normal market movement).
RESOLUTION:
  Explain market orders and slippage. Suggest (as product education, not advice) that limit
  orders let the customer control the maximum/minimum price. No refund of the difference unless a
  confirmed incident's compensation_policy says so (TRD-12).
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  I understand the price you got was different from what you saw, and I checked your order.
  Your market order for <qty> <coin> was filled in <n> parts at prices between Rs <min> and Rs <max>,
  giving an average of Rs <avg>. A market order buys/sells immediately at the best prices available
  in the order book at that moment; when prices move fast or the order is large, part of it can fill
  at the next available prices. The prices you received match the market on Acme Invest at that time.
  If you want to control the exact price, you can use a limit order, which only fills at your price
  or better.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
  (Incident variant: "We have identified a pricing issue for <coin> between <time> and <time> IST that
  may have affected your order. Our team is reviewing affected orders and we will update you within
  48 hours.")
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - LIMIT order filled worse than its limit -> Trading Ops, P1, include: order_id, limit, fills
  - Fills outside the market range or linked to a price_incidents row -> Trading Ops, P2, include:
    order_id, incident_id (if any), fills, price the customer saw
  - Customer threatens legal/regulatory action -> complaints_escalation_sop CMP-04
DO NOT:
  - Do not promise a refund or "price correction".
  - Do not call it "manipulation" or speculate about causes.

## TRD-05: Order placed but not visible / money deducted but no coins
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "Money got deducted from my INR wallet but I didn't get any BTC"
  - "I placed an order but it is not showing in my orders"
  - "Paisa kat gaya par coins nahi aaye"
  - "Bought SOL, INR balance reduced, holdings not updated"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Coin, amount, approximate time, screenshot of the order confirmation if any
TOOLS: get_orders, get_ledger_entries, check_wallet_balance, check_order
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Find orders near the reported time:
     Call get_orders(from_time=<reported_time - 2 hours>,
          to_time=<reported_time + 2 hours>).
     - No order found: check whether the INR went somewhere else (withdrawal, another asset):
       Call get_ledger_entries(from_time=<reported_time - 2 hours>).
       -> Debit with no matching order/withdrawal -> escalate Trading Ops (P1).
       -> No debit: the order was never placed (app/network error) -> app_technical_sop; tell the
          customer no money was deducted.
  3. Order found, branch on status:
     - OPEN / NEW / PARTIALLY_FILLED: INR is LOCKED for the order, not deducted. Check:
       Call check_wallet_balance().
       -> Explain the order is waiting to fill (TRD-02 / TRD-03).
     - FILLED: check trades and ledger:
       Call check_order(order_id=<order_id>). Then for each trade_id in fills:
       Call get_ledger_entries(reference_type="TRADE", reference_id=<trade_id>).
       -> TRADE_BUY credit of the coin present and check_wallet_balance shows the coin: coins are in
          the account; likely a display/refresh issue (TRD-07).
       -> INR debit present but coin credit missing -> escalate Trading Ops (P1).
     - CANCELLED / REJECTED / EXPIRED with filled_quantity = 0: funds should be back in available.
       If locked (check_wallet_balance) still holds the amount after 15 minutes (sample value) or a
       debit exists without a REVERSAL entry -> escalate Trading Ops (P1).
  4. The order exists but the customer cannot see it in the app: check the app filter (open vs
     history tab) and app version; if it is still missing on the latest app -> app_technical_sop.
RESOLUTION:
  Either (a) funds are locked for an open order, (b) coins were credited and are visible after
  refresh, (c) funds were released after cancel/reject, or (d) a genuine ledger gap, escalated P1.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Sorry for the worry. I checked your account: <"your order to buy <coin> worth Rs <x> placed at
  <time IST> is still open, so the amount is reserved (not deducted) until it fills or you cancel it"
  / "your order was completed and <qty> <coin> has been added to your holdings; please refresh the
  app or pull down on the portfolio screen" / "we found an issue with this order and have escalated
  it as urgent. You will hear from us within 24 hours">.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - INR debited but no coin credit, or debit without order/reversal -> Trading Ops, P1, include:
    user_id, order_id, entry_ids, wallet_balances snapshot
DO NOT:
  - Do not manually promise to "add the coins".
  - Do not tell the customer money is lost.

## TRD-06: Cancel failed / order filled after I cancelled
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "I cancelled my order but it still got executed"
  - "Cancel button not working, order got filled at a bad price"
  - "Maine cancel kiya tha phir bhi order execute ho gaya"
  - "Unable to cancel my limit order"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Order id or coin + time, the time they pressed cancel (IST), screenshot if any
TOOLS: check_order, get_instrument
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Call check_order(order_id=<order_id>). Check order.user_id is the customer's user_id.
     Use the order and its fills (executed_at).
  3. Compare fill times with the cancel time (order.updated_at for CANCELLED orders; for FILLED
     orders, use the time the customer reports):
     - status FILLED and all executed_at are BEFORE the reported cancel time: the order had already
       filled when the cancel was sent (race condition; cancel requests cannot stop a fill that has
       already happened). Resolve: explain; the trade is final (TRD-12).
     - status CANCELLED with filled_quantity > 0: the part filled before the cancel is final; the rest
       was cancelled. Resolve: explain (TRD-03).
     - Any executed_at is AFTER order.updated_at of a CANCELLED status, or AFTER the cancel time by
       more than 2 seconds (sample value) with evidence (screenshot) -> possible system issue ->
       escalate Trading Ops (P2).
     - status still OPEN and customer cannot cancel: Call get_instrument(symbol=<symbol>).
       Check status; if HALTED, cancels may be queued (TRD-08). If TRADING -> app_technical_sop;
       if it persists, escalate Tech On-call (P2).
RESOLUTION:
  Explain the race condition clearly, with times. If a genuine system fault is found, escalate;
  any outcome is decided by Trading Ops.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  I understand how frustrating this is. I checked the timing: your order was filled at <time IST>
  and the cancel request reached us at <time IST>, <x> seconds after the order had already been
  completed. Once an order is filled it cannot be cancelled, so the cancel had no effect.
  The trade was executed at the market price at that time.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Fills recorded after a confirmed cancel -> Trading Ops, P2, include: order_id, updated_at, executed_at list, customer screenshot
  - Cancel repeatedly failing on a TRADING instrument -> Tech On-call, P2
DO NOT:
  - Do not promise reversal of the filled quantity.

## TRD-07: Coins or portfolio value wrong / not updated
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "My portfolio value is showing wrong"
  - "BTC balance is less than what I bought"
  - "Holdings not updated after trade"
  - "Portfolio mein value kam dikh raha hai, coins gayab?"
  - "Why can't I sell all my coins, it says insufficient"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Which coin, what they expected vs what they see, screenshot
TOOLS: check_wallet_balance, get_orders, get_withdrawals, get_trades
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Call check_wallet_balance(asset=<asset>).
  3. Recompute from the ledger (source of truth): use ledger_total and matches_ledger from step 2.
     - matches_ledger is true (ledger_total = available + locked): balance is correct -> step 4.
     - matches_ledger is false -> escalate Trading Ops (P1), do not guess.
  4. Explain locked vs available:
     Call get_orders(statuses=["OPEN", "NEW", "PARTIALLY_FILLED"]).
     Call get_withdrawals(statuses=["REQUESTED", "PENDING_APPROVAL", "PROCESSING", "ON_HOLD"]).
     -> Locked coins are reserved for open SELL orders or pending withdrawals and cannot be sold
        again until released.
  5. Check fees and TDS explaining a smaller quantity:
     Call get_trades(). Look at the latest 20 trades.
     -> The coin quantity received on a buy is net of fees (sample: fee taken from the bought
        amount); on a sell, INR received is net of fee, GST and TDS (tax_statements_sop TAX-01).
  6. Portfolio VALUE (not quantity) wrong: value = quantity x latest price. Prices update
     continuously and may lag by a few seconds; values differ from other platforms. If prices are
     not loading at all -> app_technical_sop. If quantity is right, no action is needed.
RESOLUTION:
  Confirm the quantities from the ledger, explain locked amounts, fees/TDS and price-based value.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  I checked your <coin> holdings. You hold <total> <coin> in total: <available> is available and
  <locked> is reserved for <an open sell order / a pending withdrawal>. <"The quantity you received
  is slightly lower than the order amount because the trading fee is deducted from it."> Your
  portfolio value is calculated from the live market price, so it changes as the price moves.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Ledger sum does not match wallet_balances -> Trading Ops, P1, include: user_id, asset, ledger sum, balances
DO NOT:
  - Do not comment on whether the value will rise or fall.

## TRD-08: Instrument HALTED / DELISTED / POST_ONLY
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "Why can't I buy XYZ coin, it says trading paused"
  - "Coin delisted, what happens to my holdings?"
  - "Only limit orders allowed on this coin, market order option gone"
  - "Mera coin delist ho gaya, paisa wapas milega?"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Coin/pair
TOOLS: get_instrument, check_wallet_balance, get_price_incidents, get_supported_networks
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Call get_instrument(symbol=<symbol>).
     Call check_wallet_balance(asset=<asset>).
  3. Branch on status:
     - HALTED: trading is temporarily paused (maintenance, network upgrade, extreme volatility).
       Check for an incident: Call get_price_incidents(symbol=<symbol>).
       Open orders stay in the book but do not match until trading resumes; holdings are safe.
       -> Resolve: tell the customer trading will resume automatically; no fixed ETA unless
          announced. If halted for more than 24 hours (sample value) with no announcement ->
          escalate Trading Ops (P3) for an update.
     - POST_ONLY: only limit orders that add to the order book are accepted; market orders and
       limit orders that would match immediately are not accepted. Usually before full trading
       starts or resumes. -> Resolve: explain; customer can place limit orders.
     - DELISTED: the pair is no longer tradable on Acme. Holdings remain in the account.
       Check whether withdrawal of the coin is possible:
       Call get_supported_networks(asset=<asset>).
       -> withdrawals_enabled: customer can withdraw to their own external wallet before the
          announced deadline (crypto_transfers_sop). Otherwise follow the delisting announcement;
          escalate to Trading Ops (P3) for the customer's options.
     - TRADING: the coin is tradable now; the issue is elsewhere (TRD-01 / app_technical_sop).
RESOLUTION:
  Explain the status and what the customer can still do. Never say holdings are lost.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Trading in <coin> is <temporarily paused / currently limited to limit orders / no longer

  2. Call get_sip_plans().

     - SUCCESS: check the order: Call check_order(order_id=<order_id>).

## TRD-13: How to read average buy price and P&L
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "My average buy price is wrong"
  - "P&L shows loss but I am in profit"
  - "How is invested value calculated?"
  - "Avg price itna high kaise aaya?"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Coin, screenshot of the portfolio screen
TOOLS: get_trades, get_ledger_entries
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. List the customer's trades in the coin:
     Call get_trades(symbol=<symbol>).
  3. Recompute (sample method, weighted average cost):
     avg buy price = sum(price x quantity of buys) / sum(quantity of buys), recalculated only when
     buying; selling reduces quantity but does not change the average (sample rule). Fees and GST
     are shown separately and are not included in the average (sample rule).
     Worked example: buy 0.01 BTC at Rs 50,00,000 and 0.01 BTC at Rs 60,00,000 ->
     average = (50,000 + 60,000) / 0.02 = Rs 55,00,000.
     Unrealised P&L = (current price - average) x quantity held.
     Realised P&L on a sell = (sell price - average) x quantity sold.
  4. Also check non-trade credits that have zero cost (rewards, crypto deposits):
     Call get_ledger_entries(asset=<asset>, entry_types=["REWARD", "DEPOSIT"]).
     -> Coins deposited from outside or received as rewards may have no purchase price on Acme
        (sample: shown at zero/unknown cost), which changes the average and P&L.
  5. - Recomputed average matches the app: explain the method. Resolve.
     - Mismatch that deposits/rewards do not explain -> escalate Trading Ops (P3) with the calculation.
RESOLUTION:
  Explain the calculation with the customer's own numbers. For tax purposes, refer to the P&L
  statement (tax_statements_sop TAX-03); the app's P&L view is indicative.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Happy to explain. Your average buy price for <coin> is the weighted average of all your buys:
  you bought <qty1> at Rs <p1> and <qty2> at Rs <p2>, so the average is Rs <avg>. Selling does
  not change the average. Your profit or loss shown in the app is (current price - average price)
  x quantity held, so it moves with the live price. <"Coins you deposited from outside Acme Invest
  do not have a purchase price here, which affects the average.">
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Unexplained mismatch between trades and the displayed average -> Trading Ops, P3, include: trade list, recomputed values
DO NOT:
  - Do not tell the customer whether to book profit or loss.
  - Do not present the app P&L as a tax computation.
