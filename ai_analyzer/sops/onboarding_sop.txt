## ONB-00: Scope and quick triage
======================================================================
Covers everything from "I want to sign up" until the customer can log in and start KYC:
signup OTPs, email verification, already-registered errors, login and password problems,
temporary login blocks, new-device logins, duplicate accounts, referral codes at signup,
eligibility (NRI, foreign nationals, minors) and what a user can do before KYC.

Not covered here (route instead):
  - KYC documents, PAN, Aadhaar, selfie, rejections        -> kyc_sop
  - "My account was hacked" / login I did not do           -> account_security_sop SEC-01
  - 2FA reset, lost phone, change phone/email              -> account_security_sop SEC-02 to SEC-05
  - Account FROZEN                                         -> fraud_risk_sop RSK-01
  - Account DORMANT or CLOSED                              -> account_closure_sop CLS-04 / CLS-05
  - App crashes, blank screens, "something went wrong"     -> app_technical_sop

Sample onboarding policies used in this SOP (not in DD-09):
  - OTP validity: 10 minutes (sample value). Resend allowed after 60 seconds (sample value).
  - OTP rate limit: 5 OTP requests per destination per hour (sample value); after that,
    OTPs are paused for 1 hour (sample value).
  - Email verification link validity: 24 hours (sample value).
  - Password reset link validity: 30 minutes (sample value).
  - Login block: 5 failed attempts -> BLOCKED, auto-unblocks after 24 hours (DD-09 Security).
  - Eligibility: 18+ and Indian resident with a PAN linked to Aadhaar (DD-09 KYC).
  - One account per PAN (enforced at KYC with DUPLICATE_PAN).

Quick triage:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02). For signup problems there may be
     no account yet; search the OTP logs by destination instead (get_otp_logs).
  2. Check the user's status first:
     - BLOCKED -> ONB-05. FROZEN -> fraud_risk_sop RSK-01. DORMANT -> account_closure_sop
       CLS-04. CLOSED -> account_closure_sop CLS-05. ACTIVE -> continue below.
  3. Map the complaint:
     - "OTP not coming" on phone                        -> ONB-01
     - Email verification link / email OTP missing      -> ONB-02
     - "Already registered" at signup                   -> ONB-03
     - Wrong password / forgot password / cannot log in -> ONB-04
     - "Account blocked / too many attempts"            -> ONB-05
     - "New device" / "device not trusted"              -> ONB-06
     - Two accounts for one person                      -> ONB-07
     - Referral code missed at signup                   -> ONB-08 (rewards_sop RWD-03)
     - NRI / foreigner / minor                          -> ONB-09 / ONB-10
     - "What can I do before KYC?"                      -> ONB-11
     - Signed up with a wrong phone number or email     -> ONB-12

## ONB-01: Signup or login OTP not received on SMS
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "I am not getting the OTP on my mobile, tried 5 times"
  - "OTP nahi aa raha, signup stuck on verify number screen"
  - "SMS OTP is not coming, my number is on DND, is that the issue?"
  - "It says too many attempts, try later. But I never got a single OTP"
  - "Getting OTP for other apps but not for Acme"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Mobile number exactly as entered (with country code), approximate time of the attempt
  - Whether this is signup (new user) or login (existing user)
  - Mobile operator and whether the SIM is in the same phone as the app (optional)
  - Never ask for the OTP itself.
TOOLS: get_otp_logs, get_otp_delivery_health, get_auth_events
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02). Normalise the number to +91XXXXXXXXXX.
     If no account exists (signup in progress), continue with the number only.
  2. Look at the OTP attempts for that destination in the last 24 hours:
     Call get_otp_logs(destination=<phone>, channel="SMS", hours=24).
     - If no OTPs at all: the request never reached us. Common causes: number typed in a
       wrong format (missing digit, extra 0, +91 twice), or the app could not reach our
       servers. Search the same number without the country code / with a leading 0
       (the tool matches a phone on its last 10 digits):
       Call get_otp_logs(destination=<last_10_digits>, hours=24).
       -> If found under a malformed number: tell the customer to re-enter the 10-digit
          number without 0 or +91 (the app adds the country code). Resolve.
       -> If still nothing: ask the customer to update the app, check internet, and retry;
          if it fails again, route to app_technical_sop. Ask customer.
     - Else if the latest OTPs are DELIVERED or VERIFIED: the SMS reached the phone.
       -> If VERIFIED: the OTP was used successfully; ask what screen they are stuck on.
       -> If DELIVERED but not VERIFIED: ask them to check the SMS inbox / spam-SMS folder,
          and to type the latest OTP only (older OTPs stop working when a new one is sent).
          Resolve.
     - Else if status = EXPIRED: the OTP arrived but was entered after 10 minutes
       (sample value). Ask them to request a new one and enter it straight away. Resolve.
     - Else if status = FAILED: read provider_error (step 3).
     - Else if status = SENT for more than 5 minutes with no DELIVERED: operator delay.
       Ask the customer to wait 10 minutes (sample value) and retry once; offer the
       WhatsApp/email OTP option if the app shows it. Wait.
  3. For FAILED OTPs, check provider_error (sample values seen in logs):
     - DND / promotional block / "blocked by subscriber preference": OTP SMS are
       transactional and normally bypass DND, but some operator settings block them.
       -> Ask the customer to use the WhatsApp or email OTP option if offered, or to
          temporarily disable full DND with their operator (for example by messaging
          their operator's DND service). Ask customer.
     - Invalid number / unknown subscriber / "not a mobile number": the number is wrong or
       is a landline or inactive SIM. -> Ask them to confirm the number and that the SIM is
       active and can receive SMS. Ask customer.
     - Rate limited / throttled (or 5+ OTPs in the last hour): our rate limit
       (5 per hour, sample value) was hit. -> Ask them to wait 1 hour (sample value) and
       try once. Wait.
     - Handset unreachable / absent subscriber: phone off or no network. -> Ask them to
       retry with good network, SIM in the same phone. Ask customer.
     - Provider error on many unrelated numbers at the same time (check below): SMS
       provider outage.
       Call get_otp_delivery_health(channel="SMS", minutes=30). Look at failed and
       failed_destinations.
       -> If failures are spread across many numbers: escalate to Tech On-call (P2) and
          tell the customer we are aware of an SMS delay; retry in 1-2 hours. Wait.
  4. For an existing user, also check whether login is actually blocked:
     Call get_auth_events(). Look at the latest 20 events.
     - If failure_reason = ACCOUNT_BLOCKED or the user's status = BLOCKED: go to ONB-05.
RESOLUTION:
  Explain the likely cause in plain words and give one clear next step: re-enter the number,
  wait out the limit, use the alternative OTP channel, or wait for an SMS delay to clear.
  We cannot send an OTP manually or read one out.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Sorry you are having trouble receiving the OTP. I checked your recent attempts and
  <cause in plain words, e.g. "your mobile network was not accepting our SMS messages" /
  "several OTP requests were made in a short time, so new ones are paused for a while">.
  Please <next step, e.g. "wait 1 hour and then request a single new OTP" / "choose the
  option to receive the OTP on WhatsApp or email">. Always enter only the latest OTP you
  received; it is valid for 10 minutes.
  Please never share your OTP with anyone, including us.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - OTP failures across many numbers in the same period -> Tech On-call, P2, include:
    time window, count of failed OTPs, sample provider_error values.
  - Customer still cannot receive OTP after 24 hours and all steps above -> Tech On-call,
    P3, include: user_id (if any), destination, otp_ids, provider_error values.
DO NOT:
  - Do not ask for, read out or accept the OTP.
  - Do not name the SMS provider or show provider_error text.
  - Do not change the registered number from email (see account_security_sop SEC-04).

## ONB-02: Email verification link or email OTP not received
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "Verification mail not received, checked everywhere"
  - "The email verify link says expired"
  - "I think I typed my email wrong during signup, gmial instead of gmail"
  - "Email OTP is not coming, phone OTP came fine"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Registered phone number and the email address they intended to use
  - Approximate time of the attempt
TOOLS: find_user, get_otp_logs, get_notifications, get_otp_delivery_health
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02). Use the phone number because the
     email on file may be the mistyped one.
     Call find_user(phone=<phone>).
  2. Compare the account's email with the address the customer expects.
     - If it differs by a typo (gmial.com, gmail.co, missing letter, extra dot): the
       verification went to the wrong address -> ONB-12 (wrong email at signup).
     - Else continue.
  3. Check the email OTP / link attempts:
     Call get_otp_logs(channel="EMAIL", hours=48).
     Call get_otp_logs(destination=<email>, channel="EMAIL", hours=48).
     Call get_notifications(channel="EMAIL"). Look at the latest 10.
     - If DELIVERED / SENT: the email left us. Ask the customer to check Spam, Promotions,
       Updates and "All mail", search for "Acme Invest", and add our sender to contacts.
       Corporate mailboxes may quarantine it; ask them to check with their IT or use a
       personal email. Ask customer.
     - If EXPIRED or the customer says "link expired": links are valid for 24 hours
       (sample value). Ask them to tap "Resend verification email" in the app. Resolve.
     - If FAILED with a bounce-type provider_error (mailbox does not exist, domain not
       found): the address is invalid -> ONB-12.
     - If FAILED with mailbox full / temporarily rejected: ask the customer to free space or
       wait 1 hour and resend. Wait.
     - If nothing found: the resend was never triggered. Ask them to update the app and tap
       resend once. Ask customer.
  4. Call get_otp_delivery_health(channel="EMAIL", minutes=30). If many EMAIL OTPs FAILED
     across different users in the last 30 minutes, treat it as an email delivery outage ->
     escalate Tech On-call (P2).
RESOLUTION:
  Most cases are spam-folder or typo cases. Guide the customer to resend, check folders, or
  correct the email through the in-app flow.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Thanks for letting us know that the verification email has not reached you. I checked,
  and <finding, e.g. "our email was sent to <masked email> at 10:42 AM IST">.
  Please check your Spam, Promotions and Updates folders and search for "Acme Invest". If
  the link has expired, tap "Resend verification email" in the app; each link is valid for
  24 hours.
  If you still cannot find it, reply to this email and we will look into it further.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Email delivery failing for many users -> Tech On-call, P2, include: time window,
    count of failures, template names.
  - Resend works for others but repeatedly fails for this address for 24 hours ->
    Tech On-call, P3, include: user_id, destination (masked), otp_ids.
DO NOT:
  - Do not change the email on file based on an email request (account_security_sop SEC-05).
  - Do not show the full email address on file to an unverified sender; mask it
    (e.g. ra****@gmail.com).

## ONB-03: "Phone number / email already registered" at signup
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "Trying to sign up but it says this number is already registered. I never made an account"
  - "Email already in use error, but I am a new user"
  - "My old number was with someone else earlier, now I can't register"
  - "I think I made an account 2 years back and forgot"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - The phone number or email that shows as registered
  - Whether they may have signed up before (even partially)
TOOLS: find_user
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02). Do not confirm to an unverified
     sender that an account exists for a number/email they do not own (SUP-01 rule 3).
  2. Find the existing account:
     Call find_user(email=<email>, phone=<phone>).
     - If found AND the sender's email matches the account's email: it is their own old account.
       -> Tell them they already have an account and should use "Log in" / "Forgot
          password" (ONB-04). If status DORMANT -> account_closure_sop CLS-04. If CLOSED ->
          account_closure_sop CLS-05. Resolve.
     - Else if found, phone matches but email differs, and kyc_status is NOT_STARTED with
       last_login_at long ago or null: likely a recycled mobile number (the previous owner
       signed up and never completed KYC), or the customer's own abandoned signup.
       -> Ask the customer to try "Log in" with the number; login is by OTP to that number,
          so if they own the SIM they can get in and then update the email in-app
          (account_security_sop SEC-05). Ask customer.
     - Else if found, phone matches, and the account is VERIFIED with a different KYC name
       than the customer: recycled number belonging to another verified person.
       -> Do not share any details. Escalate to Support Operations/KYC Ops (P3) to handle
          the number release; ask the customer to sign up with a different number meanwhile.
     - Else if email matches another user and the phone differs: ask the customer to log in
       with the phone they used earlier, or sign up with a different email. Ask customer.
  3. Check whether the customer is trying to create a second account on purpose
     (e.g. "I want a separate account for my trading"). -> Explain one account per
     person/PAN (ONB-07). Resolve.
RESOLUTION:
  Either guide the customer into their existing account, or have them use a different
  number/email. Recycled-number cases on verified accounts are handled internally.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Thanks for reaching out about the "already registered" message.
  <If own account:> It looks like an account was created earlier using your details. Please
  tap "Log in" instead of "Sign up" and use your registered mobile number. If you do not
  remember your password, use "Forgot password" on the login screen.
  <If not theirs / cannot confirm:> For privacy reasons we cannot share details about the
  number or email you mentioned. Please sign up with a different mobile number or email for
  now. If you believe the number has been reassigned to you by your operator, reply to let
  us know and our team will review it within 5 working days.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Recycled number on a VERIFIED account belonging to someone else -> KYC Ops, P3,
    include: both user_ids (internal note only), phone, created_at dates.
  - Customer claims someone else registered with their email or number fraudulently ->
    account_security_sop SEC-01 checks, Risk & Compliance, P2.
DO NOT:
  - Do not reveal the name, KYC status or email of the account holding the number.
  - Do not delete or free up a phone/email on another user's account yourself.

## ONB-04: Cannot log in / wrong password / forgot password
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "Password is correct but it says invalid credentials"
  - "I forgot my password, reset link not coming"
  - "Login nahi ho raha, bar bar wrong password bol raha hai"
  - "Reset password link expired before I could open it"
  - "Changed password yesterday, now withdrawal is not allowed"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Registered phone/email, platform (Android/iOS/web), app version if known
  - Exact error message or screenshot
TOOLS: get_auth_events, get_otp_logs
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02) and check the user's status.
     - BLOCKED -> ONB-05. FROZEN -> fraud_risk_sop RSK-01. DORMANT -> account_closure_sop
       CLS-04. CLOSED -> account_closure_sop CLS-05.
  2. Look at recent login activity:
     Call get_auth_events(days=7). Look at the latest 30 events.
     - If LOGIN_FAILED with WRONG_PASSWORD: the password entered does not match. Count
       them in the last 24h. If 3-4 failures, warn that 5 failures block login for 24 hours
       and advise "Forgot password" now. Resolve.
     - If LOGIN_FAILED with WRONG_OTP or OTP_EXPIRED: it is an OTP problem -> ONB-01.
     - If LOGIN_FAILED with DEVICE_NOT_TRUSTED -> ONB-06.
     - If LOGIN_FAILED with ACCOUNT_BLOCKED -> ONB-05.
     - If no LOGIN_FAILED events at the time the customer mentions: the request did not
       reach us (network or app issue) -> ask them to update the app and retry; if the
       app shows an error screen, route to app_technical_sop. Ask customer.
     - If LOGIN_SUCCESS events from a device or city the customer does not recognise ->
       account_security_sop SEC-01 immediately.
  3. For "reset link / OTP not coming", check the reset attempts:
     Call get_otp_logs(hours=24).
     - DELIVERED but not used: ask them to check spam/SMS inbox and use the latest link
       within 30 minutes (sample value). Resolve.
     - FAILED / none: follow ONB-01 (SMS) or ONB-02 (email) branches.
  4. After a successful reset, check for PASSWORD_RESET in the get_auth_events output. If the customer
     complains that withdrawals are disabled: this is the 24-hour withdrawal cooldown after
     a password reset (DD-09). Tell them the time it ends (created_at of PASSWORD_RESET
     + 24 hours, in IST). Resolve.
RESOLUTION:
  Guide the customer to reset the password themselves through "Forgot password" (OTP to
  the registered phone/email, then a new password). Support cannot set or tell a password.
  For password hygiene see account_security_sop SEC-10.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Sorry you are having trouble logging in. I checked your account and <finding, e.g. "the
  last few attempts were made with an incorrect password">.
  To get back in, please:
  - Tap "Forgot password" on the login screen
  - Enter the OTP sent to your registered mobile number or email
  - Set a new password (at least 8 characters, with letters and numbers)
  Note: 5 wrong attempts in a row pause login for 24 hours, so please reset rather than
  guess. After a password reset, withdrawals are paused for 24 hours for your safety.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Correct OTP and reset succeed but login still fails with no failure recorded ->
    Tech On-call, P2, include: user_id, device_id, platform, app_version, timestamps.
  - Signs of someone else logging in -> account_security_sop SEC-01 (P1).
DO NOT:
  - Do not ask for the current or old password, or the OTP.
  - Do not set, reset or email a password on the customer's behalf.
  - Do not lift the post-reset withdrawal cooldown.

## ONB-05: Account BLOCKED after 5 failed login attempts
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "My account is blocked, it says too many incorrect attempts"
  - "Account locked for 24 hours, please unblock urgently, I need to sell"
  - "Mera account block ho gaya, kab khulega?"
  - "I didn't try to login 5 times, why is it blocked?"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Registered phone/email; whether all the failed attempts were theirs
TOOLS: get_user, get_auth_events, get_user_devices
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
     Call get_user(). Note status and last_login_at.
     - If status = FROZEN, not BLOCKED: this is a risk freeze, not a login block ->
       fraud_risk_sop RSK-01. Do not describe it as a login block.
     - If status = ACTIVE: the block has already been lifted. Ask them to try again,
       using "Forgot password" if unsure of the password. Resolve.
     - If status = BLOCKED: continue.
  2. Find when the block started and who made the attempts:
     Call get_auth_events(days=2).
     The block time is the first LOGIN_FAILED with failure_reason = ACCOUNT_BLOCKED, or
     the 5th consecutive WRONG_PASSWORD. Unblock time = block time + 24 hours (DD-09).
  3. Compare the failed attempts' device_id with the customer's known devices:
     Call get_user_devices().
     - If all failures come from the customer's own trusted device(s): customer mistyped.
       -> Tell them the unblock time in IST and to use "Forgot password" once it unblocks.
          Wait (until unblock time).
     - Else if failures come from unknown devices or many different device_ids, and the
       customer says they did not try: possible password-guessing attack.
       -> Keep the block (it protects the account). Follow account_security_sop SEC-01
          checks; advise changing the password after unblock and enabling 2FA (SEC-02).
          If any LOGIN_SUCCESS from an unknown device exists -> SEC-01, P1.
  4. If the block time + 24 hours has passed and status is still BLOCKED: auto-unblock did
     not run -> escalate Tech On-call (P2).
RESOLUTION:
  The block is automatic and lifts by itself 24 hours after it started. Support does not
  unblock early, including for urgent trades; market movements during the block are not
  compensated.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  I understand how frustrating it is to be locked out. For your security, login is paused
  for 24 hours after 5 incorrect attempts. Your account will be available again from
  <unblock time, e.g. 28 Sep 2026, 11:15 AM IST>, automatically; you do not need to do
  anything else.
  When it opens, please use "Forgot password" to set a new password rather than trying
  again from memory. We also recommend turning on two-factor authentication in
  Settings > Security.
  <If attempts were not theirs:> Since you did not make these attempts, please change your
  password as soon as the account opens. We have also reviewed the login activity.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Still BLOCKED more than 24 hours after the block started -> Tech On-call, P2, include:
    user_id, block timestamp, current status.
  - Failed attempts from unknown devices plus any successful login, order or withdrawal the
    customer does not recognise -> Risk & Compliance, P1 (account_security_sop SEC-01).
DO NOT:
  - Do not promise or perform an early unblock.
  - Do not share IP addresses or device identifiers from the failed attempts.
  - Do not call a FROZEN account "blocked" or give it a 24-hour time frame.

## ONB-06: New device login / "device not trusted"
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "Got a new phone, now login says device not recognised"
  - "It keeps asking for OTP every time on my laptop"
  - "Changed my phone and now withdrawals are disabled"
  - "Device not trusted error after reinstalling the app"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Registered phone/email, new device platform (Android/iOS/web), whether they still have
    the old device and the registered SIM
TOOLS: get_user_devices, get_auth_events
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. List devices:
     Call get_user_devices().
  3. Check the recent login attempts from the new device:
     Call get_auth_events(days=3).
     - If DEVICE_NOT_TRUSTED and no OTP_VERIFIED on that device: the new device must be
       confirmed with an OTP to the registered phone (and 2FA if enabled). Ask the customer
       to complete the in-app device verification. If OTP does not arrive -> ONB-01.
     - If the customer no longer has the registered SIM or the 2FA app: they cannot
       verify the device -> account_security_sop SEC-03 (lost phone / 2FA reset).
     - If OTP_VERIFIED exists but is_trusted is still false after 24 hours: sample policy
       is that a new device becomes trusted after successful verification plus 24 hours
       (sample value); until then withdrawals may be restricted. Tell them the time. Wait.
     - If a new device appears that the customer does not recognise ->
       account_security_sop SEC-01 / SEC-08.
  4. Web logins that "always ask OTP": browsers in private mode or with cookies cleared are
     seen as a new device every time. Ask them to use a normal browser window. Resolve.
RESOLUTION:
  The customer verifies the new device themselves through OTP (and 2FA). Support cannot mark
  a device as trusted.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Thanks for writing in. When you log in from a new phone or browser, we ask you to confirm
  it with an OTP on your registered mobile number to keep your account safe.
  Please open the app on the new device, log in, and enter the OTP when asked. <If
  applicable:> For your safety, some actions such as withdrawals may stay paused on a new
  device for up to 24 hours after verification.
  If you no longer have access to your registered number, reply and we will guide you
  through our secure recovery process.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Device verified correctly but login keeps failing with DEVICE_NOT_TRUSTED for over 24
    hours -> Tech On-call, P2, include: user_id, device_id, platform, app_version, events.
DO NOT:
  - Do not mark a device trusted or bypass OTP/2FA on request.
  - Do not share device IDs with the customer.

## ONB-07: Duplicate accounts for the same person
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "I have two Acme accounts, one on my old number and one on new number"
  - "KYC rejected saying PAN already used, but that is my own other account"
  - "Can I merge my two accounts? Balance is in the old one"
  - "Can I open a second account for my wife using my PAN?"
  - "Want separate accounts for long-term and trading"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Registered phone/email of BOTH accounts (from the registered email of at least one)
  - Which account they want to keep
TOOLS: find_user, get_kyc_applications, check_wallet_balance, get_risk_holds
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02) for the sender. Then find the other
     account the customer mentions:
     Call find_user(email=<other_email>, phone=<other_phone>).
     - If the customer cannot prove control of the other account (the sender email is not
       on either account and they cannot verify per SUP-01): ask them to write from the
       registered email of the account concerned. Ask customer.
  2. Policy: one account per PAN. Check KYC on both accounts: this account with
     Call get_kyc_applications().
     and the other account from the kyc_status in find_user's other_accounts (the tools only
     show this email's own account in detail; never ask for or share more about the other one).
     - Case A: one account VERIFIED, the other REJECTED with DUPLICATE_PAN (or not
       started). -> The VERIFIED account is the one to use. The second account cannot be
       verified. Offer to close the unverified one (account_closure_sop CLS-01). Resolve.
     - Case B: the customer wants to keep the NEW (unverified) account, e.g. because the old

       customer to write from the old account's registered email, or escalate KYC Ops, P3,
       to check them.
       -> If balance exists: they must log in and withdraw first (they may need
          account_security_sop SEC-04 to regain access to the old account). Ask customer.
       -> If zero balance, no open orders, no active holds: raise a closure of the old
          account (account_closure_sop CLS-01); after closure is COMPLETED, KYC Ops
          releases the PAN and the customer re-submits KYC on the new account. Escalate KYC
          Ops, P3.
     - Case C: two VERIFIED accounts with the same PAN (should not happen). -> Do not act;
       escalate to KYC Ops and Risk & Compliance, P2.
     - Case D: accounts under different PANs but the same person (e.g. customer admits to
       holding two PANs): possible legal issue -> escalate Risk & Compliance, P2, reply
       generically.
  3. Merging: accounts cannot be merged and balances cannot be moved between accounts
     internally. The customer withdraws INR to their own verified bank account or crypto
     to their own address, then deposits into the account they keep (withdrawal_sop,
     crypto_transfers_sop).
  4. Check active holds before offering closure:
     Call get_risk_holds(active_only=true).
     - If any: do not proceed; route per account_closure_sop CLS-03.
     - Holds on the other account are checked when its closure is raised from that account
       (account_closure_sop CLS-01 checks them first).
  5. Family members: each person needs their own account with their own PAN, phone and
     email. A PAN cannot be shared.
RESOLUTION:

  if the customer wants the new account, the old one is closed first and KYC Ops releases
  the PAN. No merging, no internal transfers.
