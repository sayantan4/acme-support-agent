## CLS-00: Scope and quick triage
======================================================================
Covers requests to close an account, closures that are blocked, dormant accounts and their
reactivation, customers writing about an already CLOSED account, data deletion requests,
cancelling a closure request, and closure for a deceased customer.

Closure policy (sample values unless DD-09 is cited):
  - Only the account holder can request closure, from the registered email or in the app
    (Settings > Account > Close account, sample flow).
  - Preconditions: zero balance in ALL assets (INR and every crypto, available and locked),
    no open orders, no ACTIVE SIPs, no pending deposits/withdrawals, and no ACTIVE risk hold.
  - Dust: crypto balances worth less than Rs 100 (sample value; equal to the minimum order
    value in DD-09) that cannot be sold or withdrawn may be written off with the
    customer's written consent in the closure request (sample policy).
  - Processing: closure completes within 7 working days after all preconditions are met
    (sample value).
  - A CLOSED account cannot be reopened (sample policy). The customer may sign up again;
    KYC Ops releases the PAN from the closed account on request (onboarding_sop ONB-07,
    kyc_sop KYC-12).
  - Retention: KYC and transaction records are retained for 5 years (sample value) after
    closure as required by law; other personal data is deleted (CLS-06).
  - DORMANT = no login for 24 months (DD-02).

account_closure_requests.status:
  REQUESTED           received, preconditions being checked
  BLOCKED_BY_BALANCE  balance or open orders/SIPs/pending transactions remain -> CLS-02
  BLOCKED_BY_HOLD     an active risk hold exists -> CLS-03 (never reveal)
  IN_PROGRESS         preconditions met, closure being processed
  COMPLETED           account CLOSED (users.status = CLOSED)
  WITHDRAWN           customer cancelled the request -> CLS-07

Quick triage:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02). Closure requests are accepted only
     from the registered email (SUP-01); otherwise ask them to use the app or write from
     the registered email.
  2. Route by users.status and request:
     - ACTIVE, "close my account"                        -> CLS-01
     - Existing request BLOCKED_BY_BALANCE               -> CLS-02
     - Existing request BLOCKED_BY_HOLD / status FROZEN  -> CLS-03
     - DORMANT                                           -> CLS-04
     - CLOSED                                            -> CLS-05
     - "Delete my data"                                  -> CLS-06
     - "Cancel my closure request"                       -> CLS-07
     - Account holder has died                           -> CLS-08
     - "Where is my closure request?"                    -> CLS-09
  3. If the customer wants to close because of a complaint (hack, lost money, bad
     experience), handle that issue first under its SOP (e.g. account_security_sop SEC-01,
     complaints_escalation_sop), then offer closure.

## CLS-01: Close my account
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "Please close my Acme Invest account permanently"
  - "I don't want to use crypto anymore, delete my account"
  - "Mera account band kar do"
  - "How do I deactivate my account?"
  - "I want to close this account and keep my other one"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Request from the registered email (or registered phone + last 4 digits of bank account
    per support_guidelines_sop SUP-01); reason for closing (optional)
  - Confirmation that they have withdrawn all funds, and consent to write off dust if any
TOOLS: get_user, get_closure_requests, check_wallet_balance, get_orders, get_sip_plans, get_deposits, get_withdrawals, get_crypto_deposits, get_risk_holds
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
     Call get_user(). Check status and kyc_status.
     - FROZEN -> CLS-03. DORMANT -> CLS-04 (reactivate first to withdraw). CLOSED -> CLS-05.
  2. Existing requests:
     Call get_closure_requests().
     - If an open request exists (REQUESTED / BLOCKED_* / IN_PROGRESS) -> CLS-09 (status).
  3. Balances:
     Call check_wallet_balance(). Look at assets with available > 0 or
     locked > 0.
     - Any such asset -> blocked by balance (step 7, then CLS-02).
  4. Open orders, active SIPs and pending money movement:
     Call get_orders(statuses=["NEW", "OPEN", "PARTIALLY_FILLED"]).
     Call get_sip_plans(status="ACTIVE").
     Call get_deposits(statuses=["INITIATED", "PENDING", "ON_HOLD", "REFUND_INITIATED"]).
     Call get_withdrawals(statuses=["REQUESTED", "PENDING_APPROVAL", "PROCESSING", "ON_HOLD"]).
     Call get_crypto_deposits(statuses=["DETECTED", "CONFIRMING", "MANUAL_REVIEW"]).
  5. Holds:
     Call get_risk_holds(active_only=true).
     - Any hold -> CLS-03 (do not tell the customer why).
  6. Branches:
     - All clear (no balances, orders, SIPs, pending items, holds): raise the closure
       request with status REQUESTED (via the ticket to Support Operations, or ask the
       customer to confirm in-app). Tell them it completes within 7 working days
       (sample value). Resolve.
     - Only dust remains (every asset worth under Rs 100, sample value): ask for written
       consent to write off, then raise the request. Ask customer.
     - Balances/orders/SIPs/pending items -> CLS-02 guidance.
  7. Remind the customer to download statements and tax reports first
     (tax_statements_sop); after closure they can only be requested from support (CLS-05).
RESOLUTION:
  Once preconditions are met, Support Operations processes the closure; users.status becomes
  CLOSED and account_closure_requests.status COMPLETED.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  We are sorry to see you go, and thank you for being with Acme Invest.
  <All clear:> I have checked your account: there are no balances, open orders or pending
  transactions. We have started the closure, and it will be completed within 7 working days.
  We will confirm by email once it is done.
  <Not clear:> Before we can close your account, please: <list, e.g. withdraw your INR
  balance, sell or withdraw your crypto, cancel your open orders and SIPs>. Reply to this
  email once done, and we will close the account.
  Before closure, please download your account statements and tax reports from the app;
  you may need them for your income-tax filing.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Preconditions met but request not moved to IN_PROGRESS within 2 working days (sample
    value) -> Support Operations, P4, include: user_id, request_id.
  - Customer wants to close due to fraud/hack -> account_security_sop SEC-01 first (P1).
DO NOT:
  - Do not close an account with any balance, open order, pending transaction or hold.
  - Do not accept closure requests from someone other than the account holder.
  - Do not try to talk the customer out of closing more than once.

## CLS-02: Closure blocked by balance, open orders or pending transactions
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "Closure request shows blocked, what is pending?"
  - "I have Rs 3 left in my account, how do I withdraw that for closing?"
  - "Tiny amount of SHIB is left, it can't be sold, now what?"
  - "Aapne bola balance hai, par mera sab withdraw ho gaya"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Registered email/phone; consent to write off dust (if applicable)
TOOLS: get_closure_requests, check_wallet_balance, get_orders, get_sip_plans, get_deposits, get_withdrawals, get_crypto_deposits, get_supported_networks, get_instrument
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
     Call get_closure_requests(). Use the latest (first) request.
  2. Re-run CLS-01 steps 3-4 to list what is left:
     Call check_wallet_balance().
     Call get_orders(statuses=["NEW", "OPEN", "PARTIALLY_FILLED"]).
     Call get_sip_plans(status="ACTIVE").
     Call get_deposits(statuses=["INITIATED", "PENDING", "ON_HOLD", "REFUND_INITIATED"]).
     Call get_withdrawals(statuses=["REQUESTED", "PENDING_APPROVAL", "PROCESSING", "ON_HOLD"]).
     Call get_crypto_deposits(statuses=["DETECTED", "CONFIRMING", "MANUAL_REVIEW"]).
     - INR available > 0 and >= Rs 100: withdraw to the verified bank account (min Rs 100,
       fee Rs 10, DD-09; withdrawal_sop). Ask customer.
     - INR available < Rs 100 (cannot be withdrawn, DD-09 minimum): dust -> consent to
       write off. Ask customer.
     - Crypto available: sell to INR (min order value Rs 100, DD-09) or withdraw to own
       wallet (check min_withdrawal):
       Call get_supported_networks(asset=<asset>).
       -> If the amount is below both the min order value and min_withdrawal: dust ->
          consent to write off. Ask customer.
       -> If the instrument is HALTED or DELISTED (Call get_instrument(symbol=<symbol>),
          check status): trade_sop /
          crypto_transfers_sop for options; closure waits. Wait.
     - locked > 0: open orders or pending withdrawals hold the funds. Cancel open orders
       in-app; pending withdrawals complete within their SLA (24 hours INR, DD-09). Wait.
     - ACTIVE SIPs: cancel in-app (trade_sop). Ask customer.
     - Pending deposits/refunds: wait for them to complete per payment_sop (refund to
       source 5-7 working days, DD-09). Wait.
  3. When everything is cleared, the request moves forward automatically on the next check
     (sample: daily) or the customer replies to confirm. If still BLOCKED_BY_BALANCE with
     zero balances for 2 working days (sample value) -> escalate Support Operations, P4.
RESOLUTION:
  Customer clears balances/orders/SIPs; dust is written off with consent; closure proceeds.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Thanks for your patience. Your account closure is on hold because a few items are still
  open:
  - <item 1, e.g. "0.0004 ETH in your wallet">
  - <item 2, e.g. "one open limit order for BTC">
  <How to clear each.> <If dust:> Some of the remaining amounts are too small to sell or
  withdraw. If you are happy for these to be written off, please reply "I agree to write
  off the small balances", and we will continue with the closure.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Balances show zero but request stays BLOCKED_BY_BALANCE beyond 2 working days ->
    Support Operations, P4, include: request_id, balance query result.
  - Ledger and wallet_balances disagree -> Payments Ops, P2, include: user_id, asset,
    both values.
DO NOT:
  - Do not write off any balance without the customer's written consent.
  - Do not write off balances worth Rs 100 or more (sample value).

## CLS-03: Closure blocked by an active hold (never reveal the reason)
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "Why can't you close my account? I have withdrawn everything"
  - "My account is frozen, I just want to close it and take my money"
  - "Closure pending for weeks, what is the hold?"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Registered email/phone only
TOOLS: get_user, get_risk_holds, get_closure_requests
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
     Call get_user(). Note status.
     Call get_risk_holds(active_only=true).
     Call get_closure_requests(). Use the latest (first) request.
  2. Branches:
     - Any ACTIVE hold (or the user's status = FROZEN): closure cannot proceed until Risk &
       Compliance releases it. Follow fraud_risk_sop RSK-01. Do not share hold_type,
       reason_code or source.
       -> If source = LAW_ENFORCEMENT: do not confirm any external request exists; use the
          same generic reply.
       -> Escalate Risk & Compliance, P3 (P2 if the customer has funds they cannot access),
          noting that a closure is requested.
     - No ACTIVE hold but request BLOCKED_BY_HOLD: stale status -> escalate Support
       Operations, P4 to re-check; tell the customer we are reviewing it.
RESOLUTION:
  Closure waits for the review to complete; Risk & Compliance decides. Support shares only
  that the account is under review.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Thank you for your request to close your account. Your account is currently under a
  routine review, and we are unable to close it until that review is complete. I have
  informed the relevant team of your closure request, and we will update you as soon as
  there is progress.
  We are sorry that we cannot share more details at this stage.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - ACTIVE hold with a closure request -> Risk & Compliance, P3 (P2 if funds blocked),
    include: user_id, request_id, hold_id (internal only).
  - Customer threatens legal/regulatory action -> complaints_escalation_sop CMP-04.
DO NOT:
  - Never disclose the hold type, reason, source, or that any agency is involved.
  - Do not suggest the hold will lift by a date.
  - Do not close the account to "get around" the hold.

## CLS-04: Dormant account - reactivation
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "Haven't used my Acme account for 2-3 years, now it says dormant"
  - "Account inactive, how do I reactivate?"
  - "I have old BTC in my account, is it still there?"
  - "Purana account chalu karna hai, login nahi ho raha"
  - "My number changed since I last used the account"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Registered email/phone (or phone + last 4 of bank account); whether they still have
    the registered number and email
TOOLS: get_user, check_wallet_balance, get_risk_holds, get_kyc_applications, get_bank_accounts
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
     Call get_user(). Note status, kyc_status and last_login_at.
     - If status is not DORMANT: route per its status (ACTIVE -> onboarding_sop ONB-04 for
       login; BLOCKED -> ONB-05; FROZEN -> fraud_risk_sop RSK-01; CLOSED -> CLS-05).
  2. Balances are kept safe while dormant; confirm existence at summary level only to a
     verified sender:
     Call check_wallet_balance(). Look at assets with available > 0 or
     locked > 0.
  3. Holds:
     Call get_risk_holds(active_only=true).
     - Any -> fraud_risk_sop RSK-01 before reactivation.
  4. Reactivation path (sample flow):
     a. Customer logs in with OTP to the registered number. If they no longer have the
        number -> account_security_sop SEC-04 (change number via registered email +
        selfie) or SEC-03.
     b. App shows "Reactivate account" -> fresh selfie verification.
     c. KYC check: if kyc_status = RE_KYC_REQUIRED (or VERIFIED more than 2 years ago,
        sample value), the app asks for re-KYC -> kyc_sop KYC-16.
        Call get_kyc_applications(). Use the latest (first) application's
        status and reviewed_at.
     d. Bank account: check it is still VERIFIED; if the bank account was closed, add a new
        one (bank_account_sop).
        Call get_bank_accounts(status="VERIFIED").
     e. Reactivation review completes within 48 hours (sample value); the user's status
        returns to ACTIVE.
  5. Branches:
     - Reactivation submitted < 48 hours ago: Wait.
     - Submitted >= 48 hours ago and still DORMANT: escalate KYC Ops, P3.
     - Customer only wants to withdraw and close: they still need to reactivate first,
       then CLS-01.
RESOLUTION:
  Customer reactivates in-app (OTP + selfie, re-KYC if required, bank re-check). Holdings
  are unchanged by dormancy.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Welcome back! Your account was marked inactive because it had not been used for a long
  time. Your holdings stay safe while an account is inactive.
  To reactivate it:
  - Log in with your registered mobile number and follow the "Reactivate account" steps
  - Take a quick selfie, and complete the updated verification if the app asks for it
  - Check that your bank account is still active in the Bank accounts section
  Reactivation is usually reviewed within 48 hours. If you no longer use your registered
  mobile number, please reply and we will guide you through updating it securely.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Reactivation pending beyond 48 hours -> KYC Ops, P3, include: user_id, submission time.
  - Balance appears different from what the customer expects -> Payments Ops / Crypto Ops,
    P2, include: user_id, asset, ledger summary.
DO NOT:
  - Do not reactivate manually or skip the selfie / re-KYC.
  - Do not share exact balances with an unverified sender.

## CLS-05: Customer writes about a CLOSED account
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "I closed my account last year, can I reopen it?"
  - "Found some crypto left in my old closed account"
  - "Need my old transaction statement for tax, my account is closed"
  - "Got a refund after my account was closed, where did it go?"
  - "Signing up again but it says PAN already used"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Registered email of the closed account (or phone + last 4 of bank account); what they
    need (reopen, leftover balance, statements, refund)
TOOLS: get_user, get_closure_requests, get_ledger_entries, check_wallet_balance, get_refunds, get_tax_records
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
     Call get_user(). Note status.
     Call get_closure_requests(). Use the latest (first) request.
  2. Branch by what the customer needs:
     a. Reopen: closed accounts cannot be reopened (sample policy). The customer can sign
        up again with KYC; because the PAN is linked to the closed account, KYC Ops
        releases it on request -> escalate KYC Ops, P3 (see kyc_sop KYC-12,
        onboarding_sop ONB-07). If the account was closed while a hold existed or by
        Acme (not by the customer) -> Risk & Compliance decides, P3; reply generically.
     b. Leftover balance / credit after closure:
        Call get_ledger_entries(from_time=<completed_at>) (the closure
        request's completed_at).
        Call check_wallet_balance(). Look at assets with available > 0 or
        locked > 0.
        -> Any credit after closure (refund, reward, airdrop, late deposit): money belongs
           to the customer; escalate Payments Ops (INR) or Crypto Ops (crypto), P2, to
           return it to the verified bank account / customer's address.
        -> No balance: explain the account was closed with zero balance. Resolve.
     c. Refund landed after closure:
        Call get_refunds().
        -> PROCESSED with refund_utr: share the UTR so they can trace it at their bank.
           Resolve. FAILED: escalate Payments Ops, P2.
     d. Old statements / tax documents: records are retained 5 years (sample value).
        Call get_tax_records().
        -> Within retention: raise a statement request to be sent to the registered email
           (tax_statements_sop); P3, 5 working days. Beyond retention: not available.
  3. If the sender is not verified: do not confirm the account existed; ask for
     verification (support_guidelines_sop SUP-01).
RESOLUTION:
  Reopen is not possible (new signup with PAN release instead); leftover funds are returned
  via Ops; statements are sent to the registered email within the retention period.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Thanks for getting in touch about your closed account.
  <Reopen:> A closed account cannot be reopened, but you are welcome to sign up again. As
  your PAN is linked to the old account, our team will release it so you can complete
  verification on the new one. Please reply once you have signed up, with the new mobile
  number you used.
  <Leftover funds:> I found <amount/asset> credited after your account was closed. Our team
  will return it to your verified bank account (or wallet) and update you within 48 hours.
  <Statements:> We will email your statement for <period> to your registered email within
  5 working days.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Credits after closure -> Payments Ops (INR) or Crypto Ops (crypto), P2, include:
    user_id, ledger entry_ids, amounts.
  - PAN release for re-signup -> KYC Ops, P3, include: closed user_id, new user_id.
  - Statement request -> per tax_statements_sop, P3.
DO NOT:
  - Do not reopen a closed account or change its status.
  - Do not send statements to an address other than the registered email.

## CLS-06: Data deletion - "delete my data"
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "Delete all my personal data from your servers"
  - "I want my Aadhaar and PAN details removed"
  - "Under data protection law I request erasure of my data"
  - "Stop sending me marketing messages and delete my number"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Request from the registered email; whether they also want the account closed
TOOLS: none
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02). Only the account holder can ask.
  2. Branches:
     - Only wants marketing to stop: guide to Settings > Notifications to turn off marketing
       (sample flow); transactional and security messages continue while the account is
       open. Resolve.
     - Wants data deleted and account is ACTIVE/DORMANT: data deletion requires closure
       first -> CLS-01 (same preconditions). After closure, the deletion runs.
     - Account CLOSED: raise the deletion request. Sample policy:
       -> Retained for 5 years (sample value) after closure, as required by law: KYC
          records (PAN, Aadhaar details, selfie), transaction, ledger and tax records,
          and communications linked to transactions.
       -> Deleted within 30 days (sample value): marketing preferences, device data,
          app analytics, non-transaction support history, profile extras.
       -> Escalate Support Operations, P4 (Legal if the customer disputes retention).
     - ACTIVE risk hold or FROZEN: deletion cannot start -> CLS-03 handling (do not
       reveal why), Risk & Compliance.
  3. If the message cites a specific law, regulator or threatens complaint ->
     complaints_escalation_sop CMP-04 (holding reply only).
RESOLUTION:
  Close first, then delete non-mandatory data; mandatory records kept for the legal period
  and then deleted.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Thank you for your request. We respect your privacy and will remove your data as far as
  the law allows.
  <If account open:> To delete your data, we first need to close your account. <Closure
  steps / what is pending.>
  <If closed:> We have started the deletion. Your profile, device and marketing data will be
  deleted within 30 days. By law we must keep your identity verification and transaction
  records for 5 years after closure; they are stored securely, used only for legal and
  regulatory purposes, and deleted after that period.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Deletion request on a CLOSED account -> Support Operations, P4, include: user_id,
    closure completed_at, request date.
  - Customer disputes the retention or cites a law -> Legal, P3 (via CMP-04 if a threat).
DO NOT:
  - Do not promise deletion of KYC or transaction records before the retention period.
  - Do not delete anything yourself.

## CLS-07: Withdraw (cancel) a closure request
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "I requested closure yesterday, please cancel it, I want to continue"
  - "Changed my mind, don't close my account"
  - "Closure request by mistake"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Request from the registered email
TOOLS: get_closure_requests
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
     Call get_closure_requests(). Use the latest (first) request.
  2. Branches:
     - REQUESTED, BLOCKED_BY_BALANCE: can be withdrawn. Ask Support Operations to set
       WITHDRAWN (or the customer cancels in-app, sample flow). Resolve.
     - BLOCKED_BY_HOLD: can be withdrawn, but the review continues (fraud_risk_sop RSK-01).
       Resolve (withdraw) and keep the hold messaging generic.
     - IN_PROGRESS: may still be stoppable -> escalate Support Operations, P3 urgently.
     - COMPLETED: cannot be undone -> CLS-05 (sign up again).
     - WITHDRAWN already: confirm the account stays open. Resolve.
     - No request found: nothing to cancel; confirm the account is active. Resolve.
RESOLUTION:
  Request marked WITHDRAWN; account continues as before.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  No problem. <Withdrawn:> We have cancelled your closure request, and your account will stay
  open. You can continue using it as usual.
  <Completed:> Your account was already closed on <date>, so the closure cannot be reversed.
  You are welcome to sign up again, and we will help you complete verification.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - IN_PROGRESS request to cancel -> Support Operations, P3, include: request_id.
DO NOT:
  - Do not change users.status yourself.

======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:

  - "I am the nominee of the account holder who died"
  - "Account holder expired last month, need to transfer holdings to legal heir"

  - None through the AI reply. Legal handles documents (death certificate, heirship
    proof) through its own process.
TOOLS: find_user
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. This message must not be auto-answered beyond the holding reply
     (support_guidelines_sop SUP-05). Route to complaints_escalation_sop CMP-07.
  2. If you can identify the account from the details given, note the user_id in the
     internal note only:
     Call find_user(email=<email>, phone=<phone>). Lookup only, no details shared.
  3. Do not share balances, holdings or any account detail with the sender, even if they
     write from the deceased customer's email.
RESOLUTION:
  Legal handles transmission to nominee/legal heirs and the closure per CMP-07.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Thank you for writing to us. Your message has been passed to a senior member of our
  team, who will get back to you within 24 hours.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Always -> Legal via complaints_escalation_sop CMP-07, P2, include: sender details,
    user_id if identified, the message.
DO NOT:
  - Do not confirm that an account exists or share any detail.
  - Do not ask for death certificates or documents by email in the AI reply.
  - Do not start a normal closure (CLS-01) for a deceased customer.
