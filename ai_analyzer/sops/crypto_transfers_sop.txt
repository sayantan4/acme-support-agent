## CRY-00: Scope and quick triage
======================================================================
Covers crypto sent INTO Acme Invest from an external wallet/exchange (crypto deposits) and
crypto sent OUT of Acme Invest (crypto withdrawals): networks, confirmations, memos/tags,
addresses, whitelisting, network fees and holds.
Not covered here: INR deposits (payment_sop), INR withdrawals (withdrawal_sop), buying and
selling (trade_sop), fee disputes (fees_charges_sop), frozen accounts (fraud_risk_sop RSK-01),
scam victims (fraud_risk_sop RSK-05).

Quick triage (after identifying the user per support_guidelines_sop SUP-02; a tx hash quoted
by the customer is the fastest lookup):
  - "Sent crypto, not credited"                          -> CRY-01
  - "Sent on the wrong network"                          -> CRY-02
  - "Forgot memo / tag"                                  -> CRY-03
  - "Sent to wrong / old address"                        -> CRY-04
  - "Deposit option disabled / network maintenance"      -> CRY-05
  - "Withdrawal stuck processing"                        -> CRY-06
  - "Withdrawal success but not received"                -> CRY-07
  - "Withdrew to the wrong address"                      -> CRY-08
  - "Cannot use new address / whitelist / cooldown"      -> CRY-09
  - "Minimum withdrawal / network fee"                   -> CRY-10
  - "Withdrawal on hold"                                 -> CRY-11
  - "Unknown / airdrop / unsupported tokens received"    -> CRY-12
  - "Deposit failed / under review", "withdrawal failed" -> CRY-13

Key rules:
  - Blockchain transactions are irreversible. Never promise recovery of funds sent to a wrong
    address or network; recovery (where possible) is best-effort, not guaranteed.
  - Recovery policy (data_dictionary DD-09): unsupported network or missing memo/tag needs manual
    recovery, fee Rs 2,500 equivalent, 7-15 working days, not guaranteed.
  - Required confirmations, minimum withdrawal and withdrawal fee per network are in
    supported_networks; always quote from there, never from memory.
  - Never disclose hold reasons (CRY-11), other users' addresses, or internal wallet details.
  - A customer asking to "urgently" whitelist or withdraw to a new address because someone told
    them to may be a scam victim (support_guidelines_sop SUP-06, fraud_risk_sop RSK-05).

## CRY-01: Crypto deposit not credited
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "I sent 100 USDT from another exchange, not showing in Acme"
  - "BTC deposit pending for 2 hours"
  - "Transaction is successful on blockchain but not credited"
  - "Crypto bheja tha, wallet mein abhi tak nahi aaya"
  - "Here is my tx hash, please check"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Asset, network, amount, transaction hash (tx hash / TxID), sending time
TOOLS: get_crypto_deposits, find_user_by_reference, get_deposit_addresses, get_supported_networks, check_wallet_balance
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02; tx hash lookup is supported there).
  2. Find the deposit:
     Call get_crypto_deposits(tx_hash=<tx_hash>). Only this customer's deposits come back. None found:
     Call find_user_by_reference(tx_hash=<tx_hash>).
     (belongs_to_you false = the hash is on another account: share nothing about it.)
     (or, if no hash: Call get_crypto_deposits() and use that asset's
     deposits from the last 7 days.)
     - No deposit found: we have not detected the transaction yet.
       a) Ask for the tx hash if missing (from the sending platform's history).
       b) Confirm the destination address and network belong to this user:
          Call get_deposit_addresses().
          -> Address matches but network differs -> CRY-02. Address not this user's -> CRY-04.
       c) The customer should check the tx on a block explorer: if it is still pending/unconfirmed
          on the sending side, wait (sending platform may not have broadcast it yet).
       d) Tx confirmed on-chain to the correct address/network, and older than 2 hours (sample
          value) with no deposit found -> escalate Crypto Ops (P2).
       e) Not yet 2 hours -> ask the customer to wait 2 hours (sample value).
  3. Branch on status:
     - DETECTED / CONFIRMING: compare confirmations with required_confirmations.
       Call get_supported_networks(asset=<asset>, network=<network>).
       -> Normal: tell the customer the number of confirmations still needed; credit is automatic.
          Typical waits (sample values): BTC 30-60 minutes, ERC20 5-15 minutes, TRC20/BEP20 under
          5 minutes, subject to network congestion.
       -> confirmations >= required_confirmations but status not CREDITED for more than 1 hour
          (sample value) -> escalate Crypto Ops (P2).
     - CREDITED: confirm the ledger credit (ledger_credited from get_crypto_deposits):
       Call check_wallet_balance(asset=<asset>).
       -> ledger_credited is true: tell the customer it is credited (time in IST). Amount smaller than
          sent: the sending platform deducted its own fee; Acme does not charge for deposits (sample
          value).
       -> CREDITED but ledger_credited is false -> escalate Crypto Ops (P1).
     - UNSUPPORTED_NETWORK -> CRY-02.  MISSING_MEMO -> CRY-03.
     - MANUAL_REVIEW or FAILED -> CRY-13.
  4. Also check deposits are enabled on that network (CRY-05). If deposits were disabled when the
     customer sent, the deposit may be credited after maintenance ends.
RESOLUTION:
  Credit is automatic once required confirmations are reached. Only escalate when the chain shows
  it confirmed and we have not credited within the thresholds above.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Thanks for sharing the transaction details. I can see your deposit of <amount> <asset> on the
  <network> network. It currently has <x> of the <required> network confirmations needed, and it
  will be added to your Acme Invest wallet automatically once the rest are complete, usually within
  <time>. You do not need to do anything else.
  (Credited variant: "Your deposit was added to your wallet on <date> at <time IST>.")
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Confirmed on-chain to the user's address/network, no detection after 2 hours (sample value) -> Crypto Ops, P2, include: tx_hash, asset, network, to_address
  - Required confirmations reached, not credited after 1 hour (sample value) -> Crypto Ops, P2
  - CREDITED without ledger entry -> Crypto Ops, P1
DO NOT:
  - Do not ask for the customer's private key or seed phrase (never needed).
  - Do not share internal wallet/hot-wallet addresses.

## CRY-02: Deposit sent on the wrong / unsupported network
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "I sent USDT on BEP20 but my Acme address was TRC20"
  - "Selected wrong network while withdrawing from another exchange"
  - "Wrong chain pe bhej diya, please recover"
  - "Sent ETH on a different network, will I get it back?"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Asset, network actually used, amount, tx hash, destination address
TOOLS: get_crypto_deposits, find_user_by_reference, get_supported_networks, get_deposit_addresses
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Call get_crypto_deposits(tx_hash=<tx_hash>). Only this customer's deposits come back. None found:
     Call find_user_by_reference(tx_hash=<tx_hash>).
     (belongs_to_you false = the hash is on another account: share nothing about it.)
  3. Call get_supported_networks(asset=<asset>).
     Call get_deposit_addresses(asset=<asset>).
  4. Branch:
     - status UNSUPPORTED_NETWORK: we received the transaction on a network we do not support for
       this asset. Recovery is manual: fee Rs 2,500 equivalent, 7-15 working days, NOT guaranteed
       (DD-09). Ask the customer to confirm in writing that they accept the fee and that recovery
       is not guaranteed. On confirmation -> escalate Crypto Ops (P3).
     - The network used IS supported for the asset and the address matches that network's deposit
       address: it is not a wrong-network case -> CRY-01.
     - No deposit found and the to_address matches a user deposit address on a different network
       (e.g. same address format on ERC20/BEP20/POLYGON): escalate Crypto Ops (P3) to check whether
       the funds reached an address we control on that chain; recovery terms are the same as above.
     - No deposit found and to_address is not ours -> CRY-04 (cannot recover).
RESOLUTION:
  Explain the recovery terms honestly, get the customer's written acceptance, then escalate.
  Recovery outcome is decided by Crypto Ops.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  Thanks for the details. I can see your <amount> <asset> was sent on the <network used> network,
  which is not supported for <asset> deposits on Acme Invest. We can attempt a manual recovery, but
  please note:
  - A recovery fee equivalent to Rs 2,500 is deducted from the recovered amount.
  - It takes 7-15 working days.
  - Recovery is not guaranteed.
  Please reply confirming you would like us to proceed on these terms, and we will start the process.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Customer accepts recovery terms -> Crypto Ops, P3, include: tx_hash, asset, network used, amount, to_address, customer acceptance
  - Amount is large (sample: above Rs 5,00,000 equivalent) -> Crypto Ops, P2
DO NOT:
  - Do not promise recovery or a date. Do not waive or reduce the fee.

## CRY-03: Missing or wrong memo / tag
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "I forgot to add the memo while sending XRP"
  - "Entered wrong destination tag"
  - "Tag daalna bhool gaya, coins nahi aaye"
  - "Deposit shows but not credited, memo missing"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Asset, network, amount, tx hash, the memo/tag they used (if any)
TOOLS: get_crypto_deposits, find_user_by_reference, get_deposit_addresses, find_deposit_address_owner
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Call get_crypto_deposits(tx_hash=<tx_hash>). Only this customer's deposits come back. None found:
     Call find_user_by_reference(tx_hash=<tx_hash>).
     (belongs_to_you false = the hash is on another account: share nothing about it.)
     Call get_deposit_addresses(asset=<asset>, network=<network>).
  3. Branch:
     - status MISSING_MEMO: the deposit reached our shared address without a memo/tag, so it could
       not be matched to the account automatically. Recovery: fee Rs 2,500 equivalent, 7-15 working
       days, not guaranteed (DD-09). Ask for written acceptance + proof of ownership (screenshot of the
       sending platform's withdrawal record showing tx hash and amount). Then escalate Crypto Ops (P3).
     - memo_tag present but belongs to another user (check with
       Call find_deposit_address_owner(address=<to_address>, memo_tag=<memo_tag>)): the funds were
       credited to another account. Do not reveal who. Escalate Crypto Ops (P2) with proof of ownership.
     - memo_tag matches this user and status CREDITED -> CRY-01 (already credited).
RESOLUTION:
  Same recovery terms as wrong network. Remind the customer that memo/tag is mandatory for assets
  that show one on the deposit screen.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  I found your deposit of <amount> <asset>. It arrived without the memo/tag that identifies your
  account, so it could not be added automatically. We can try to recover it manually:
  - A recovery fee equivalent to Rs 2,500 applies.
  - It takes 7-15 working days and is not guaranteed.
  Please reply confirming you accept these terms and attach a screenshot of the withdrawal record
  from the platform you sent from, showing the transaction hash and amount.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Customer accepts and sends proof -> Crypto Ops, P3, include: tx_hash, amount, memo used, proof
  - Memo belonged to another user -> Crypto Ops, P2
DO NOT:
  - Do not reveal another user's identity or account. Do not guarantee recovery.

## CRY-04: Sent to an address that is not the user's Acme deposit address
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "I sent BTC to an old address, is it lost?"
  - "Copied wrong address while depositing"
  - "Galat address pe bhej diya"
  - "I sent to someone's address who said it is Acme wallet"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Asset, network, amount, tx hash, destination address used
TOOLS: find_deposit_address_owner
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Check whether the destination is any Acme deposit address:
     Call find_deposit_address_owner(address=<to_address>).
     - Belongs to THIS user (possibly an older address, see created_at): old addresses remain valid
       (sample rule) -> CRY-01.
     - Belongs to ANOTHER Acme user: do not reveal anything about that user. Escalate Crypto Ops (P2)
       with proof of ownership; recovery depends on that user and is not guaranteed.
     - Not found: the address is not controlled by Acme Invest. The funds cannot be recovered by us.
       Resolve with the "cannot recover" reply.
  3. If the customer says someone gave them the address (a "support agent", investment scheme,
     trader) -> scam victim path: fraud_risk_sop RSK-05.
RESOLUTION:
  Only transfers to the user's own Acme deposit address can be credited. Transfers to external
  addresses cannot be reversed.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  I am sorry to hear this. I checked the address you sent to, and it is not an Acme Invest deposit
  address. Blockchain transfers cannot be reversed, and because we do not control that address we
  are unable to recover the funds. If you know the owner of the address, only they can return the
  funds. Please always copy your deposit address directly from the Deposit screen in the app.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Address belongs to another Acme user -> Crypto Ops, P2, include: tx_hash, to_address, proof
  - Scam indicators -> fraud_risk_sop RSK-05
DO NOT:
  - Do not confirm or deny whether an address belongs to another Acme user.

  Acme Invest Support

  - Do not give a specific resume time unless it is in maintenance_note or an announcement.

## CRY-06: Crypto withdrawal stuck in PROCESSING
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "My USDT withdrawal is processing for 3 hours"
  - "BTC withdrawal pending, no tx hash"
  - "Withdrawal abhi tak processing mein hai"
  - "When will my crypto withdrawal complete?"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Asset, amount, time of request
TOOLS: get_withdrawals, get_supported_networks
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Call get_withdrawals(asset=<asset>). Look at the latest 5 (crypto only,
     asset is not INR).
  3. Branch on status:
     - REQUESTED / PENDING_APPROVAL: waiting for checks. Large withdrawals may need manual review
       (sample: up to 24 hours). Older than 24 hours -> escalate Crypto Ops (P2).
     - ON_HOLD -> CRY-11.
     - PROCESSING with tx_hash present: the transaction has been broadcast to the blockchain. Give the
       customer the tx hash and ask them to track it on a block explorer for that network. Waiting for
       network confirmations on congested networks can take longer; the receiving platform credits
       after its own confirmation count. If the explorer shows it confirmed -> CRY-07.
     - PROCESSING without tx_hash:
       -> created less than 2 hours ago (sample value): normal batching/signing; ask the customer to
          wait 2 hours.
       -> older than 2 hours (sample value): check withdrawals_enabled and maintenance_note with
          Call get_supported_networks(asset=<asset>, network=<network>); if paused -> CRY-05
          (processed after maintenance). Otherwise escalate Crypto Ops (P2).
     - SUCCESS -> CRY-07.  FAILED / CANCELLED -> CRY-13.
RESOLUTION:
  Share tx hash and explorer guidance when available; otherwise give the wait window or escalate.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  I checked your withdrawal of <amount> <asset> requested on <date, time IST>.
  <"It has been sent to the blockchain. Transaction hash: <tx_hash>. You can track it on any block
  explorer for the <network> network; once the network confirms it, the receiving wallet will show
  the funds." / "It is being processed and should be sent within the next <x> hours. We will
  notify you when it is sent." / "It is taking longer than usual, so I have escalated it to our
  team. You will get an update within 48 hours.">
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - PROCESSING without tx_hash > 2 hours (sample value) on an enabled network -> Crypto Ops, P2, include: withdrawal_id, created_at, network
  - PENDING_APPROVAL > 24 hours (sample value) -> Crypto Ops, P2
DO NOT:
  - Do not cancel the withdrawal on the customer's behalf once it is PROCESSING.

## CRY-07: Withdrawal SUCCESS but the receiving wallet / exchange says not received
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "Acme shows completed but the other exchange hasn't received my USDT"
  - "Withdrawal successful but coins not in my wallet"
  - "Other exchange support says no deposit came"
  - "Success dikha raha hai par wallet mein nahi aaya"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Asset, amount, withdrawal time
TOOLS: check_withdrawal
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Call check_withdrawal(withdrawal_id=<withdrawal_id>). Check that withdrawal.user_id is the
     customer's and withdrawal.status is SUCCESS.
  3. Share the tx_hash, destination address, network and memo/tag with the customer (these are their
     own details) and check with them:
     - Address/network/memo match what the receiving platform shows: the transfer is complete on-chain.
       The receiving platform must credit it; ask the customer to give the tx hash to that platform.
       Resolve.
     - Receiving platform requires a memo/tag and memo_tag is empty or different: the funds reached
       their address without a memo; only the receiving platform can recover it. Resolve with guidance.
     - Network differs from what the receiving platform supports: the receiving platform must handle
       it (their recovery process). Resolve with guidance.
     - Customer says the explorer shows no transaction for tx_hash, or the tx failed on-chain:
       escalate Crypto Ops (P1) - possible false SUCCESS.
  4. Confirm the debit: use the ledger_entries returned by check_withdrawal.
RESOLUTION:
  Once a withdrawal is confirmed on-chain, Acme cannot reverse or re-send it; the customer works
  with the receiving platform using the tx hash.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  I checked your withdrawal of <amount> <asset>. It was completed on <date, time IST> and confirmed
  on the <network> network. Details you can share with the receiving platform:
  - Transaction hash: <tx_hash>
  - Destination address: <address>  <memo/tag: <memo>>
  You can also check it on a block explorer. Since the transfer is complete on the blockchain, the
  receiving platform will need to credit it; please contact their support with the transaction hash.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - tx_hash not found or failed on-chain while status is SUCCESS -> Crypto Ops, P1, include: withdrawal_id, tx_hash
DO NOT:
  - Do not re-send or refund a confirmed withdrawal. Do not name the receiving platform in writing.

## CRY-09: New address cooldown / whitelist issues
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "I added a new address but cannot withdraw to it"
  - "Why do I have to wait 24 hours for a new address?"
  - "Address whitelist pending"
  - "Naya address add kiya, withdraw button disabled hai"
  - "Please remove the cooldown urgently"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Asset, network, when the address was added
TOOLS: get_address_whitelist, get_auth_events
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Call get_address_whitelist().
  3. Branch on status:
     - PENDING_COOLDOWN: new addresses have a 24-hour cooldown (DD-09). Tell the customer the exact
       time it becomes usable (active_from in IST). The cooldown cannot be removed or shortened.
     - ACTIVE: whitelisting is fine; check network matches the withdrawal network and whether a
       security cooldown applies:
       Call get_auth_events(event_types=["PASSWORD_RESET", "PHONE_CHANGED",
       "EMAIL_CHANGED", "TWO_FA_RESET"], days=1). Use only events from the last 24 hours.
       -> Any found: 24-hour withdrawal cooldown after security changes (DD-09 states this for INR;
          applied to crypto too as a sample rule). Tell them when it ends.
       -> None: app issue -> app_technical_sop.
     - REMOVED: the customer (or security action) removed it; re-add it (new 24-hour cooldown applies).
     - Address not listed at all: the customer must add it in the app; support cannot add addresses.
  4. The customer did NOT add the address -> account_security_sop SEC-01 (P1).
  5. Urgency plus pressure from a third party -> support_guidelines_sop SUP-06 / fraud_risk_sop RSK-05.
RESOLUTION:
  Explain the cooldown is a security protection and give the time it ends.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  For your security, any new withdrawal address can be used 24 hours after it is added. The
  <asset> address you added on <date, time IST> will be ready to use from <date, time IST>. This
  waiting period cannot be shortened, as it protects your funds if someone else ever gains access
  to your account.
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Address added that the customer does not recognise -> account_security_sop SEC-01, Risk & Compliance, P1
  - ACTIVE past active_from and still blocked with no security event -> Tech On-call, P3
DO NOT:
  - Do not add, remove or activate addresses on request by email. Do not shorten the cooldown.

## CRY-10: Minimum withdrawal and network fee questions
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "Why is the withdrawal fee so high for ERC20?"
  - "What is the minimum USDT I can withdraw?"
  - "I received less than I withdrew"
  - "Network fee kitna lagta hai?"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Asset and network
TOOLS: get_supported_networks, check_withdrawal
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02) if they refer to a specific withdrawal.
  2. Call get_supported_networks(asset=<asset>).
     -> Quote each network's minimum and fee. Different networks have different fees; the fee covers
        the blockchain network cost and is a fixed amount per withdrawal in the asset (sample rule).
  3. For "received less": Call check_withdrawal(withdrawal_id=<withdrawal_id>). Use withdrawal.amount,
     fee and network.
     -> Amount received = amount - fee (sample rule: fee deducted from the withdrawn amount). The
        receiving platform may also charge a deposit fee. Resolve.
     -> Received amount lower than amount - fee -> ask for the receiving platform's record; if the
        on-chain amount (block explorer) is lower than amount - fee -> escalate Crypto Ops (P2).
  4. Fee dispute or refund request -> fees_charges_sop.
RESOLUTION:
  Quote values from supported_networks; explain that lower-fee networks are available where supported,

## CRY-11: Crypto withdrawal ON_HOLD
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "My BTC withdrawal is on hold, why?"
  - "Withdrawal held for verification since 2 days"
  - "Hold kyun lagaya? release karo"
  - "Why is my crypto blocked from withdrawing?"

TOOLS: get_withdrawals, get_risk_holds
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Call get_withdrawals(status="ON_HOLD").
     Call get_risk_holds().
  3. Follow fraud_risk_sop RSK-01 for all holds. Never disclose hold_reason, reason_code or source.

CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:

  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Follow fraud_risk_sop RSK-01 escalation rules; hold > 48 hours without update -> Risk & Compliance, P2
DO NOT:
  - Do not reveal hold reasons, rules, thresholds or that any agency is involved.
  - Do not promise a release date.

## CRY-12: Received unsupported tokens, airdrops or spam tokens
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "I got some random token in my wallet, what is it?"
  - "Sent a token that Acme doesn't list to my ETH address"
  - "Airdrop not credited on Acme"
  - "Unknown token aaya with a website link to claim reward"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Token name/contract (if known), network, tx hash
TOOLS: get_supported_networks, get_crypto_deposits
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Call get_supported_networks(asset=<asset>).
     Call get_crypto_deposits(tx_hash=<tx_hash>).
  3. Branch:
     - Customer sent an unlisted token to their Acme deposit address: it will not be credited
       automatically. Recovery is at Crypto Ops' discretion under the same terms as DD-09 (fee Rs 2,500
       equivalent, 7-15 working days, not guaranteed); tokens that cannot be safely handled cannot be
       recovered. Get written acceptance, then escalate Crypto Ops (P3).
     - Customer expected an airdrop for a coin they hold on Acme: airdrops are credited only if Acme
       announces support for that airdrop (sample rule). No announcement -> not supported. Resolve.
     - Unknown token showing in an external wallet or a message with a "claim" link: likely spam or a
       phishing attempt. Advise not to interact with it or visit links. Resolve; if they already
       connected a wallet or shared details -> fraud_risk_sop RSK-05.
RESOLUTION:
  Only listed assets on supported networks are credited. Everything else is best-effort recovery
  or not supported.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  <Unlisted: "The token you sent is not supported on Acme Invest, so it could not be added to your
  wallet. We can review whether a manual recovery is possible (fee equivalent to Rs 2,500, 7-15

  <Spam: "Unknown tokens with links to 'claim' rewards are a common scam. Please do not open the link,
  connect your wallet or share any codes. Acme Invest will never ask you to do this.">
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Customer accepts recovery terms -> Crypto Ops, P3, include: tx_hash, token contract, network, amount
DO NOT:
  - Do not comment on the value of an unlisted token or whether it will be listed.

## CRY-13: Deposit in MANUAL_REVIEW / FAILED, or withdrawal FAILED / CANCELLED
======================================================================
CUSTOMER MAY SAY:
  - "Deposit shows under review"
  - "My crypto withdrawal failed, where is my money?"
  - "Withdrawal cancelled automatically"
  - "Failed dikha raha hai, coins wapas aayenge?"
INFO NEEDED FROM CUSTOMER:
  - Asset, amount, tx hash or time
TOOLS: get_crypto_deposits, find_user_by_reference, get_risk_holds, check_withdrawal
DEBUGGING STEPS:
  1. Identify the user (support_guidelines_sop SUP-02).
  2. Deposit branch:
     Call get_crypto_deposits(tx_hash=<tx_hash>). Only this customer's deposits come back. None found:
     Call find_user_by_reference(tx_hash=<tx_hash>).
     (belongs_to_you false = the hash is on another account: share nothing about it.)
     - MANUAL_REVIEW: under compliance/ops review (e.g. source-of-funds checks). Timeline: up to
       72 hours (sample value). Check for a DEPOSIT_HOLD with
       Call get_risk_holds(hold_type="DEPOSIT_HOLD");
       follow fraud_risk_sop RSK-01 wording.
       Older than 72 hours -> escalate Crypto Ops (P2).
     - FAILED: the chain transaction failed or was reverted (the customer's explorer will show it).
       If the explorer shows success to the user's address -> escalate Crypto Ops (P2).
  3. Withdrawal branch:
     Call check_withdrawal(withdrawal_id=<withdrawal_id>). Use withdrawal.status and its
     ledger_entries (reversed is true when a REVERSAL entry exists).
     - FAILED / CANCELLED with a REVERSAL (or no debit) entry: the amount (including fee, sample rule)
       is back in available balance. Resolve; the customer may retry (check CRY-05, CRY-09, CRY-10
       for common causes: network paused, address not active, below minimum).
     - FAILED / CANCELLED with a debit and no REVERSAL after 1 hour (sample value) -> escalate Crypto Ops (P1).
RESOLUTION:
  Confirm where the funds are now and what the customer can do next.
CUSTOMER REPLY TEMPLATE:
  Hi <first name>,
  I checked your <deposit/withdrawal> of <amount> <asset>. <"It is under a routine review and we will
  update you within 72 hours." / "The withdrawal could not be completed, and the full amount has been
  returned to your Acme Invest wallet. You can try again from the app.">
  Best regards,
  Acme Invest Support
ESCALATE WHEN:
  - Failed withdrawal debited without reversal -> Crypto Ops, P1, include: withdrawal_id, entry_ids
  - MANUAL_REVIEW > 72 hours (sample value) -> Crypto Ops, P2
DO NOT:
  - Do not disclose review reasons.
