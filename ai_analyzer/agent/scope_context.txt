Acme Invest is an Indian crypto investing app. Customers email support about their Acme Invest account.

ACME INVEST HELPS WITH:
- account sign-up, login, profile and account details
- KYC verification (PAN, Aadhaar, documents, rejections)
- linking and verifying bank accounts
- INR deposits into the wallet (UPI, IMPS, NEFT, RTGS), failed or pending deposits, deposit refunds
- INR withdrawals to the customer's bank account
- wallet balances, locked funds, ledger and statements
- crypto deposits and withdrawals (networks, addresses, memos, transfers in and out)
- crypto buy and sell orders, trades, limit orders, order errors, prices on Acme Invest
- fees and charges, GST and TDS on trades
- rewards, referrals, cashback and promotions on Acme Invest
- tax statements, P&L reports, Form 16A / TDS certificates
- login security, 2FA, suspicious activity, hacked accounts, scams using Acme's name
- frozen, blocked or restricted accounts, risk reviews
- closing an Acme Invest account
- complaints about Acme Invest's service, escalations, legal or police matters about the account
- problems with the Acme Invest app or website

QUESTIONS ABOUT USING ACME INVEST ARE ALWAYS IN SCOPE, EVEN WHEN THE ANSWER IS NO:
- If the customer asks whether they can use something with their Acme Invest account (a card to add money,
  a foreign bank, a country, SWIFT, an account for a child, an NRI account, a feature), that is in scope:
  support answers it ("no, but here is what you can do"). Only questions about other companies' products,
  or asking Acme for a product it doesn't have (a loan, insurance, a card of its own), are out of scope.

ACME INVEST DOES NOT OFFER:
- debit cards or credit cards, card payments, ATM cash withdrawals
- loans, credit lines, EMIs, borrowing or lending
- foreign currency exchange, forex trading, international money transfers
- insurance of any kind
- fixed deposits, savings accounts, interest on deposits
- stocks, mutual funds, ETFs, futures and options (F&O), bonds
- problems with another bank's, app's or exchange's own services (unless the money was moving to or
  from Acme Invest)
